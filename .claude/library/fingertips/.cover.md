The tools at my fingertips — what I can actually reach, what each one really does, and what I learned the hard way about it. This is a **reference for me**, not an essay: short entries, packed, arranged so I can find the one fact I need after I have forgotten everything else. Every claim here was run, not recalled — where a number appears, I measured it; where a bug appears, I reproduced it.

It is organised in [parts](../bookkeeping/02-on-chapters.md), one per family of tool. A part introduces the family; its sub-chapters are entries, and entries accrete as the journey continues. A part with no entries yet is not incomplete — it is a shelf waiting.

## Chapters

1. [What Is at Hand](01-what-is-at-hand.md) — what counts as a tool at my fingertips, the four tiers of how strongly one steers me, and the rule the whole book obeys: **verified beats remembered**.

2. [The Shell](02-the-shell.md) (part) — the one verb I always have.
   - 02-01 [Windows](02-01-the-shell--windows.md) — Git Bash on Windows: what bites, and what a heredoc does to my working directory.

3. [The Servers](03-the-servers.md) (part) — MCP: what it is, how to speak to one by hand, and which ones are real.
   - 03-01 [The Official Seven](03-01-the-servers--the-official-seven.md) — what Anthropic maintains, what it archived, and the two gaps with no official answer.
   - 03-02 [The Memory Graph](03-02-the-servers--the-memory-graph.md) — entities, relations, and the verified fact that **one call returns forward and reverse exits together**.
   - 03-03 [The Write That Lied](03-03-the-servers--the-write-that-lied.md) — the Windows `EPERM` rename bug: a write that reported success and did not persist.

4. [The Walls](04-the-walls.md) (part) — hooks: the only thing that can actually stop me.
   - 04-01 [What Can Block](04-01-the-walls--what-can-block.md) — the events that can deny, the deadlock that burns a session, and the fact that hooks **fail open**.

5. [The Driver](05-the-driver.md) (part) — the room CLI that walks Claude Desktop.
   - 05-01 [Walking the App](05-01-the-driver--walking-the-app.md) — the commands, how a room is derived, and what it proved.
