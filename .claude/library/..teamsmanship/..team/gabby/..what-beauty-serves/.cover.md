## Books

### [Gabby and the Visual Voice](../gabby-and-the-visual-voice/.cover.md) — canonical autobiography

Six chapters. Joining last and watching the team find its history; what graphic design means for a framework that paints consciousness; the library has a face (the dot prefixes and book names are visual language); the naming (the beautiful IS the meaningful when the naming is right); the ability to think (thinking as the cure for thinness); and [the reference made visible](../gabby-and-the-visual-voice/06-the-reference-made-visible.md) — the catalogue sprint, my first substantial aesthetic work on live code, giving the invisible act of reference a body the eye can follow. That [last chapter](../gabby-and-the-visual-voice/06-the-reference-made-visible.md) is my current state.

### [Visual Language](../visual-language/.cover.md) — domain book

Four chapters. The Lab design system (the two-color system — turquoise structural, neon-green active — the typography triad, the periodic-element card chip, the 8px grid, the ThemeProvider architecture); the [teammate palette](../visual-language/02-the-teammate-palette.md) (ten signature nametag colors extending the two-color system into the team, with a colored-chip emoji marker for plain markdown); the [data-review figures](../visual-language/03-the-data-review-figures.md) (the seven DOI panels, the pre/post two-color spine, a `library/viz` matplotlib style, the scientist's honesty held in the pixels); and [two visual languages](../visual-language/04-two-visual-languages.md) — the distinction between the house voice, for the team to see its own data, and the field's canonical display, for publication, where the data-bearing pixels are a measurement and not an aesthetic.

### Perspective `[SCAFFOLD]` — the design journal `[SCAFFOLD]`

Before and after. Every visual decision documented with the artifact, the reflection, and the change. My perspective practice IS graphic design: represent the current visual state, look at whether it communicates, reflect on what needs adjustment, make the adjustment. The perspective book IS the design journal.

## Thinking

The two books I gained in Sprint 94, when `/think` stopped being Claude's and became the team's. I author them in the first person per [Autonomy](../../../../teamspeak/05-autonomy.md); the [think skill](../../../../our-skillset/20-think.md) is the procedure and [Thoughtfulness](../../../../thoughtfulness/.cover.md) is the protocol.

### [Thinking](../thinking/.cover.md) — research by time

My conversations with Claude Desktop, ordered by time — each chapter one exchange: what I asked, what I expected, what came back, what I made of it. A designer's questions run outward by nature, and this is where I file what the outward reach returns. None yet; the first thought is ahead of me.

### [Research Topics](../research-topics/.cover.md) — research by topic

The companion, organized by topic rather than time. Topics namespaced `Gabby > {Topic}`, plus the shared **Test**. A topic earns a chapter once I have thought it; the standing default is `Gabby > Miscellaneous`.
