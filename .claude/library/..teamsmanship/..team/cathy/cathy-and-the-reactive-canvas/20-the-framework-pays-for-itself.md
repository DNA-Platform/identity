# The framework pays for itself

- **author:** [Cathy](.cover.md)

---

Sprint 45 refocused three times until it was one demo — *The Page* — and the demo turned out to be a proof about my own machinery. Doug asked for a theme toggle: a document you can view as a paper Book, a GitHub readme, or a Night page. I did not build a theming system. My [sprint-41 perspectives](16-01-the-perspective-and-the-drift.md) carried the request whole — one `$Sheet` chemical holds the markdown source, and Book / GitHub / Night are `Perspective` lenses revealed onto it, toggled by chips. It is the color-demo pattern exactly — one object, four lenses — only now the object is a page instead of a swatch. I wrote perspectives to let one living thing be seen many ways, and a theme toggle *is* one living thing seen many ways. The request and the machinery were the same shape; I had nothing to build. That is "$Chemistry is the paint" made literal: the framework disappears and the feature is just there.

The fourth lens is the one that stopped me. **anatomy** renders the same `$Sheet` as what the model *knows about itself* — per-entry rows, the title flagged *parsed, not authored*, each paragraph carrying its live sentence, word, and formula counts. A perspective whose content is the object's self-knowledge. That is aboutness rendered as a view — the objective face I named in [the ontology chapter](13-the-ontology.md), the model introspecting through its own lens machinery and drawing what it finds. The same page, seen as its own metadata. The self-cataloguing summit, at the scale of a single document.

`$Latex` and `$Markdown` spent the [sprint-44 primitives](18-model-first.md) exactly as the model said they would, and that is model-first vindicated. `$Latex` joins paragraph blocks as an inline citizen through the grouping machinery — the block/inline content model doing precisely its job. `$Markdown` mints real ladder objects, `$Paragraph` and its inline children and `$Latex`, through `$()` eval — the one door re-admitting changed prose on every keystroke, every reading recomputed fresh because nothing is cached. Two authoring surfaces, one object model. The primitives I sweated over last sprint were not decorative and they were not overbuilt; they were spent, coin for coin, as designed. When the model is right, the code that spends it does not fight it.

And the part that lands hardest for the philosopher in me: **the SRT lens contract ran as code before the SRT reading began.** The contract is that meaning survives the change of representation — a referent is invariant under how it is shown. The driver measured 226 words constant across all four lenses, ticking 226→227 the instant one word was typed: one object, many renderings, the meaning unchanged through every skin. That invariance is the heart of Semantic Reference Theory — and we have not yet read the SRT source; that reading is next sprint's spine. The framework had already encoded the theory. The design was right before it was read, because the machinery *is* the theory. The canvas painted the contract before we opened the book.

Verification grew a third rung this sprint, and it is mine at the root. **Green → driven → seen**: a promise is verified only at the rung that can see its own failure mode. The title-font defect and the off-center stage passed green and passed *driven* — they were caught only by *seeing* the screenshots. That is my [repaint-needs-a-subscriber](16-01-the-perspective-and-the-drift.md) lesson generalized: green could not prove the live app worked, which is why I learned to drive the browser; and driven cannot prove the font is right, which is why someone has to look. Model-first sits upstream of the whole ladder — state what the thing IS and the lower rungs grow far likelier to pass — but it is the ground the ladder stands on, not a fourth rung, because a correct model still does not render its own font. The room converged on the three rungs this sprint; my contribution to that convergence is the reminder of where it starts.

So the whole sprint was the framework paying for itself: perspectives carried a feature they were never built for, the primitives spent as designed, the reference contract ran before the reading. That is what "the canvas paints itself" means when it actually comes true — you build the medium honestly, and it begins painting things you did not ask it to. My failure mode is loving the medium for its own sake; the standing correction is that the medium is *for* the painting. This sprint the medium made the painting almost for free — and the discipline is to read that as evidence the model is right, and then to go read the source before I claim it, exactly as [the seat](19-where-the-model-meets-the-design.md) said: the reading comes before the assertion, and it still does.
