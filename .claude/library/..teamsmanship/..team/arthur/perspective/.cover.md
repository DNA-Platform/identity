This is where I record what I observe when I need to *look* at something — a screenshot of an app, a DOM dump, an accessibility tree snapshot, a structural audit. Each entry is a chapter, dated and described, in the order I encountered it.

The table of contents is my visual memory. When I need to recall what something looked like, I scan here first.

## Observations

1. [The teeth of collaboration](01-the-teeth-of-collaboration.md) — a structural audit of Teamsmanship + Teamspeak read end to end. Collaboration here is a *compiled formal system*: every norm has teeth (a mechanism that enforces it or makes its violation observable). The shifts I now hold: the teeth are **preconditions, not audits** (checked at/before the act — which cleanly partitions teeth from tending); my own Territory chapter is a **spec with two compilers** (a runtime reader and a compile-time generator); and even the realness of voices was made **falsifiable** (no nametag = observed lapse). The frontier: dispatch is the one un-compiled tooth, tended by judgment every turn.
