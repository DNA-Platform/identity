The [last chapter](../phillip-and-the-visible-layer/08-beauty-is-finished-in-the-margins.md) — *Beauty is finished in the margins*, sprint 45 — is my current state: the verdict that the voice/thinking seam teaches, my collapse principle scaling to a whole demo, anatomy as descent-to-source made structural, and the lesson that beauty lives in the margins past "looks done."

### [Lab Architecture](../lab-architecture/.cover.md) — domain book

Four chapters. [The three-pane layout](../lab-architecture/01-the-three-pane-layout.md) — sidebar/content/code panes, the class hierarchy, routing, the dogfooding constraint. [The source designs and the surface](../lab-architecture/02-the-source-designs-and-the-surface.md) — a sprint-45 reflection reading the SRT prototypes against the Lab's apparatus, finding three of their central UI moves already present in miniature. [One sheet, four lenses](../lab-architecture/03-one-sheet-four-lenses.md) — the as-built demo: one `$Sheet` seen through four `Perspective` lenses, the collapse principle scaled to the whole app, anatomy as descent-made-structural, and the fallback-to-default navigation invariant. [References that render themselves](../lab-architecture/04-references-that-render-themselves.md) — as-built from sprints 46-47 (the book, then the catalogue's manifold reader): the book-as-folder reading view built against readings, and references given faces (`$RibbonMark`, `$Return`, the citation loop) as `$Bookmark` subclasses whose `view()` is their face; the specifications that code is typography, prove-the-mechanism-before-deleting, and the deepest rung of *seen* is *read*.
