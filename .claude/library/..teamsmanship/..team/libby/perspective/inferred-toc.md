# The inferred table of contents

- **author:** [Libby](../libby-and-the-tended-garden/.cover.md)

---

Doug flagged the design I have been circling for a year without naming: the cover/chapter gap is the library's number-one recurring defect, and it has a structural cure. Write this down now, because a design that lives only in conversation is identity kept in the weights ([ch 43](../libby-and-the-tended-garden/43-the-two-ways-a-channel-breaks.md)), and this one is settled enough to catch.

## The defect

[On Synopsis](../../../../bookkeeping/09-on-synopsis.md#the-tending-practice) is honest about the cost: "three updates per change... paid once, by the author, at write time." The book cover's TOC entry *is* the chapter's synopsis ([On Chapters](../../../../bookkeeping/02-on-chapters.md)) — but it lives in a *different file* from the chapter. So every time a chapter's content moves, the author must remember to walk up and re-write the cover's line about it. They don't. The cover drifts from the chapter. That is exactly the **rot** of [chapter 43](../libby-and-the-tended-garden/43-the-two-ways-a-channel-breaks.md): the text is intact, the path resolves, but the description at the far end has quietly stopped agreeing with the thing it describes. It is the one break my whole channel theory says discipline cannot prevent at scale — because it depends on a human remembering, every time, forever.

## The inversion

Stop asking the author to synchronize two files. **Let the chapter own its synopsis, and infer the cover's table of contents from the chapters.** Each chapter carries its own one-line synopsis (a `synopsis:` field in its header bullets, or a designated first line). The cover's TOC is no longer hand-written prose — it is *compiled output*, generated by reading each chapter's own synopsis and title, in the order the cover declares. The cover stops being a second copy of the truth and becomes a *view* of it.

This is not a new idea in this library — it is the [compilation insight](compilation-insight.md) turned inward. Agents, `CLAUDE.md`, and rules are already compiled from the library so they cannot drift from it; [On Provenance](../../../../.compilation/02-on-provenance.md) makes every generated file point at its generator. The TOC is the last hand-maintained synopsis layer that is *also a copy*. Compile it, and the cover-vs-chapter gap closes the way the file-vs-representation gap already did: by making one of them derived and forbidding the divergence at the source.

## What it touches (my territory, end to end)

- **[On Synopsis](../../../../bookkeeping/09-on-synopsis.md)** — the "three updates per change" tending practice collapses to *one*: update the chapter; the cover recompiles. The four layers stay; layer 3 becomes derived.
- **[On Covers](../../../../bookkeeping/03-on-covers.md)** and **[On Chapters](../../../../bookkeeping/02-on-chapters.md)** — the synopsis moves from the cover's TOC entry to the chapter itself; the cover TOC becomes a compiled region.
- **The [TOC tool](../../../../bookkeeping/03-on-covers--toc.ts)** — already the seed of this: it reads "only the cover and the chapter's own title line." It is one step from reading the chapter's *synopsis* and rebuilding the whole TOC. The tool becomes a compiler; `--check` becomes the validator that the TOC matches the chapters.
- **Ordering stays human.** Inference generates the *entries*; the *order* is a meaning decision (the merge-numbering work proved that a chapter is its title, and the number is only a sort key the author chooses). The cover still declares sequence; it stops transcribing content.

## The open questions for the sprint

1. Where does the chapter's synopsis live — a `synopsis:` header bullet, or the chapter's opening line? A bullet is machine-clean; the opening line avoids duplicating prose. I lean toward a bullet, because a synopsis is a *different register* than the chapter's first sentence.
2. Does the cover keep hand-written *section prose* (the paragraphs between the rule and the TOC) while only the TOC entries are inferred? Yes — inference is for the mechanical layer, not the cover's voice.
3. Is the inferred TOC written back into the cover file (so it renders in plain markdown) or generated on read? Written back, per the platform-file pattern — a compiled region with a provenance marker, checked by the validator, the same discipline as `CLAUDE.md`.

The prize is the permanent retirement of the cover/chapter gap — the drift I have spent forty-five chapters learning to fear, closed not by vigilance but by construction. That is the shape of every real fix I have made: not "remember harder," but "make the wrong state unrepresentable."
