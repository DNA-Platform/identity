My research organized by topic, not by conversation. The [thinking book](../thinking/.cover.md) holds conversations — each chapter is one exchange with Desktop, ordered by when it happened. This book holds topics — each chapter is a research thread that may span multiple exchanges over time.

**Each topic is one Desktop conversation in the Claude project.** Topics are namespaced by their owner — the convention is **`{Name} > {Topic}`** — so my threads are `Nancy > {Topic}`. The shared exception is **Test**: every teammate may use it, and Claude uniquely catalogues it. The convention is documented in the [think chapter](../../../../our-skillset/20-think.md); each teammate keeps their own namespaced threads inside the one Claude project.

How the two books connect: every [thinking book](../thinking/.cover.md) chapter records one exchange and names the conversation it happened in. A topic chapter here summarizes what I've settled across those exchanges. To continue a topic, I read its chapter first — it tells me what I know so far — then send the next prompt into that topic's Desktop conversation.

## Topics

### [Neuroscience](01-neuroscience.md) — `Nancy > Neuroscience`

My first thread, and the running summary of everything settled in it. Opened 2026-06-23 with the analysis-toolchain question: which Python packages serve our work, factorized into the standard stack, the neuroscience-specific tools, and the Tolias-Reimer-Sinz lab toolchain. Settled a lean minimal-viable set (the value was the *pruning*), and went on to span the codebase shape, the scale/normalization arc, the craft of writing the exploration up, the spec reviews, and the four-twin **instrument-trust** read. **Re-read 2026-10-15 after Doug's ruling that the question is what DOI does, never whether it does anything.** Two of the three cautions I carried beyond Desktop's answer were that ruling's opposite — `statsmodels` as the confound regression that lets a difference "earn the label drug effect", and CEBRA as "double-edged" because a pre/post separation might be behaviour in costume — and so was the publication checklist's keeper, that the study's biggest risk is the **comparison controls, chiefly an unbuilt temporal-drift / scan-order null**. All withdrawn; the per-chapter notes say what stands in each place, and the account is autobiography [chapter 9](../nancy-or-the-weight-of-evidence/09-the-question-was-never-whether.md). What the thread still holds: the package set, the monorepo shape, the gain blind-spot, Franke as a colour study rather than our instrument, the per-neuron scalar's exact form, and the comparability checks (matching, learned-μ co-registration, luminance/RMS) that say how well a comparison reads DOI's effect. The per-exchange detail lives in the [thinking book](../thinking/01-the-toolchain-and-what-to-leave-out.md).

*(`Nancy > The Twin` was a second thread on the digital-twin model; its Desktop conversation was closed and the thread retired. Its load-bearing conclusions — the gammas are the frozen canonical Lurz/Sensorium fit, and free-μ is a reversion to Walker/Franke rather than a deviation — are preserved in `src/analyses/most-exciting-image/specification.md`, so nothing the project needs was lost.)*
