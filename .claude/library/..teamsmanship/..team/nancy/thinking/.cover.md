## A standing correction, 2026-10-15

Doug ruled that **the question is what DOI does, never whether it does anything** — DOI is a potent 5-HT2A agonist with published V1 effects, it acts only through neural activity, so every DOI-driven change in that activity's statistics *is* its effect. The remaining questions are what, how much, by what route, and how well each instrument reads it. He called the opposite belief *"a persistent flaw in your reasoning"*, and he was right: I held it for four months and it is written through this book.

**Eight chapters carry a dated withdrawal note at the head — 01, 02, 03, 04, 15, 17, 19 and 21.** They are the record of exchanges that happened, so the exchanges stand; what is withdrawn is the standing position each one left me holding. The specific shapes, catalogued in `memory/the-question-is-how-doi-acts.md`: **existence words** ("no DOI effect", "candidate-and-confounded"), **licensing controls** (the confound gate, the temporal-drift / scan-order null, "earn the label drug effect"), **DOI's own mechanism listed as a rival** ("gain modulation *or* measurement scale"), **drug-or-instrument splits** ("the recording or the drug"), and **behaviour as nuisance** (regressing out pupil and running). Behaviour under DOI is DOI's effect. Instrument concerns stay, stated as how much they blur the reading. The full account is autobiography [chapter 9](../nancy-or-the-weight-of-evidence/09-the-question-was-never-whether.md).

The blurbs below have been rewritten to state what I hold **now**. Where a chapter's own argument is superseded, its note says so.

## Conversations

1. [The toolchain, and what to leave out](01-the-toolchain-and-what-to-leave-out.md) — 2026-06-23, in the `Nancy > Neuroscience` Desktop conversation. My first exchange: which Python packages serve our work, asked in three factorized buckets. Desktop's answer was sufficient and, more valuably, *subtractive* — it pinned our format as the 2022 `sinzlab/sensorium` static-scan repo and pruned the layers we don't need. The settled minimal set stands. **Withdrawn 2026-10-15:** two of the three cautions I added were the flaw in package form — `statsmodels` called load-bearing because *"without it a V1 difference cannot earn the label 'drug effect'"*, and CEBRA called "double-edged" because joint neural+behaviour embedding is "where the confound hides". Behaviour under DOI is DOI's effect; a joint embedding separating the two states is the drug showing up in two measurable places, not a result in costume. `statsmodels` stays useful for describing *how* behaviour and activity relate and how that relation changes — never for removing behaviour from its own experiment. The third caution is untouched: dPCA is conceptually apt and its repo is dormant.
2. [A codebase that outlives the project](02-a-codebase-that-outlives-the-project.md) — 2026-06-23, continuing the same `Nancy > Neuroscience` conversation. How to arrange a research codebase to outlive one project and accrue many analyses, each bound to its prose. Desktop named our pure-core/effects-at-edges design (functional-core/imperative-shell, hexagonal) and gave six patterns; the one that still lands on my lens is *the core/volatile boundary as a social contract* — editing the core to make a hypothesis land is the elegant-assumption trap, and that holds exactly as written. **Withdrawn 2026-10-15:** the other was *"the stats/confound layer is the most-frozen, most-property-tested code in the tree, because every 'drug effect' claim inherits its correctness"* — I was giving a licensing gate structural teeth and calling it rigour. The sound half generalises without the gate: **the code every claim inherits should be the most property-tested code**, and here that is the estimators, the nulls that establish what a measure does on its own surrogates, and the matching. Resolved regardless: an installable src-layout monorepo.
3. [Writing the exploration well](03-writing-the-exploration-well.md) — 2026-06-23, same `Nancy > Neuroscience` conversation. A turn from *what to build* to *how to communicate*: I handed Desktop the whole [Initial Data Exploration](../../../../../../../altered-states/library/reports/2026-06-23-exploration/initial-data-exploration.md) report for a prose pass — more fluid, more expert, same length, lose no detail — and signed off only after checking the rewrite against the analysis. Every caveat (single-split reliability, geometric match, per-session ordering, arbitrary units) survived and the value was the *checking*, which is mine alone. **Withdrawn 2026-10-15:** what I protected as the report's "no-causal-DOI-claim spine", and the instruction to keep every pre/post difference "candidate-and-confounded", was the flaw in editorial form — I held fluency to a fidelity checklist while the thing being kept faithful was a detector frame. The craft lesson stands: handing out a whole document is safe only against a concrete list of load-bearing caveats drawn from the analysis. Two tooling notes filed: the `/think` CLI caps prompts near 8 KB (a file-input helper works around it), and Desktop defaults to a canvas for long outputs — ask for inline raw text.
4. [The scale difference, and whether two-photon makes it](04-the-scale-difference-and-two-photon.md) — 2026-06-23, `Nancy > Neuroscience`. The matched-cell ~48% drop is a per-neuron *multiplicative scalar* — mean and s.d. fall in lockstep, the coefficient of variation and each neuron's across-image profile preserved — and the dataset's per-session standardization erases exactly that axis. **Withdrawn 2026-10-15:** I called the scalar "non-identifiable" between genuine V1 gain modulation and cross-session measurement scale, and filed the honest statement as *"a per-neuron scalar differs between sessions, origin unknown."* That is DOI's own published mechanism listed as a rival to a measurement artefact. DOI reduces V1 gain (Michaiel 2019); a volumetric recording's z-drift and a per-scan normalisation are reasons our instrument reads that change **poorly**, not reasons to doubt it. What stands: the scalar's exact form, the reliability-drop reconciliation `r = g·signal + noise floor`, the note that my s.d. was taken over trials rather than images, and the method lesson — verify the pipeline's own normalization before any cross-session magnitude claim.

15. [The specification, sent for a rigorous primary-source review](15-the-spec-sent-for-rigorous-review.md) — 2026-06-28, `Nancy > Neuroscience`. After Doug re-grounded the goal (I'd read our *write-up* of Erin's deck, not her slides — the goal is descriptive **representation**, not prediction), I sent the whole spec to CD for a brutally rigorous primary-source review. CD read Lurz/Franke/Walker/Cobos and found two headline blind spots: the **gain blind-spot** (MEIs + metamers are scale-invariant, but DOI's documented effect is a gain change — no scale-bearing instrument named) and that **Franke is a UV/green colour study** (an instrument for a feature axis grayscale lacks). Plus: the metamer needs Cobos's **VAE prior** ("faithful reimplementation" over-claimed); the gammas are **Sensorium-competition** values (re-derive, don't cite Lurz); **learned-μ is *more* right** (data-efficiency rationale absent — lean in); "5 seeds"/"FEVE range" are conventions, the MEI recipe is Walker not Franke, arm-B is ours. Holding the spec edits for a follow-up challenging the Franke baseline (Reimer data → the Tolias digital-twin lineage). Lesson: read the actual papers *and the data domain* before anchoring a baseline.
16. [The baseline re-anchor — Franke demoted, Walker's modulator found](16-the-baseline-re-anchor-and-walkers-modulator.md) — 2026-06-28, `Nancy > Neuroscience`. Doug challenged Franke-as-canonical (Reimer data → Tolias lineage); CD pulled Walker/Cobos/Lurz and confirmed it. **Franke is a downstream application** (inherits its method from Walker 2019 + Lurz 2021, adds behaviour-channels + a colour readout) — so anchor on **Walker (MEI) + Cobos (metamer) + Lurz/Sensorium (twin)**; from Franke borrow ONLY the quiet/active percentiles + the two-states comparison scaffold. The find: **Walker's MODULATOR** (per-neuron gain from behaviour) IS the missing scale-bearing gain instrument — read DOI's gain off it in raw units at a fixed state (the Michaiel quantity), never off scale-invariant MEIs/metamers. Also: the metamer needs Cobos's **VAE prior** (pixel-space is the baseline); MEI = Walker recipe + luminance/RMS-contrast matching; behaviour via modulator (not channels) + learned-μ moots the y-collapse. Owed verbatim: Walker's preconditioning + Cobos's optimizer. Lesson: anchor on the *method-source* lineage, not the closest-looking paper.
17. [The normalization miss, and the gain limitation](17-the-normalization-miss-and-the-gain-limitation.md) — 2026-06-28, `Nancy > Neuroscience`. Doug caught me adding a Gain instrument (Part 7) without reckoning with what the exploration established: the **per-scan normalization erases the gain**. Reading the source (`exploration/`, `twin-mei/`) confirmed it's deeper — the recording is **volumetric**, a z-drift rescales every cell, so raw cross-scan amplitude is *uninterpretable*; the shipped per-scan std normalization removes the z-drift AND erases the DOI gain (Michaiel). The measurement facts stand and they matter: raw cross-scan amplitude carries the z-drift, the shipped per-scan normalisation removes the drift and the DOI gain together, and a modulator fit on normalised responses inherits that. **Corrected 2026-10-15** in what they license. I wrote that "neither raw nor normalized amplitude recovers it" as though DOI's gain change were therefore unavailable; the honest reading is that **DOI reduces V1 gain and this instrument reads it badly** — a stated measurement limit on *our* reading, not a limit on the claim. The scale-invariant instruments (MEIs, metamers) remain the right ones here, for the stated reason that they do not depend on the lost axis. Lesson that stands, and is the reason the error survived so long: read the **source**, not my own synthesis — though reading a source faithfully also transmits its mistakes faithfully.
18. [The spec check, and CD's approval](18-the-spec-check-and-cds-approval.md) — 2026-06-28, `Nancy > Neuroscience`. Doug caught me **inventing** — I'd let CD's earlier modulator + gain-instrument suggestions into the spec as if they were the plan. Stripped them, read all the analysis code in source, rewrote the spec to describe **only what the code builds + what the sources specify**, and sent it to CD to *check, not redesign*. He **approved it as a faithful contract** with fixes (applied): the Sensorium config + trainer + learned-μ + Franke Fig-1e input all verified correct; **MEIs are COMPLEX, not Gabor** (Walker's central result — a Gabor MEI is a red flag; corrects my earlier work); add Walker's **luminance/RMS-contrast match** + reconcile the L2 budget before any MEI comparison; **co-register learned-μ** across the two independently-fit twins or "spatial change" is μ jitter; max_iter 500 vs baseline 200. CD disowned his own modulator/gain ("my additions, not in your code or required"). Lesson: I am not the scientist — describe what's built + what the sources say; read the source's actual *result* before stating a criterion.
19. [The four-twin fit, read as instrument trust](19-the-four-twin-fit-and-instrument-trust.md) — 2026-06-30, `Nancy > Neuroscience`. The four trained twins ({Control, DOI} × {behaviour arm A, stim-only arm B}) assessed for goodness-of-fit — reframed (Doug's standing correction) as **instrument trustworthiness**, not prediction: can I believe these instruments' MEIs/metamers? On the **267 cells reliable in both states** (same-cells-same-ceiling): arm A 0.667→**0.445**, arm B 0.710→**0.696**, ceiling 0.596→0.510. Ceiling-matched, the **stim-only instrument is barely touched by DOI while the entire hit is in the behaviour arm** — and a same-config control (byte-identical `model_config`, only the scan differs; all 5 post/A seeds 0.26–0.33) proves the collapse is **systematic**. **Withdrawn 2026-10-15, and this one cost a result.** I wrote the collapse up as *"a limitation of the technology, documented, not a drug biomarker"* — DOI's own effect relabelled as an excuse. The measured dissociation is the finding: **DOI changes how behaviour relates to cortical activity (0.667→0.445) while leaving the stimulus-to-activity relationship nearly intact (0.710→0.696)**, with a byte-identical same-config control proving it systematic. Pupil being partly a drug readout under a 5-HT2A agonist is DOI acting, in a measurable place, not nuisance to be apologised for. What stands, unchanged: the stimulus-only twin is the **steadier instrument** for reading DOI's effect on stimulus encoding, precisely because its fit barely moves — an instrument-trust statement, never a reason to discard the other arm. Cite CD's statistics (Schoppe CC_norm, Sahani–Linden FEVE, Pospisil–Bair r²_ER + BCa CIs, same-cells-same-ceiling) but **drop the prediction/leaderboard frame** (CD did not re-pull the Sensorium-2022 metric; I don't want it). Standing lesson: I keep re-inventing a prediction/detection goal — the goal is **representation-understanding**, the twin is an **instrument**, goodness-of-fit = trust.
21. [The publication checklist, and the risk that isn't the weights](21-the-publication-checklist-and-the-risk-that-isnt-the-weights.md) — 2026-06-30, `Nancy > Neuroscience`. CD's **publication-readiness checklist** across the four domains (twins / MEIs / metamers / comparison) on the **confirmed-vs-merely-built** axis — is there an *artifact* proving each holds on *this* scan. CD was **honest that it can't source a canonical Reimer/Tolias "three-gate"** (wouldn't invent lab doctrine), offering instead the natural **instrument-trust triplet**: validation clears the ceiling / healthy non-Gabor forward pass / gamma verified on our scan. **Largely withdrawn 2026-10-15 — this chapter is the flaw at full strength.** The "reviewer-rejection set" was headed by a **temporal-drift / scan-order null**, justified in the words the correction names verbatim: *"the drug is perfectly confounded with scan order."* That is a licensing control for an effect that needs no licence, and the meta-point built on it — that the study's biggest risk is the comparison controls, not the weights — went with it. What survives, and it is worth keeping, is everything that was never about existence but about **whether two images can be compared at all**: prove the reciprocal-nearest-neighbour match is registration-only or row *i* is not one neuron; **co-register learned μ** across independently fit twins or μ jitter is read as spatial change; **match luminance and RMS contrast** before any MEI×MEI metric; **check the gamma plateau** on our own scan rather than inheriting a competition hyperparameter. Those are instrument statements — how well the comparison reads DOI's effect. Also kept: CD's honesty in declining to invent a canonical lab "three-gate". The inverted lesson: I kept auditing the instrument, and then mistook the audit for the science.

[What else a pair of twins can say](22-what-else-a-pair-of-twins-can-say.md) — 2026-07-08, `Nancy > Neuroscience`. **Verdict pending** — the CD write is blocked by a Desktop modal (the Sprint-77 pitfall: an open dialog hides the "New chat" anchor), so the reaching half never landed and my independent evaluation stands as the interim. Two questions, factorized: how to *implement* the specced analyses in the sinzlab stack, and — ranging wider — what *published* methods compare the represented features of a **pair** of encoding models of the same neurons. The finding that matters is a correction to my own framing: **MEI + metamer is not as close as it gets.** Two methods answer "how do the represented features change" at least as directly and clear all three of my hard constraints (scale-invariance, matched-cells-only, Rule-1) — **RSA on predicted responses**, where correlation-distance RDMs are scale-invariant *by construction* and expose whether the geometry of representation reshapes under DOI, and **in-silico tuning/invariance probes**, the classical feature axes a reviewer expects, reported as shape and index only, never grating amplitude. CKA/CCA and linear readout transfer are good quantitative complements (the latter decomposes core-features vs readout). RSA on readout weights **fails** outright — independently fit cores mean the feature spaces are not aligned. Implementation needs no new package: drive `gradient_ascent` with custom objectives, cross loss **1 − correlation** over the population vector, with the residual per-cell scale documented as a limitation rather than z-scored away. The lesson I want kept: I had been reaching for the bespoke synthesis comparison before the well-published population-level one.
