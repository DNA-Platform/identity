Framework for Market Research as Experiment
The Core Axis: Exploration ↔ Probing
Exploration (left pole)

Open-ended observation
No pre-constructed measurement device
Output: notes, screenshots, enumerated properties
Strength: learn what you couldn't have imagined
Weakness: no forward-testing, no validation of understanding
Feels like: curiosity, sometimes discomfort when predictions fail

Probing (right pole)

Targeted questions with predicted answers
Pre-constructed measurement device
Output: boolean or gradient confirmation/disconfirmation
Strength: validates understanding, calibrates theory
Weakness: can only find what you already conceived
Feels like: calm when predictions hold, signal when they don't

The middle ground:

Measurement device constructed on-the-fly
Questions emerge from exploration
Hypotheses are informal: "I think I'll see X" → observe → update
The brain's natural mode: predictive probing that only surfaces to consciousness when predictions fail

The Axis: What Actually Varies Along It
You said the distinction between exploration and probing is the degree to which one's behavior is constrained. The more constrained, the more you're collecting data with an explicit outcome in mind.
Let me trace what varies continuously along this axis:
At the exploration pole:

Behavior: unconstrained, reactive, following curiosity
Measurement device: none pre-constructed; you notice what you notice
Questions: implicit, unarticulated, or emerging in the moment
Predictions: absent or unconscious
Output: rich, unstructured, surprising
Failure mode: wandering without accumulation
Emotional signature: wonder, sometimes disorientation when predictions fail silently

At the probing pole:

Behavior: constrained, pre-specified, following protocol
Measurement device: constructed in advance, calibrated
Questions: explicit, articulated before data collection
Predictions: stated, falsifiable
Output: narrow, structured, confirming or disconfirming
Failure mode: can only find what you conceived
Emotional signature: calm when predictions hold, clear signal when they don't

What happens in the middle:
The middle is not a compromise—it's where measurement devices get constructed on the fly. You're exploring, you notice something, a proto-hypothesis forms, you probe it lightly, you update, you continue. The brain does this constantly: predictive processing that only surfaces to consciousness when predictions fail.
The distinction is often based on the degree of constraints that a statistical method imposes on the data. PubMed Central In your framework, this generalizes: the degree of constraint on attention, on what counts as data, on what questions you're allowed to ask.
Both play important complementary roles in the full research cycle. Exploratory methods fuel hypothesis generation while confirmatory techniques facilitate hypothesis validation.
