If you disagree
If you disagree with the information in this notice, or require additional explanation, follow the instructions below. You
can respond online, or by fax, or mail. We will review the information you provide and notify you of our findings.
Respond online
If you already have an Online Services account, you may be able to resolve this matter quickly and easily.
Visit the Tax Department's website, log in to your account, and use the Respond to Department Notice online service.
When prompted, enter the following:
Document Locator Number: PF2600454166 Notice ID: DTF-160
If you do not have an Online Services account, you can visit our website to create one.
Respond by fax or mail
You can send us a written explanation of why you disagree with this notice. Be sure to include:
— copies of any documents that support your position, such as canceled checks or other proof of payment
— the Document Locator Number and Notice ID that appear above
— your taxpayer identification number
— your daytime phone number
— a completed power of attorney (or other authorization form), if applicable
Send by fax: 518-435-2990; or mail to: NYS TAX DEPARTMENT, OPTS-Liability Correspondence Section-Income, WA
HARRIMAN CAMPUS, ALBANY NY 12227-0882.
If not using U.S. Mail, see Publication 55, Designated Private Delivery Services.
Protest rights:
You have the right to protest this notice in either of two ways:
— File From CMS-1-MN, Request for Conciliation Conference (available on our website, search: croslmn). Or you
may call 518-530-4179 and request to have the form mailed to you.
Note: Making this call does not extend your time to file the request, or
— File Form TA-100, Petition, (available at www.dta.ny.gov) to request a tax appeals hearing.
For more information, visit our website (search: protest).
You must file your protest within two years from the date of this notice.
For a full explanation of your rights as a taxpayer, visit our website (search: rights).
Authorized representative
If you are not responding online and would like someone to contact us on your behalf, you must give them permission
to do so. You can do this in any of the following ways:
— using the Tax Department's EZ-Rep/TR-2000 (the easiest and fastest way);
— designating them as your third-party designee on your New York State return or credit form; or
— filing a completed Power of Attorney (Form POA-1).
Questions?
You may call us at 518-485-6549.
Text telephone (TTY) or TDD users: Dial 7-1-1 for the New York Relay Service.
