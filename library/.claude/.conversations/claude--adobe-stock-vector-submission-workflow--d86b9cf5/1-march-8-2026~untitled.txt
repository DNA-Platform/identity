# Stock Metadata Research — Competitor Keyword Analysis

> Actual keyword lists pulled from top-selling stock map vectors on Alamy and Adobe Stock.
> Use these as reference when writing metadata for new maps.

## Methodology

Keywords were extracted by fetching individual listing pages from Alamy (which shows full keyword lists publicly, unlike Adobe Stock which gates them). Each listing below was fetched via `WebFetch` and the complete metadata recorded.

## Patterns Across All 6 Competitors

### Keyword Count
| Listing | Keywords |

| Furian (Hormuz) | 49 |

| Bardocz (Iran) | 47 |
| pytyczech (Levant) | 49 |

| Naeblys (Israel/Lebanon) | 47 |
| **Average** | **47.8** |

**Takeaway**: Top sellers use 42-49 keywords on Adobe Stock (hard limit 49). Nikolaenkow's 51 was on Alamy which has a higher limit. Our target: exactly 49.

### Keyword Structure
1. **Single words dominate** (80%+): `iran`, `map`, `gulf`, `conflict`, `cartography`
2. **Simple pairs** (~15%): `persian gulf`, `middle east`, `saudi arabia`, `oil trade`
3. **Rare 3+ word phrases** (<5%): `strait of hormuz`, `united arab emirates`, `iran israel war`
4. **NO long buyer-intent phrases**: Nobody uses "Iran military strike map" as a keyword

### Universal Keywords (appear in 4+ of 6 listings)
> iran, map, middle east, illustration, gulf, persian gulf, country, region, geography, cartography, iraq, israel, saudi arabia, conflict, war, political, arab, arabia, arabian, sea, jordan, syria, lebanon, oman, qatar, uae, egypt, emirates

### Category-Specific Vocabulary
- **Conflict/News maps**: crisis, tension, violence, clash, confrontation, battle, military, missile, attack
- **Geographic/Reference maps**: atlas, cartography, topography, contour, outline, border, territory, labeled, editable
- **Energy/Economic maps**: oil, oil trade, oil reserves, shipping, maritime, trade, transport, pipeline, tanker, strategic
- **Cultural/Historical**: persia, persian, mesopotamia, levant, semite, islamic

### Title Patterns
- **Descriptive sentence format**: "[What it is]. [Key detail]. [Another detail]."
- **Titles often exceed 70 chars** — the guideline is for display truncation, not a hard limit
- **Geographic specificity in title**: List the countries/features shown
- **Style indicator**: "Vector map illustration", "political map", "geographic chart"

## How To Use This Research

When writing metadata for a new map:

1. **Start with the universal keywords** that match your map's region
2. **Add every geographic proper noun** visible on the map (cities, countries, bodies of water, features)
3. **Add conceptual/emotional terms** appropriate to the map's theme (from the category vocabulary above)
4. **Add cartographic/style terms**: map, illustration, cartography, geography, political, labeled, etc.
5. **Target 48-52 keywords total**
6. **Use single words or simple 2-word pairs**, not long phrases
7. **Title should be a descriptive sentence** with geographic specificity
