Think of every English sentence as a tree generated by a small set of rewrite rules, similar to a context-free grammar (CFG).

S → NP VP
where

S

Everything you will ever say in English is a variant or elaboration of this one rule.

Category	Function	Examples

Verb (V)	denotes an action or state	run, think, be
Adjective (Adj)	modifies nouns	red, tall, broken
Adverb (Adv)	modifies verbs/adjectives/clauses	quickly, very, often

Lesson 7: Recursion and Embedding
