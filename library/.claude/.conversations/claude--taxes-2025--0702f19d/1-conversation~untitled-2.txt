Without cost basis, the conservative default is zero basis (full proceeds taxable as gain), but Doug should try to recover the actual basis first.

Coincidentally equal to the federal withholding, but they are NOT the same thing — the withholding goes on 1040 line 25b, the penalty goes on Schedule 2 line 8.

4. **NO state tax was withheld on the IRA distribution.** NY will tax the $46,500 distribution as ordinary income on IT-201. This is an owed-at-filing item.

5. **Foreign tax credit: $78.37.** Under the $300 single-filer de minimis — can claim directly on Schedule 3 line 1 without filing Form 1116.
