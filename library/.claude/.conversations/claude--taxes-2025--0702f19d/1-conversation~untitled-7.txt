Patrick can fill the forms today using the data we have, but will need expert clarification on the gaps below before producing the final filings.

**Confirmation request:** Should Coinbase short-term Box H totals be entered on Schedule D Line 3 (treating Box H as the digital-asset analog of Box C), or does the IRS expect a separate line that we haven't seen on this form?

### 🔴 4. IT-204 coverage is severely incomplete

IT-204 has **151 numbered lines across 11 sections** spanning 9 pages. The guide covers **maybe 10 lines**.

Sections the guide does not address at all:
- **Section 2 detail (Lines 1–26)** — federal income/deductions per Form 1065 Page 1, line by line. The guide says "copy ordinary income and guaranteed payments from federal" but the form actually requires every income/deduction line individually.
- **Section 3 (Lines 27–39)** — Cost of Goods Sold from Form 1125-A. All $0 for our service business but the lines exist.
- **Section 4 (Lines 40–65)** — Balance sheets per books. Should be skipped under small partnership exception, but please confirm whether IT-204 honors that exception (the form has the balance sheet section regardless).
- **Section 5 (Lines 66–84)** — Reconciliation of income per books with income per return (M-1 and M-2 equivalents). Same exception question.
- **Section 6 (Lines 75–84)** — Capital accounts analysis (M-2 equivalent).
- **Section 7 (Lines 86–106)** — Partners' share of income, deductions, etc. **This is the NY analog of federal Schedule K and DOES need to be filled.** Lines 86 (ordinary income), 90 (guaranteed payments), and 100 (intentionally left blank) are the relevant ones for us.
- **Section 8 (Lines 107–115)** — NY modifications. Lines 107, 109, 115 are "intentionally left blank." Lines 111a–111f are NY additions to itemized deductions; 113a–113f are subtractions. All $0 for us but the lines exist.
- **Section 9 (Lines 116–117)** — NY-specific other info. Line 116a is "New York source gross income" — must be $0. Line 117c asks if the partnership is a partner in another partnership — No.
- **Section 10 (Lines 118–126)** — NY allocation schedule. Line 118 is the books-and-records question (Yes/No). Lines 119–126 only apply if No to 118. We say Yes (we use books and records, no NY source).
- **Section 11 (Lines 127–151)** — Partners' credit information. All $0 for us (no NY tax credits).

**Fix needed:** The guide says "Federal income/loss section: Copy from federal" but the IT-204 form actually requires line-by-line entry. Please either give Patrick a per-line table (preferred) or confirm a "leave non-essential lines blank" rule.
