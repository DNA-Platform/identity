Or in a document on a computer that can be executed as a simulation. 

Dad: Sure a model describes a mapping, but to what exactly? It seems to me that a model of physics is a metaphor for a collection of hypothetical conscious experiences that we prefer to call measurements or observations. But those things are inexplicable. The model does not acknowledge them. It tried to explain them away, as if no one ever had to observe the universe for us to have a model of it. So I prefer to think of the model as a metaphor
