You're absolutely right! In FOL, finiteness is expressed through axioms, not domain constraints. Here are the standard approaches:

Method 1: Explicit Enumeration

∀x: x = a₁ ∨ x = a₂ ∨ ... ∨ x = aₙ
"Everything is one of these specific elements"

Method 2: Bounded Existence

∃x₁,x₂,...,xₙ: ∀y: y = x₁ ∨ y = x₂ ∨ ... ∨ y = xₙ
"There exist exactly n things, and everything is one of them"

Method 3: Cardinality Bounds

¬∃x₁,x₂,...,xₙ₊₁: x₁ ≠ x₂ ≠ ... ≠ xₙ₊₁
"You can't find n+1 distinct elements"
