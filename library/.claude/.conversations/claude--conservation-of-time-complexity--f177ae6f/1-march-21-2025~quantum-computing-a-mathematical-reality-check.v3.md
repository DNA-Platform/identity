**Quantum Computing: A Mathematical Reality Check**

After decades of quantum hype, let's do some actual math:

Quantum factorization progress:
- 2001: 15
- 2012: 21
- 2022: 35

RSA encryption: 2048-4096 bits

Quantum progress rate = 0.059 bits/year
Years to break 3072-bit RSA = (3072 - 5.13) ÷ 0.059 = 52,055 years

Meanwhile, encryption complexity grows at 4.06% annually.
By 2100: RSA ≈ 66,594 bits vs. quantum capability ≈ 9.7 bits

The gap is widening, not closing.

I'll believe quantum computing when they build ONE fully fault-tolerant qubit. We're still at zero.

This isn't skepticism. This is arithmetic.
