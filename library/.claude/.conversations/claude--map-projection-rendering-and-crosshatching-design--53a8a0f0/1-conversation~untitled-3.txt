## GROUP A — THE ROAD AS FINGERPRINT

**001**
NEW YORK | United States of America | *"The city that never sleeps"*
CENTER: 40.7484°N, 73.9967°W — this is 34th Street and 8th Avenue. Not Midtown.
Centering here puts the Empire State Building at center, the island tip at bottom of circle,
72nd Street at top. Manhattan reads as a complete vertical object, not a crop.
RADIUS: 5.5km — tight enough that Manhattan dominates but both rivers are visible voids.
INCLUDE: OSM highway=motorway, trunk, primary, secondary, tertiary, residential.
EXCLUDE: All features west of lon -74.020 (eliminates New Jersey road bleed).
Exclude all features east of lon -73.900 (eliminates Queens/Brooklyn clutter beyond the rivers).
OVERRIDES: Broadway (name=Broadway) → 1.4px / 0.85 — the 29° diagonal must read
distinctly through the residential grid or the composition loses its signature.
FDR Drive → 1.6px (not 2.2px — pen tier at standard weight fuses with adjacent roads).
West Side Highway → 1.6px same reason.
Avenues (5th, Park, Madison, Lexington) → 0.8px / 0.55 — the north-south spines.
QUADRANTS: Top-left: Upper West Side grid + small Hudson void entering circle.
Top-right: Upper East Side grid + East River void entering circle.
Bottom-left: Hell's Kitchen + Midtown West. Bottom-right: Midtown East + Turtle Bay.
Center horizontal band: the dense crosstown streets from 14th to 57th. Central Park void
appears as a large rectangle breaking the grid in the upper 30% of circle.
PALETTE: Latitude 40.7° → bg #F4EEE2, ink #1C1408 (warm parchment / brown-black)

**002**
NEW YORK — THE ISLAND | United States of America | *"Thirteen miles long, two miles wide"*
CENTER: 40.7282°N, 73.9942°W — this is lower Manhattan, Canal Street.
Centering here puts the island tip (Battery Park) in the lower quarter,
and cuts off above 96th Street. Central Park appears as a long rectangular
silence in the top 25% of the circle. Below it: unbroken grid.
RADIUS: 4.5km — shows the full island width with both rivers as frame voids.
The island is 2.3km wide at its widest. At 4.5km radius, the island takes up
exactly the middle 51% of circle width, with each river as a dark void on either side.
INCLUDE: Same as 001.
EXCLUDE: Same lon exclusions as 001. Also exclude all features north of lat 40.797°N
(keeps the circle from showing Harlem, preserves the park-as-silence reading).
OVERRIDES: Broadway → 1.3px / 0.9, continuous from tip to park edge.
The diagonal is the ONLY non-grid element in lower Manhattan — it must be visible.
Wall Street / Fulton / John Street cluster in Financial District → keep at pencil tier,
their pre-grid organic layout is historically interesting texture at the bottom of the island.
QUADRANTS: Top: Central Park rectangle, solid cream void, hard edges.
Left: Hudson River — pure void, roads stop abruptly at waterline.
Right: East River — pure void, roads stop abruptly, FDR Drive traces the shore.
Bottom: island tip, roads converge and terminate. Financial District.
Center: the Manhattan grid at maximum density, 155 crosstown streets.
PALETTE: Same as 001 — this is a companion piece, same palette required.

**003**
CHICAGO | United States of America | *"City of the big shoulders"*
CENTER: 41.8827°N, 87.6233°W — the Loop, intersection of State and Madison.
RADIUS: 9km — gets Lake Shore Drive, Lincoln Park, the river forks, and the lakefront void.
INCLUDE: OSM highway=motorway, trunk, primary, secondary, tertiary, residential.
EXCLUDE: All features east of lon -87.600 (eliminates lake — lake is a void, not a feature).
Actually: do NOT exclude — let the lake be naturally absent. Roads stop at the shore.
OVERRIDES: Lake Shore Drive (name=Lake Shore Drive) → 1.8px / 1.0 — this is the defining
arc of Chicago, hugging the lakefront. Must read as a bold curve defining the void edge.
Chicago River north branch + south branch → render as NHD Flowline at 1.5px / 0.28 opacity.
The river fork is the other signature of Chicago and must be a different color.
QUADRANTS: Right: Lake Michigan — complete void, right 30% of circle empty.
Top: Lincoln Park void (park, then lake). Lake Shore Drive traces this edge.
Center: the Loop grid, then the river fork cutting diagonally NW and N.
Left: the residential grid extending west — regular, slightly angled (8° east of true north).
Bottom: the South Side grid, regular and dense, extending to the circle edge.
PALETTE: Latitude 41.9° → bg #F2EDE4, ink #1A1A1A (standard — Chicago is the reference)

**004**

The contrast between the Quarter's regularity and the curves around it is the composition.

**005**
WASHINGTON D.C. | United States of America | *"A city of magnificent distances"*
CENTER: 38.8951°N, 77.0369°W — the Capitol Building. Not the White House. Not the Mall center.
The Capitol as center puts the Mall running left across the circle with the Lincoln Memorial
reaching the left edge, and the residential quadrants of SE and NE in the right half.
RADIUS: 6km — keeps the composition tight around the planned city. At 6km,
all of L'Enfant's original diagonal avenues are visible without the outer suburbs swamping them.
INCLUDE: OSM highway=motorway, trunk, primary, secondary, tertiary, residential.
EXCLUDE: All features south of lat 38.820°N (across the Potomac into Virginia — different grid,
different political entity, compositional pollution).
Features west of lon -77.120°W (Alexandria VA, different grid again).
OVERRIDES: Pennsylvania Avenue → 2.0px / 1.0 — the axis of American power, must be the
dominant diagonal in the composition. It runs from the Capitol center-left to the White House.
Massachusetts, Connecticut, New Hampshire avenues → 1.4px / 0.8 — the radial spines.
The Mall (bounded by Constitution Ave N and Independence Ave S) → render the two bounding
avenues at 0.8px / 0.4 and let the Mall itself be a void between them. Width ~300m.
Rock Creek → NHD Flowline at 1.2px at 0.30 opacity (same ink as roads, lighter weight), threading upper-left quadrant.
QUADRANTS: Left: The Mall void + Lincoln Memorial. Potomac River void bottom-left.
Top-left: Dupont Circle / Georgetown grid with diagonal avenues creating star patterns.
Top-right: Residential NE DC, regular grid. The Capitol itself at dead center.
Bottom: Capitol Hill neighborhood, then Anacostia River void bottom-right.
PALETTE: Latitude 38.9° → bg #F3EDE1, ink #1A1A14 (slightly warm neutral)

**007**
SAVANNAH | United States of America | *"The city of squares"*
CENTER: 32.0809°N, 81.0912°W — Johnson Square, the original center square.
RADIUS: 2.5km — the small radius is critical. Savannah's plan is COMPACT.
At 2.5km all 22 squares are visible and individually legible as voids.
At 5km the squares become too small to read and the composition loses its subject.
INCLUDE: OSM highway=primary, secondary, tertiary, residential WITHIN the radius.
OSM leisure=park WHERE the park corresponds to a town square (these are the voids).
EXCLUDE: All features north of lat 32.095°N (the river area) — they're industrial and break
the residential texture. Features south of lat 32.065°N — suburban sprawl.
OVERRIDES: Bull Street, Drayton Street, Abercorn Street, Barnard Street, Whitaker Street
(the north-south park axes) → 0.9px / 0.6 — slightly heavier so the rhythm of square→street→square reads.
The four trusts squares vs. the ward squares — no distinction needed, all rendered same.
QUADRANTS: Every quadrant should contain roughly 5–6 square voids arranged in a grid.
The Savannah River appears at the top edge of the circle as a wide void.
Forsyth Park (2 blocks large) appears at the bottom as a larger void.
The regularity is total. This is a product about mathematical planning.
PALETTE: Age palette — Savannah founded 1733. bg #CEB98A (deep parchment), ink #261406 (sepia-dark)

**008**
SALT LAKE CITY | United States of America | *"Wide enough for a wagon and a team of oxen"*
CENTER: 40.7608°N, 111.8910°W — Temple Square.
RADIUS: 5km — the unusually wide streets register at this scale. At 8km the blocks
start to look normal-sized because context dissolves the scale cue. At 5km
the 132-foot street width (vs. standard 66-foot) is palpable.
INCLUDE: OSM highway=primary, secondary, tertiary, residential.
EXCLUDE: The Great Salt Lake is outside the radius, no exclusion needed.
Wasatch Mountains (east) are naturally void — roads stop at the foothills.
OVERRIDES: Temple Square itself → render as a solid void (leisure=park or amenity=place_of_worship).
The Temple block is the pivot point. North Temple, South Temple, East Temple (Main Street),
West Temple → 1.2px / 0.7 — the four bounding streets of the Temple block.
QUADRANTS: Center: the enormous block grid. Each block is ~660×660 feet — nearly twice
standard. The streets between blocks are wide enough to register as wide even at scale.
East quadrant: the grid ends abruptly at the Wasatch foothills. Mountains are void.
The grid is purely orthogonal — perfect cardinal alignment. No diagonals. No curves.
Top: the Capitol Hill area, slightly denser where roads climb the hill.
PALETTE: Age palette — founded 1847. bg #E0D4B8 (warm parchment), ink #201408
