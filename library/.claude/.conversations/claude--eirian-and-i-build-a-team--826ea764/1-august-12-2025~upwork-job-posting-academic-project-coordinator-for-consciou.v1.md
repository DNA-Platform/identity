# Academic Project Coordinator & Research Collaboration Specialist - Consciousness Studies

## Project Overview
We are seeking an experienced academic professional to serve as project coordinator for groundbreaking research in consciousness studies. Our work integrates formal logic, neuroscience, and philosophy of mind to develop new theoretical frameworks for understanding consciousness, with empirical validation through anesthesia research.

## About Our Research
Our team has developed novel theoretical contributions including:
- Formal mathematical models of consciousness using metalogical transduction
- Semantic Reference Theory applications to conscious experience
- Experimental validation through microtubule-stabilizer studies (published in eNeuro)
- Revolutionary approaches to the "hard problem" of consciousness

We need someone who can help us navigate the academic landscape and build the collaborative network necessary to bring this work to prominent journals and conferences.
