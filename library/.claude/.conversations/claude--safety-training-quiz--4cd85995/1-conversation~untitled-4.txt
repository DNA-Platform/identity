Retaliation

Retaliation is any action taken by BCM, its employees or agents that adversely affects the employment, contractual, or other institutional status of a BCM Community Member because the individual or entity has, in good faith, made an allegation concerning the violation of a law, rule, policy, procedure, inadequate institutional response to such violation, or has cooperated in good faith with an investigation of such allegation. Baylor Policy and various Federal and State laws prohibit retaliation

Important: Anyone who commits or condones retaliation will be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination. An individual who self-reports allegations of improper activity may still be subject to disciplinary action if they were involved in the improper activity

Retaliation does not excuse a reporter's improper actions

Engaging in protected activity does not shield an employee or learner from all discipline or discharge. Employers are free to discipline or terminate workers if motivated by non-retaliatory and non-discriminatory reasons that would otherwise result in such consequences and as long as it does not have a “chilling” effect on any future complaints/claims.
