These terms treat an explanation as a blueprint, system, or machine rather than a simple sentence.
• Explanans: In the philosophy of science (specifically the Hempel-Oppenheim model), the explanans is the formal object containing the laws and initial conditions that logically entail the phenomenon being explained (the explanandum).
• Construct: A conceptual object or framework intentionally built from simpler elements to explain a complex phenomenon.

• Fabulation: The psychological or creative act of inventing an explanation or narrative to fill a gap in reality, often believed by the person telling it.
