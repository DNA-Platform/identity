When a Partnerships return is filed late, we charge a penalty. The penalty is $255.00
for each partner during the year, for each month the return is late, for up to 12 months,
per IRC Section 6698.
Revenue Procedure 84-35, provides that reasonable cause for filing late will be
presumed, and the penalty waived, if all of the following criteria are met:
• The partnership has no more than 10 partners. (Two partners filing a joint return can
be counted as a single partner.)
• Every partner is an individual or the estate of a deceased individual.
• Every partner filed a timely income tax return where all partnership distributive items
were properly reported.
• Every partner's percentage share is the same for every distributive partnership item
for that partner. (E.g., if a partner receives a 20% share of the partnership's net
income, that partner must also receive a 20% share of all other partnership items.)
The partnership must not have made an election to be subject to consolidated audit
procedures that is still in effect.
If the partnership qualifies for relief under this provision, return this notice with your
request asking for relief under Rev. Proc. 84-35. The request must specifically state the
partnership meets each of the criteria above, and it must be signed under penalties of
perjury.
We understand that circumstances-such as a serious illness or injury, a family
