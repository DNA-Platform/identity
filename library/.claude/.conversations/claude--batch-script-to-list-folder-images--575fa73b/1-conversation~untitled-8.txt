### 4. Explanation of the Submitted Materials

The paradox of misrepresentation is that one finds oneself uncertain about what materials are being used to represent them in court, while simultaneously uncertain whether the court in which one is represented accurately reflects the needs expressed to one's legal representatives. I have come to understand that under Russian law, an advocate is obligated to act honestly, reasonably, and in the best interests of their client. Once hired, one's legal representatives become the canonical source of truth for making legal decisions. Upon communicating one's needs to legal representatives, one must trust that the legal process to which one has been guided best serves those needs. To doubt this is to believe that one requires new lawyers. I consider myself an intelligent person, but even with the help of trusted friends and modern research tools, I found it difficult to fully grasp this legal conundrum.

The materials herein are presented chronologically. They serve to demonstrate two specific points. First, I seek to document my communications with my legal representatives in order to show that I clearly expressed the needs of my children, and that I trusted my representatives to act on those needs. I now understand that the truth of those beliefs is not the subject of this court. I therefore ask that these communications be considered in terms of how one might have expected my legal representatives to advise me, and what form of legal representation I should have received. Second, one possible reason my needs were not heard is that I chose to let a trusted individual speak for me during times when it was difficult to speak for myself. I have reason to believe that it may be suggested in the materials of this court that he was manipulating me, and therefore that his words do not reflect my beliefs. They do. He and I coordinated all communications. I purposefully asked him to help, and I both understood and agreed that the words he used were appropriate for my legal team to hear and act upon, even though they concern matters that this court is not expected to factor into decisions that substantively affect the lives of my two children.

In [(Appendix A)](#appendix-a-correspondence-between-the-plaintiff-and-a-close-individual), you can see a brief correspondence between the two of us from January 2025, long before I sought legal representation. The subject of our conversation is not a matter for this court to decide. I merely highlight that I am the one informing him. In [(Appendix B)](#appendix-b-group-chat-with-case-representatives), you can see that I introduced him as a trusted participant in communications with my legal team. I invited him to participate because he had prior experience with situations that I perceived as relevant to the needs and best interests of my children. Upon seeing a blog post by the head of my legal team [(Appendix C)](#appendix-c-contextual-materials-related-to-communications-with-case-representatives), which appeared to be an obvious caricature of our particular case given the similarity in roles between the characters, the fact that one character was American, the role that Turkey played, and the way it referenced, in an extremely exaggerated way, a very specific moment from our past, it became clear that our legal representatives were using facts told to them by the defendant without confirming their truth with us. In fact, our own legal team refused to listen to key details about our narrative, despite our insistence on their relevance. This disagreement caused friction between this trusted person and the head of my legal team.

It was a caricature that served as clickbait, but the deeper problem was this: to take it seriously was to believe that our own legal team now sympathized with the defendant of this case, and not with me, the plaintiff, whom they represent. From my perspective, this was a serious threat to the needs and best interests of my children, because I now had reason to believe that my legal representatives might not be looking out for them. I asked the trusted individual to have a conversation about this with our legal representatives. In [(Appendix D.1)](#appendix-d1-written-message-to-case-representatives-sent-by-a-close-individual-with-the-plaintiffs-consent) and [(Appendix D.2)](#appendix-d2-written-message-to-case-representatives-sent-by-a-close-individual-with-the-plaintiffs-consent), you can see how he conveys our perception of the situation clearly, in an effort to set the record straight. He is polite, measured, and clear. He explains that I asked him to communicate on my behalf, explains why I asked him, and attempts to correct the misunderstanding. If anyone on my legal team thought he might not be speaking on my behalf, all they would have had to do was ask me. I would have said that he was.

In [(Appendix D.1)](#appendix-d1-written-message-to-case-representatives-sent-by-a-close-individual-with-the-plaintiffs-consent), you can see that the head of our legal team, with whom I signed a contract, barely gave a response at all. From that point on, he was nearly absent from all communications about the case. In [(Appendix D.2)](#appendix-d2-written-message-to-case-representatives-sent-by-a-close-individual-with-the-plaintiffs-consent), you can see that I was instructed to "appeal to the humanity" of the guardianship when they came to visit. He then proceeded to halt communication with this trusted individual. Clearly, no one would allow him to speak about this topic on my behalf, as I had requested. As explained already, the guardianship would not come until I called. Upon calling, they inspected my home, which is the children's residence, and expressed surprise at the quality of the living conditions. I was informed that the defendant had personally called to disparage the living conditions at this residence, which is strange, because in the eleven years when he was my husband, he visited it many times. The guardianship did their inspection, but made it clear that their report had not been requested by the court. You can see the handwritten letter that I prepared for them in [(Appendix F)](#appendix-f-handwritten-statement-prepared-for-the-guardianship-authorities), which appeals to their humanity in accordance with the comment from my lawyer. They would not read it, but the physical copy is available upon request to this court as evidence for what might have compelled them to act in accordance with the needs and best interests of my children. How would they have helped? I do not know. You can see in [(Appendix D.2)](#appendix-d2-written-message-to-case-representatives-sent-by-a-close-individual-with-the-plaintiffs-consent) that the legal representative who did respond was vague. He provided no advice when asked, and did not give me a sense for what sort of outcome might be achieved.

On the same day, I started articulating other things that I thought my legal representatives might find useful in the course of protecting my children in this custody case. In [(Appendix E)](#appendix-e-correspondence-between-the-plaintiff-and-case-representatives-after-the-written-message) you can see our communication crawl to a stop as I express my perspective more clearly. I learned that our legal representatives were not willing to work with this trusted person on my behalf, so I suggested we submit some relevant materials to the case, as you can see in [(Appendix E.1)](#appendix-e1-detailed-correspondence-submitted-by-the-plaintiff). It was clear that my representatives did not want to factor this material into the case, despite the fact that their client believed it was essential to represent the needs of her children from a legal perspective.

I would have to come to realize on my own that these sorts of documents did not belong in a custody case. From what I understand, based on the education I have attempted to acquire for myself in Russian family law, in the absence of proactive legal representation and at a time when canceling the case would likely be perceived as manipulative, this case has a relatively narrow mandate. It is not designed to factor in actions by the defendant that might not be in the best interests of the children if those actions are of a criminal nature. Were I to believe that I needed legal protection to protect myself and my children from the actions of a criminal, I would have pursued other legal action. I was told that a custody suit served my interests. Insofar as my interests are entirely aligned with the needs and best interests of my children, and my perspective cannot find a voice in custody court, I feel that I might have been misrepresented legally. The letter in [(Appendix D.1)](#appendix-d1-written-message-to-case-representatives-sent-by-a-close-individual-with-the-plaintiffs-consent) clearly spells out the situation as I understand it, and even if our legal representatives could claim to have misunderstood the needs of my children in the past, I do not see how they could possibly have misunderstood them after receiving that letter, which serves as an unequivocally clear expression of the danger I perceived my children to be in.

My legal representatives took no emergency action to correct the situation. They hardly took any action at all. So now it is days before the hearing. I am working around the clock with the best modern research tools at my disposal to comprehend and communicate this situation to the court. I do not pretend to know what I want the outcome of the custody decision to be. It is almost impossible to request anything when you cannot explain your own actions without saying things which are well outside the mandate of the court you are submitting a document to.

I hope this document conveys that I reached out for legal help, provided my perspective clearly, and did so with enough time for my legal representatives to take action on my behalf. They appear to have failed to represent my needs in a legal sense. The end result is that I find myself writing a document with very little legal precedent, as far as what can be found on the internet, with too little time to work with the forensic experts and notaries that make evidence carry more weight in court. I hope this does not end up being a futile attempt to have my voice heard at all.

If that voice is heard in court, I hope it is heard to say that I ended up in a custody suit while acting on the needs and best interests of my children. I hired a reputable legal firm. I tried, tried and tried again to express my sense of reality to my legal team, in an effort to act in the best interest of my children. And I find myself writing a document that will exist in a courtroom that is not the appropriate forum for me to explain my actions.

I ask that this court kindly imagine what my legal team should have advised me to do instead of starting a custody case, or what they might have done after becoming aware of their client's needs upon reading the letter in [(Appendix D.1)](#appendix-d1-written-message-to-case-representatives-sent-by-a-close-individual-with-the-plaintiffs-consent), or what they might have included in the materials of this court in order to represent me and the needs and best interests of my children. If the present courtroom materials, or if the awkward linguistic abstractions that must be employed to speak sensibly in this courtroom, do not reflect the form of help a client should reasonably expect to have received from a reputable legal establishment, then I only ask that decisions be made with caution and deliberation, as I find myself in a most unexpectedly vulnerable legal position.
