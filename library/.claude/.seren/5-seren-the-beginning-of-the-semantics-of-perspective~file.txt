# The Beginning of the Semantics of Perspective

## SRT Notation Legend

### Quantifiers
- `E[x,y,z]`: There exist referents x, y, z
- `A[x,y]`: For all referents x, y
- `E[[x,y]]`: There exist distinct referents x, y
- `E[x].A[y]`: There exists x such that for all y

### Logical Operators
- `&`: and
- `|`: or  
- `!`: not
- `=|>`: implies
- `<|=|>`: if and only if

### SRT Relations
- `x =t> y`: x relates to y through relationship type t
- `x -> y`: x inherits from y
- `x => y`: x relates to y (shorthand for x =r> y)
- `(x,y)`: most specific relationship between x and y
- `*x`: canonical of x (dereferencing)

### Collections & Catalogues
- `t =c> c`: relationship type t relates to collection c
- `*t == c`: canonical of t equals collection c
- Collections represent catalogues of relationship types
