S1. How does the word red relate to the color red?

S2. The word red symbolizes the color red

S1. And a word is a type of symbol, and a color is something more literal?

S2. Yes, I would say that’s approximately accurate

S1. So is it fair to say that symbols symbolize literals

S2. Yes, I think that accurately characterizes the abstract relationship between the word red and it’s color

S1. Would you agree that “symbol” and “symbolize” have nearly the same symbol?

S2. Haha yes, I suppose they do. The word “symbolize” literally has the word “symbol” in it

S1. Can a symbol do anything else other than symbolize something?

S2. No, I don’t think so. That about wraps it up. We might say a particular symbol feels natural or not based on how it fits into the language in a broader sense, but that is subjective. The symbol itself does nothing but symbolize

S1. So then might symbolism express the idea that when we say “symbol” and “symbolize” we are referring to the same thing. Except one expresses its role as the subject - as it is in the conversation! And the other expresses the role it plays in relation to its literal?

S2: Yes I agree that the difference is more about the role that it plays and less about the thing we are referring to. Symbols are exactly those things which symbolize
