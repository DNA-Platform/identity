Letters are like properties of a prototype-word

But they are not symbolic properties — they are formational ones

You could define a hasLetter property:

word =hasLetter> lᵢ
lᵢ =type> Grapheme


But again:

The word is the symbol

The letters are semantic conditions that give it form, but not identity

VII. Closing Formulation

So, in your language:

A word is a symbol, meaning (word, referent) == word.
A letter is an element in the collection that constitutes the symbol.
It is represented within the symbol, but it does not symbolize anything itself.
Its role is structural, not symbolic.

This shows how even symbols have internal representational structure, and how symbols can be collections, not atoms — as long as the symbolic relation refers to the whole.
