Here's the deal: there isn't a direct one-click "transfer" from an iOS App Store subscription to web/Stripe billing on Claude. Anthropic doesn't handle Apple billing — Apple does — so moving off iOS means cancelling on Apple's side and resubscribing on the web.
The process:
Cancel the iOS subscription first on your iPhone or iPad: Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Claude → Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period, so you won't lose anything immediately.
Wait until the iOS subscription period ends (or at least make sure it's cancelled) before resubscribing, otherwise you risk being double-billed.
Resubscribe on the web at claude.ai/settings/billing using the same account you've been signed into. Billing there is handled by Stripe (credit card).
One important thing to double-check: the account email on Claude must match how you were accessing the subscription on iOS. If you originally signed up using "Sign in with Apple" with Hide My Email, the email Claude has on file may differ from your real Apple ID email — that's caused headaches for some people. As long as you're logging into the same Claude account you've been using, just cancelling on Apple and resubscribing on the web should be straightforward.
If anything looks off after you resubscribe (e.g., features not unlocking), Claude support can be reached at support.claude.com.

Good news on the cancellation timing — that's exactly the right setup. But I want to flag something important on the second part of your question, because there's a real footgun here.
The login provider matters, not just the email. If you originally signed into Claude on iOS using "Sign in with Apple," your Claude account is tied to Apple as the auth provider — even if the email looks identical to your Google email. If you used "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple," your email address alone will not work as a sign-in credential — Claude treats the auth provider as part of the account identity.
There's a documented bug where signing in via a different provider with the same email address can silently create a duplicate Claude account under a different auth provider, locking the user out of their original paid account. People with Max subscriptions originally created via Apple Sign-In have hit this exact issue.
What I'd recommend:
Log in the same way you always have — if you've been using Sign in with Apple, keep using Sign in with Apple on the web (claude.ai supports it). Don't switch to "Continue with Google" on first login from the web, even if the underlying email address is the same.
Once you're logged into your existing account on the web, go to Settings → Billing and subscribe there with a credit card (Stripe). Do this on or after May 24th, once your iOS subscription has actually ended, to avoid double-billing.
If you really want to use Google OAuth going forward, get the subscription working first under your existing auth method, then contact Claude support to migrate the auth provider — don't try to do it by just logging in with Google, because that's what triggers the duplicate-account bug.
To directly answer your question: I can't confirm Google OAuth will continue your account on the web unless you originally signed up with Google. If you signed up via Apple on iOS, logging in with Google on the web will likely land you in a brand-new empty account, not your existing one.
