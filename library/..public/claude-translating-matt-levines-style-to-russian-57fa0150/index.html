Here's the translation. A few notes on the Levine-voice choices:
The hardest part was keeping that signature Levine move where he states something absurd-sounding with total deadpan confidence and then lets the reader catch up — "Это не метафора" hits differently in Russian because Russian prose tends toward more hedging. I kept it blunt. Same with the one-liner closers ("Это обычный вторник в фотостудии", "Это не философия. Это вычисление.").
I preserved the second-person "ты" throughout rather than "вы" — Levine writes like he's talking to one person at a bar, not lecturing an audience, and "ты" captures that. The title "Физичные штуки" is deliberately colloquial — "штуки" has that same throwaway casualness as "stuff."
