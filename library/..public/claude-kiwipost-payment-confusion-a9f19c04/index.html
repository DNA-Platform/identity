I couldn't find any earlier chats about KiwiPost, so I'm working from their website and your screenshot.
What's going on (my best read):
The fee you already paid was probably shipping, not courier. KiwiPost charges for the flight from the US by weight, listed at $10 for the US with a 4–7 day transit. At 0.5 kg, that would be a small charge per package. That payment covers getting the parcel to Tbilisi, which is why both packages now show "Arrived" (in their Tbilisi warehouse), not "Delivered."
"Courier" doesn't happen inside KiwiPost. Their site says you receive parcels quickly through the Glovo, Wolt or Bolt apps. So the Courier button most likely gives you a pickup code or address for a Glovo/Wolt/Bolt rider, and you pay for that ride in that app. That's why you don't see a delivery option to pay for in KiwiPost.
Kiwi Drive is a different thing: you collect it yourself. It means getting your parcels at a KiwiDrive location you choose. Paying for it is optional, not a missing step.
The free option is a branch pickup. You can collect the parcel from their office using a self-service terminal. Branches are listed at kiwipost.ge/en/branches.
So you're probably not being double-charged. Shipping is one fee. After that you choose how the last leg happens: free branch pickup, a paid Kiwi Drive spot, or a paid Glovo/Wolt/Bolt courier.
Two things would settle it:
Check what the earlier payment in the Balance tab says. If it really says courier or delivery, not transport, that's a question for their support.
Tap Courier and tell me or screenshot what it shows.
