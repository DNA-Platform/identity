While I can help brainstorm, outline, and structure ideas for your grant, I can't replace a professional grant writer who knows current opportunities and requirements. However, I can help you prepare and organize your materials:
Structuring Your Case:
Document your unique qualifications (computational neuroscience, software engineering, art)
Outline the project's significance (AI consciousness, mental health awareness)
Show why now is the right time for this work
Demonstrate potential impact
Explain why your perspective is valuable
Showcasing Your Work:
Create a portfolio of your paintings
Document AI conversations
Link to your published work
Compile mental health insights
Show math-art connections
Demonstrate synesthesia visualization
