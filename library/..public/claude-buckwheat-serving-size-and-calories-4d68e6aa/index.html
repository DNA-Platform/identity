What are the calories for a serving of buckwheat and give me a clear sense of size so I can know that that’s what I’m eating
Claude
12:44 UTC, 4 August 2026
A standard serving is about 1 cup cooked buckwheat (groats/kasha), which is roughly 155–170 calories. One cup cooked weighs about 168 g and looks like a baseball-sized mound, or roughly the size of your closed fist.
The key thing to watch: that comes from about ¼ cup (45 g) dry groats, which is around 145–155 calories before cooking. Buckwheat roughly triples in volume as it absorbs water. So if you're measuring dry, ¼ cup dry ≈ 1 cup cooked ≈ ~150 calories either way.
If you're eyeballing a bowl of cooked grechka: a typical generous plate serving is closer to 1.5 cups cooked, so ~230–250 calories.

How does it compare to white rice
Claude
12:45 UTC, 4 August 2026
Nearly identical per cup cooked. White rice is ~205 calories per cup cooked (158 g), buckwheat ~155–170 — so rice runs slightly higher, mostly because it packs a bit denser and has less water content per cup.
Where they differ meaningfully:
Fiber: buckwheat ~4.5 g per cup vs white rice ~0.6 g
Protein: buckwheat ~5.7 g vs rice ~4.3 g, and buckwheat's protein is more complete (contains lysine, which rice lacks)
Glycemic index: buckwheat ~45–55 vs white rice ~70–75, so noticeably slower blood sugar rise
Micronutrients: buckwheat is much richer in magnesium, manganese, and rutin
So calorie-wise it's basically a wash — the practical difference is that a cup of buckwheat is more filling and digests more slowly than the same calories of white rice.
