Sierra Interactive and Sierra Entertainment (the video game company) are unrelated companies that happen to share the "Sierra" name.
Searched the web for “Sierra Interactive company”
Sierra Interactive is a real estate software company that develops residential real estate software and services for agents, teams, and brokers in the U.S. and Canada. It was founded in 2007 by Ben Peskoe and is headquartered in Louisville, Kentucky. Their platform includes IDX websites, a back-end CRM, and lead generation services. In 2022, Sierra Interactive was acquired by ASG, a portfolio company of Alpine Investors that buys and builds vertical SaaS companies.
No relationship to the video game company. Sierra Entertainment (originally Sierra On-Line), founded by Ken and Roberta Williams, made Quest for Glory, King's Quest, Space Quest, Leisure Suit Larry, and many other classic adventure games. It's a completely different company — just a name coincidence. Sierra Entertainment went through various corporate owners (CUC/Cendant, Havas, Vivendi) and the brand is now owned by Activision Blizzard (Microsoft).

As for the company's size and financials:
Employees: Approximately 57 employees across 3 continents (though the 2022 acquisition announcement cited 85+ team members — the number may have fluctuated).
Revenue: Annual revenue is estimated at around $15M. Other sources put it in the $10-50M range.
Profit: Since Sierra Interactive is privately held (now owned by ASG/Alpine Investors), they don't publicly disclose profit figures. However, their job postings notably described the company as "self-funded, financially independent, profitable, and high growth" prior to the acquisition.
