Based on my research, here's what Ana needs to know about notary services in Georgia (the country):
No Appointment Usually Required
You can schedule an appointment to process documents through the MYGOV.GE portal by selecting a specific time and date for your visit. However, this step was only mandatory during the quarantine period. Now, you can simply come without any appointment and still receive quick service.
Two Main Options for Notary Services
1. Public Service Hall / House of Justice There are locations in both Tbilisi and Batumi. These government buildings offer many services including notary services under one roof. At first glance, the building impresses with its architecture, but in addition to that, this national bureau is renowned for its wide range of legal services. It operates under a "one-stop-shop" principle.
2. Private Notary Offices The Notary Chamber of Georgia maintains a directory at notary.ge where you can find all registered notaries with their contact info (phone numbers, emails, addresses, and even Skype addresses for online services).
How to Check/Book
Online portal: mygov.ge – for scheduling appointments at Public Service Halls (optional but available)
Notary directory: notary.ge – lists all notaries in Batumi and Tbilisi with direct contact details
Direct contact: Most private notaries can be reached by phone or email to confirm availability
For Russian Citizens Specifically
For residents of CIS countries, a notarized Georgian translation of both the bride's and groom's passports, as well as those of two witnesses, is usually sufficient. This can be done quickly at private notary offices located near the House of Justice.
Online Notary Option
Georgia also offers online notary services via Skype for certain documents (power of attorney, consents, declarations). The following notary acts may be performed on-line, via internet: any type of consent, declaration, Power of Attorney. The said notary acts may be performed from any country of the world.
