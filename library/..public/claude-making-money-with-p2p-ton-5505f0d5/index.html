Based on my research, "P2P TON" can refer to a few different things, so let me break it down:
TON's Built-in P2P Infrastructure
Yes, TON (The Open Network) has robust P2P infrastructure baked into its design:
P2P Transactions — Wallet, a TON-based platform directly integrated into Telegram, enables instantaneous P2P transactions and borderless payments for merchants within Telegram.
P2P Marketplace — P2P trading is available right from the app. It is enough to select the desired tab, currency, cryptocurrency and payment method. The @wallet bot in Telegram functions as a P2P exchange where users can trade directly with each other.
Network Services — TON also offers TON Proxy (decentralized VPN access), TON Storage (encrypted file storage), and TON DNS.
P2P.org + TON (Staking Platform)
There's also P2P.org, a separate institutional staking provider that recently integrated with TON. Staking platform P2P.org is launching an integration with The Open Network (TON) blockchain, lowering staking caps for over 20 million active wallets on the network. The expansion into TON primarily targets retail users as P2P.org removed caps on the number of stakers and set the minimum staking requirement to just 1 Toncoin (TON).
Can You Make Money?
Several ways:
P2P Trading — Sellers on Telegram will pay a 0.9% commission on transactions, which the developers say are peer-to-peer and free for buyers. You can buy low, sell higher on the P2P market.
Staking — The TON Network generates staking rewards in two ways: Inflation (generation of new TON) and transaction fees (collected for processing transactions). By staking your TON, you actively contribute to the network's security while earning a share of the rewards generated.
Arbitrage — Price discrepancies exist between P2P markets and exchanges.

Yes, exactly — that's precisely what TON's P2P marketplace is designed for.
How It Works
You can post buy or sell offers on the Telegram Wallet P2P market. So if you have USD and want TON, you post a buy offer. If someone wants your USD and has TON, they take your offer. Vice versa for selling.
Go to P2P, choose Buy TON, pick your currency and payment method (card, bank transfer). After you pay, coins land on your balance instantly.
For selling: In the same section create a "Sell TON" ad and receive fiat to your card.
The Economics
Buyers pay 0% fee
Sellers pay 0.9% commission
The exchange services act as guarantors for each transaction. In any case of disagreement between the seller and the buyer, the service will conduct a dispute resolution process.
Making Money as a P2P Trader
The way people profit is by setting their own rates — you buy TON slightly below market when posting buy offers, sell slightly above market when posting sell offers, and pocket the spread. Classic market-making.
At the moment, you can only buy or sell the following tokens for fiat currency on the P2P Market: TON, USDT, BTC, NOT, ETH.
Limits
The wallet from Telegram has three levels of verification. The default level is basic, with a limit of 15,000 EUR per day. At the "Plus" level, you can increase the limit to 100,000 EUR per day, and "Maxi" removes limits on incoming transactions and P2P trading.
So yes — you'd be a P2P merchant essentially. The risk is counterparty fraud (though escrow helps), price volatility while trades settle, and needing sufficient volume to make the 0.9% fee plus your time worthwhile.

The catch with B: You need inventory on both sides. To sell TON, you need TON. To buy TON, you need USD. And the 0.9% seller fee eats into your margin.
So which are you thinking?
If you want to hold TON long-term, P2P buying is just a patient way to get a better entry — but you're not really "making money," you're reducing cost basis.
