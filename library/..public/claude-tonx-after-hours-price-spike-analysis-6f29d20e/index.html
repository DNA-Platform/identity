That's the right read on the microstructure, and it's one of the more interesting features of the asset. The slow information-to-price transmission isn't inefficiency in the textbook sense — it's a structural artifact of three things stacked:
The holder base is bifurcated between strategic capital (Stotz/Kingsway, Pantera, operational principals, Foundation-adjacent holders) and a thin layer of independent retail. Strategic capital doesn't trade off Durov posts — they're on a years-long thesis. Independent retail mostly isn't watching TON-native channels live. So the marginal price-setter is a tiny group, and even when news hits, there's no efficient transmission from "Pavel posts on his channel" to "TONX bid moves."
There's no arbitrage desk closing the gap. BTC ETFs have dedicated market-makers running creations/redemptions intraday; gaps close in minutes. TONX is a closed-end-style perpetual concession with no creation/redemption mechanism to enforce alignment, and no comparable arb infrastructure has emerged because the float is too small to be worth a desk's attention. The vehicle is structurally insulated from the kind of mechanical arb that would tighten correlation.
The information path itself is multi-hop and lossy. Durov posts → crypto-native press picks it up → financial press summarizes → TONX-specific coverage (which barely exists) → retail TONX traders. Each hop adds latency. Today's MTONGA news moved TON within minutes; TONX is still working through the implications and the +10% after-hours print is probably the first leg, not the full repricing.
Your "not sure how many own this who aren't somehow associated with its creation" intuition is likely close to literally true. At $118M market cap with heavy insider/strategic concentration, the independent-holder pool may be small enough that you'd recognize a meaningful fraction of the non-affiliated names if they were public. That's also why the borrow looks the way it does — there's no organized institutional view in either direction. It's mostly held, not traded.
The trading implication you've already internalized: this is exactly your "pre-propagation signal value" setup, and the edge exists because most TONX holders aren't running your information stack. As long as the asymmetry persists, watching TON-native sources directly and acting on them gives you minutes-to-hours of headroom before the TONX market catches up. The window only closes when more sophisticated independent capital shows up — which is what "discovery" actually means for an asset like this. Until then, the latency is the gift.
