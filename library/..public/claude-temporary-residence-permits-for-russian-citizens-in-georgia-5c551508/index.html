Part 1: Temporary Residence Permits for Russian Citizens in Georgia
Current Situation (2025)
Russian citizens face increasing rejection rates - in 2023, 41% of applications were rejected compared to 31% in 2022. Georgia now requires criminal record certificates from Russian applicants, who can be denied if they are being prosecuted or have been convicted of a criminal offense in the past 5 years. Georgia's 2025 immigration reforms have introduced stricter residence permit requirements with enhanced monitoring procedures.
Types of Temporary Residence Permits
1. Work Residence Permit
Requirements: Company annual turnover must exceed GEL 50,000 per foreign employee, and the applicant's monthly income must be at least five times the Georgian minimum wage
Problem for Russians: Only 22% of work permit applications were approved in 2023, down from 59% in 2022, with 62% rejected
2. Real Estate Residence Permit
For property owners with real estate (excluding agricultural land) valued over USD 100,000, available to the owner and eligible family members
More stable approval rates
3. Investment Residence Permit
Requires USD 300,000 investment for indefinite permit, or USD 100,000 for 5-year temporary permit
Best option for Russians: Roughly 70% approval rate for Russian citizens - the most stable category
4. Student Residence Permit
Requires enrollment confirmation from educational institution and bank statement showing sufficient funds (twice the subsistence minimum, currently 230.5 GEL monthly as of March 2025)
5. Family Reunification
For foreign nationals reuniting with family members who are Georgian citizens or have valid Georgian residency permits
2,123 spouses of Georgian citizens applied for permanent residence in one year, with 17% of applications denied
Strategies for Success
Investment route is most reliable - highest approval rate for Russians at ~70%
Avoid work permits - highest rejection rate at 78% in 2023
Criminal record certificate required - must be clean for past 5 years
Family reunification through marriage to Georgian citizen offers better path
Real estate option provides middle-ground stability
