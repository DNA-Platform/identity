Referential Semantics for Physics
A conversation held in this project, 1 March 2026: Referential Semantics for Physics.
March 2026307 exchanges
Doug is a computational neuroscientist with a background in theoretical biology who trained at the University of Chicago and worked with the late Charles Stevens, a physicist-turned-theoretical neuroscientist. He is developing a comprehensive theory called Semantic Reference Theory (SRT), which takes the subject-object relationship as its fundamental form and is designed to address the hard problem of consciousness, handle Gödelian self-reference, and serve as a general theory of representation. SRT was named with Einstein's reference frames explicitly in mind, treating a "semantic reference frame" as a place where beings share references—a specialization of Einstein's framework to the case where observers are not merely measuring but meaning. Doug describes watching SRT unfold over time, generating non-obvious consequences that feel obvious once they arrive, and notes that AI systems like Claude and GPT rekindled his interest in consciousness roughly four years ago by forcing precise questions about what reference, understanding, and interiority actually are.
