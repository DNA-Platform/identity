Yes, fluvoxamine is an SSRI (selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor). It's sold under brand names like Luvox in the US and Fevarin (Феварин) in Russia.
Primary Uses
It's most commonly prescribed for:
Obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD) - this is its primary indication
Social anxiety disorder

50mg is not a therapeutic dose - it's a starting dose.
