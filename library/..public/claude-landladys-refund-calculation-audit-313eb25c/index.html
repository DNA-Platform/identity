Here's what she's saying and where the math lands:
Her top-line accounting (this part checks out):
You paid $2,670 total upfront; $860 of that was the deposit
She's converting the $860 deposit at 44 TL/USD = 37,840 TL
Plus your last month's rent of 43,500 TL
So she's holding 81,340 TL of yours ✓
She's deducting 47,635.95 TL in costs
Refunding you 33,704.05 TL ✓ (81,340 − 47,635.95 = 33,704.05)
But the itemized deductions don't add up to her claimed 47,635.95 TL.
Adding her line items:
Electric 111.95 + Electric 2,418 + Electric 600 = 3,129.95
Pillow/misc 278
Cleaning 5,000 + cleaning materials 850 = 5,850
Painting (downstairs) 20,000
Sofa washing 2,750 + sofa repair 3,500 = 6,250
Internet 2 months 2,750 + internet cancellation 7,300 = 10,050
Water 746
Total: 46,303.95 TL
She's claiming 47,635.95 TL. There's a 1,332 TL gap she hasn't itemized. Worth asking her what that is.
Other things worth flagging before you accept:
The 111.95 electric line — she wrote "I did send you received," which sounds like that bill was already paid separately. If so, it shouldn't be deducted again. That would widen the unexplained gap to ~1,444 TL.
20,000 TL for "painting from downstairs" is the biggest single charge and the phrasing is odd — painting somewhere that isn't your unit? Worth clarifying what was painted and why you're paying for it.
7,300 TL "internet to close" — internet cancellation fees that high are unusual; you may want to see the bill from the provider.
The 44 TL/USD rate on your deposit — you might check what the rate was when you actually paid the deposit vs. now. If TL has weakened since, converting at the old rate is fine; if it's strengthened, she's giving you fewer TL than your dollars are worth today. (Deposits are typically returned at the original rate or in original currency, but worth knowing where you stand.)
Bottom line: ask her to account for the missing ~1,332 TL, clarify the 111.95 electric, and get receipts/explanations for the painting and internet cancellation charges before agreeing.
