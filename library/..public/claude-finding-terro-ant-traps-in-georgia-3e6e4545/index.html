The short answer: true borax-based slow-kill bait stations like Terro are hard to find in Georgia, but there are solid equivalents — the closest being Raid Ant Gel and possibly Combat bait stations.
Here's the practical breakdown:
What's confirmed available in Tbilisi: Domino (the Georgian hypermarket chain) carries Raid aerosol (ant + roach spray). That's a contact killer though — not the same mechanism as Terro. Raid also makes an Ant Gel (thiamethoxam-based, bait-style) which is the closest functional equivalent to Terro: ants take it back to the colony and it kills the queen. This is widely sold across Europe/CIS and Domino tends to carry Raid's broader line.
For Wolt/Glovo specifically: Domino stores are on Glovo in Tbilisi, so if they stock Raid Gel (not just aerosol), that's your best bet. Search in the app for "Raid" or "инсектицид" / "მწერები" (insects in Georgian). Carrefour and Goodwill are also on these apps and may stock Combat bait stations.
If you can't find gel-style bait: The Russian-market equivalent that circulates widely in Georgia is "Великий Воин" (Great Warrior) ant gel — diazinon + chlorpyrifos based, same colony-kill mechanism. It shows up on MyMarket.ge and occasionally through Wildberries.ge.
DIY fallback (always available): borax + sugar + water in a bottle cap works identically to Terro. Borax (бура / борная кислота) is sold at pharmacies across Tbilisi — ask for борная кислота (boric acid). Mix with powdered sugar 1:3. It's what Terro essentially is.
Bottom line: check Glovo for Domino → search Raid; if you find the gel tube rather than the aerosol can, that's your Terro equivalent. Otherwise the pharmacy borax route is genuinely effective and takes 15 minutes to set up.

Neither of those works for ants — the Raid is mosquito repellent strips, and Dichlophos is a contact-kill spray (kills what it hits, doesn't reach the colony).
Try searching Glovo/Wolt for these terms instead:
"муравей" (ant)
"гель" (gel)
"Combat"
"ловушка" (trap)
Also try Carrefour or Goodwill if they're on your Glovo — they tend to have broader household chemical sections than whatever store you're currently in.
Honest assessment though: the bait-station / slow-kill gel category just seems to be thinly stocked in Tbilisi delivery apps. The fastest reliable solution at this point is the DIY borax route:
Buy борная кислота (boric acid) at any pharmacy — it's cheap, ~1-2 GEL, and every Georgian pharmacy carries it
Mix with powdered sugar (сахарная пудра) 1:3 ratio, add a drop of water to make a paste
Drop small amounts on bottle caps or scraps of cardboard near ant trails
This is literally what Terro is. Works the same way — ants carry it back, queen dies, colony collapses over 1-2 weeks. With kids in the house just keep the caps in spots they can't reach.
