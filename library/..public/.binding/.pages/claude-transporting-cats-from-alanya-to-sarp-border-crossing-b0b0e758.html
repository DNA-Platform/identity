The Driver's License Problem
You've been on ikamet for 6 months, which means you're legally required to have a Turkish driver's license to drive. The law is clear:
Foreign nationals can drive on their license for 6 months maximum from entry
After 6 months as a resident, you must have a Turkish license
US licenses cannot be converted - the US isn't part of the UN Vienna Convention
You'd need to get a Turkish license from scratch: driving school (~3 weeks theory), tests, practical lessons, driving exam
Why this matters critically:
Your car insurance is invalid if you drive without a proper license
If you have any accident, you're personally liable for all damages
If someone dies in an accident, you could be liable for lifetime compensation to the family
Fines are the least of your worries
