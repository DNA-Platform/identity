Good news: Georgia isn't in the EU, so the EU's 2020 ban on crushball/menthol cigarettes doesn't apply there. Crushball cigarettes (including Camel Crush) are legal to sell.
That said, I couldn't confirm that Camel Crush specifically is distributed in Georgia. I did find that regular Camel variants (Camel Original Blue, Camel Caster) are available through delivery services like 2nabiji.ge, so Camel products have distribution there.
Your best bets to find out:
Check larger tobacco shops in Tbilisi (there are specialty smoke shops on Chavchavadze Ave and in central areas)
Ask at supermarkets like Carrefour or Goodwill that carry international brands
Delivery apps like 2nabiji.ge or Glovo - you can search their tobacco inventory
Since JTI (which distributes Camel in the Caucasus region) has a regional hub in Tbilisi, there's a decent chance they carry it or can tell you where to find it. Worst case, if you can't find Camel Crush specifically, you might find other crushball brands - the market isn't restricted like in the EU/UK.
