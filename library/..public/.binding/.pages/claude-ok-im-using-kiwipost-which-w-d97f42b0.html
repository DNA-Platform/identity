It won't be — that's the catch. "Kiwi Drive" isn't home delivery. Per KiwiPost's own site, Drive means you pick up packages from preferred drive locations — it's a drive-through pickup point. The note on your screen ("we will deliver all parcels addressed to you to the drive") means the parcel gets routed to that drive location, where you collect it from your car. And the one you've selected is Didi Dighomi — across the river, well away from Saburtalo.
For actual door delivery, KiwiPost's home option works differently: you receive packages using the Glovo, Wolt or Bolt apps — a courier service you activate separately, not the Drive option.
So your three real options: contact support (support@kiwipost.ge / 032 205 23 24) to reverse the 5 GEL Drive fee and reroute; switch to courier delivery through the app's Delivery Service option; or just leave it at your default Vazha-Pshavela VII Quarter branch, which is your closest pickup anyway. Not verified whether the Drive fee is refundable once paid — that's a support question.
