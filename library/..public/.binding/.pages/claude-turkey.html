Generalized anxiety disorder diagnosis across countries
A conversation held in this project, 5 March 2026: Generalized anxiety disorder diagnosis across countries.
The person asked about international psychiatric classification systems, specifically how a particular personal circumstance would be categorized in Turkey and Russia compared to the American diagnostic framework. Claude explained that both Turkey and Russia use the ICD-10 system rather than the DSM-5, and that the equivalent ICD-10 classification is F41.1, which shares the same name but differs somewhat in diagnostic criteria — notably being less prescriptive about symptom counts and emphasizing autonomic symptoms. Claude also provided practical context about how the F41.1 code functions as a universal anchor for medical documents, insurance, and administrative purposes in both countries.
