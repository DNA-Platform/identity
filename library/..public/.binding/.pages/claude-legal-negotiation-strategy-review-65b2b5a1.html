The other change is structural: I split the hypothesis into two short sentences. The first names what he actually wants ("контакт именно со мной"). The second names how he plans to get it ("общение с детьми он рассматривает как путь к этой цели"). Then "Поэтому" carries the inference forward into the negotiation point. That break is what makes the logic readable in Russian — one idea per sentence, the connection between them stated explicitly.
