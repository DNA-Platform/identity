Claude
19:13 UTC, 18 April 2026
At the top is Mtatsminda Park — the old Soviet-era amusement park perched on the mountain. A few fun things to do up there:
Ride the big Ferris wheel on the edge of the cliff — best panoramic view of Tbilisi you can get, especially at sunset
Restaurant Funicular at the top station — historic dining room, great khinkali, sweeping city views from the terrace
Roller coasters and kids' rides if you're with Ana's kids — bumper cars, a small coaster, carousel, etc.
Walk the ridge trail — quiet paths behind the park with wildflowers and more viewpoints
Grab ice cream and just sit on the terrace — honestly one of the best low-effort evenings in the city
If you get off at the middle station instead of riding all the way up, you can visit the Pantheon of Mtatsminda next to Mama Daviti Church — Georgia's famous writers and public figures are buried there, and the view from the churchyard is great.
Evenings are the move — go up 1–2 hours before sunset, wander the park, eat, then watch the city light up.

What do I put into Google maps to get us to the funnicula
Claude
19:14 UTC, 18 April 2026
Type "Funicular Tbilisi" or "ფუნიკულიორი" into Google Maps — it'll take you to the lower station on Chonkadze Street (Daniel Chonkadze St. 4), which is where you buy tickets and board.
Just note: you need a Metromoney card (the same card used for buses/metro) to pay for the funicular — they don't take cash at the turnstile. If you don't have one, you can buy it at the station.
