Here are the main Spoonflower competitors where designers can sell their designs on wallpaper:
Contrado (UK, contrado.co.uk) — Offers a minimum commission of 20%, up to 25% plus markup, paid within 10 days of the sale. They do both fabric and wallpaper (peel-and-stick and traditional non-pasted), handmade in their London HQ. This is probably the one you were thinking of given the better commission structure.
Patternbank (UK, patternbank.com) — Designers earn a 50% commission on sales with no PayPal fees. However, this is more of a B2B design licensing marketplace (selling pattern files to businesses) rather than print-on-demand wallpaper to consumers. They also have a curated application process requiring a portfolio and 12 sample designs.
Print Me Pretty (UK, printmepretty.co.uk) — Similar concept to Spoonflower where you upload patterns and sell on fabric and wallpaper.
MyFabricDesigns (US) — A boutique alternative focusing on small-batch custom printing for artists and independent designers, allowing you to upload artwork for printing on fabrics and wallpaper.
Hygge & West (US) — Collaborates with artists on wallpaper designs, though it's more of a curated collaboration model than open uploads.
For Ana's illustration work, Contrado seems like the strongest option given the higher commission (20-25% vs. Spoonflower's 10%) and the fact that it handles wallpaper well. Does that sound like the one?
