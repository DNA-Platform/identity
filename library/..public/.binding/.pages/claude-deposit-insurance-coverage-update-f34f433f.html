The title reads "Deposit Insurance" and the message is already bilingual — the bottom half is English:
Pursuant to the Law of Georgia on the Deposit Insurance System, effective from April 1, 2026, the amount held by each depositor in any deposit and/or account shall be insured up to GEL 50,000 per depositor, regardless of the number of deposits and/or accounts, in each commercial bank and microbank. All funds held in all accounts of each depositor in a commercial bank or microbank are automatically insured by the Deposit Insurance Agency without any additional charge. For further information, please visit the official website of the Deposit Insurance Agency at: www.diagency.ge
This appears to be an official notification from Credo Bank (or similar) sent March 31 — one day before the new insurance coverage took effect.
