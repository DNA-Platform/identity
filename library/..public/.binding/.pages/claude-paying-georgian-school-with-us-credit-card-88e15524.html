Citibank can definitely send an international wire to that IBAN - the question is whether they'll let you denominate it in GEL or only USD.
Check this yourself: Log into Citibank online → Transfers → Wire Transfer → set up a new international wire. When you get to the currency selection screen, see if GEL (Georgian Lari) appears as an option.

It probably won't be rejected - Credo Bank will likely convert the incoming USD to GEL and credit the account. This is standard for international wires to countries with minor currencies.
But "probably" isn't great when you're wiring money. The risk is:
The account might be GEL-only and reject foreign currency
Credo Bank's conversion rate could be unfavorable
The school ends up with slightly less than 1,400/1,556 GEL
Quickest way to know for sure: Ask the school. They deal with international parents - they've probably handled this before. Something like:
"Can your Credo Bank account receive USD wire transfers? If I send approximately $520 and $580 USD, will that work, or do you need exact GEL amounts?"
They may even have a USD account they can give you instead, or tell you exactly what to do.
