I have a TV that has these two controllers and I want to know if there’s a way of either showing a movie on a flash drive or figuring out a way to plug my computer into the TV. Is that possible and what would I need to do it?
Claude
11:21 UTC, 12 July 2026
Yes, both are doable. You've got a Philips TV plus a Magti IPTV set-top box (the second remote controls the box, not the TV — you can ignore it for this).
Option 1: Flash drive (easiest)
Most Philips TVs of this generation have a USB port on the back or side and a built-in media player.
Put the movie file on a FAT32- or NTFS-formatted flash drive (MP4/H.264 is the safest format; MKV usually works too)
Plug it into the TV's USB port
On the Philips remote, press SOURCES and select USB, or it may pop up automatically
If the TV complains about the file, the container/codec is the issue — re-encoding to MP4 with HandBrake fixes it almost every time.
Option 2: Laptop via HDMI
Check the back of the TV for a free HDMI input (the Magti box is occupying one)
You need an HDMI cable plus, depending on the laptop, possibly an adapter — your HP laptop likely has a full-size HDMI port, so just a cable; if it's a newer machine with only USB-C, you'd need a USB-C-to-HDMI adapter
Press SOURCES on the Philips remote and pick the HDMI input you plugged into
HDMI cables and adapters are easy to get in Tbilisi — Alta, Zoommer, or even Glovo from an electronics shop.
Worth checking first: look at the TV's back panel and tell me what ports you see (USB, number of HDMI). That determines whether you need anything at all beyond a cable or a flash drive you probably already have.

Claude

Right — that's expected, and worth knowing why: the USB port on a TV is an input for files, not a video input. The TV reads the drive like a computer reads a flash drive. A laptop plugged in by USB has nothing to offer it — TVs can't receive a video signal over that kind of USB connection.
So the two paths stay as they were:
Flash drive → USB port: works, since the TV reads files directly
Laptop → HDMI port: the only way to mirror your laptop screen; USB won't substitute
If the flash drive route is the one you want, the main thing that can still trip you up is file format. If the TV shows the drive but says "unsupported file" or shows the file grayed out, tell me the file extension and I'll give you the quickest conversion path.
If you want the laptop route, check whether there's a free HDMI socket next to where the Magti box is plugged in — most Philips TVs have 2-3.
