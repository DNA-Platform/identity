Claude

Claritin (loratadine) is generally considered quite safe for children. It's approved for kids as young as 2 years old, is non-drowsy, and has a well-established safety profile over decades of use. Pediatric dosing is weight/age-based — typically 5mg for ages 2–5 and 10mg for 6 and up.
Childhood allergies are absolutely real and managed essentially the same way as adult allergies: antihistamines (Claritin, Zyrtec, Allegra), nasal corticosteroids (Flonase), and in more persistent cases, allergy testing and immunotherapy.
The main practical cautions are just standard pediatric ones: confirm the child's age/weight falls within the dosing range, avoid combination products (some "children's" formulations add decongestants like pseudoephedrine that have a narrower safety window in kids), and check with a pediatrician if the child is under 2 or has kidney/liver issues.
Nothing alarming about it as a drug class for kids.
