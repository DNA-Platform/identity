A conversation held in this project, 1 August 2026: Post-surgery weight loss plan for Georgia.
August 202610 exchanges
Doug is a resident of the Saburtalo neighborhood in Tbilisi, Georgia, seeking a highly regimented weight loss plan. He is 5'7" and 180 lbs, targeting aggressive fat loss of approximately 0.8–1.0 kg per week through a 1,500 kcal/day intake. A key constraint shaping his dietary structure is that he takes a wellness-related prescription that increases evening hunger, which led to building his entire eating plan around a late-day window rather than traditional meal timing. He also prefers fasting through most of the day, eating only in the final six hours before bed, with the bulk of calories (~1,000 kcal) at the start of that window and a slower-digesting ~500 kcal pre-bed meal to manage nighttime hunger. Claude confirmed this aligns with established intermittent fasting approaches such as the Warrior Diet and OMAD frameworks.
