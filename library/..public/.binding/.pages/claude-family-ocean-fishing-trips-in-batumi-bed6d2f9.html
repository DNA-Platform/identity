A few notes on booking this week:
Format: these are private charters, not scheduled departures - you call/WhatsApp, tell them party size and date, they organize. Booking a day ahead is the norm; morning slots are best for actual fishing (and August heat).
Sea Life Batumi (yacht-club-batumi.com, +995 557 90 90 88) explicitly markets family sea-fishing trips, gear included, and offers taking your catch to the fish market afterward to have it cooked - probably the best fit with Katya and Dima.
Mirage is smaller but the reviews specifically praise Captain Ruslan's fishing trips.
Weather caveat: August storms cancel trips on short notice, so hold a backup day.
I couldn't verify current prices - the Russian-language sources gate them behind WhatsApp contact. Rough marina benchmark from reviews: short pleasure rides run ~20 GEL/person on shared boats, but a private fishing charter will be substantially more; get the total price confirmed before boarding.
If you want, I can also draft a short Russian message Ana can send via WhatsApp to get quotes from two or three of them at once.
