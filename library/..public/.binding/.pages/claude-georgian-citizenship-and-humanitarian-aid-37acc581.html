Getting Georgian citizenship for Russian nationals involves several pathways, each with different requirements.
Main Routes to Georgian Citizenship
Naturalization (Standard Path)
This requires legally residing in Georgia for 5 years continuously, plus demonstrating knowledge of Georgian language, history, and basic law. The language requirement is the most significant barrier for most applicants—it requires passing an exam.
Additional requirements include having a legal source of income and either owning real estate in Georgia or being employed in Georgia.
Naturalization by Marriage
If Anna were to marry a Georgian citizen, the residency requirement drops to 2 years. The other requirements (language, history/law knowledge, income) still apply.
Exceptional Naturalization
Georgia's President can grant citizenship to individuals who have made special contributions to Georgia or who would serve the country's interests. This bypasses the standard requirements but is discretionary and unpredictable.
Investment-Based Residency (Not Direct Citizenship)
Investing $100,000+ in Georgian real estate or $300,000+ in a Georgian business can provide a path to permanent residency, which then feeds into the naturalization timeline.
For the Children
Children under 18 generally follow their parents' citizenship status. If Anna naturalizes, the children can be included in her application.
Practical Considerations
The Georgian language requirement is genuinely demanding—it's not a formality. The exam tests real conversational and reading ability. For Anna, being bilingual already is helpful for language learning generally, but Georgian is linguistically unrelated to both Russian and English.
