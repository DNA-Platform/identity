Short version, Doug: Claude doesn't actually support a traditional password at all right now, so there's no Google-to-password switch to make.
The two login options are "Continue with Google" using your Google credentials, or "Continue with email," which sends a one-time login link to your email address — no password is ever created. As Anthropic's help center puts it directly, Claude uses email login links instead of passwords, so there isn't a way to create a password at the moment.
So what you can do depends on what you're actually after:
If you just want to stop logging in through Google but keep the same account, you can unlink Google and switch to the email magic-link method instead. The catch is that if you've previously authenticated using Google, you can unlink your Google account from Claude — the support article had steps for that, though the snippet cut off before listing them. The key thing is the email you use for the link needs to be the same address, so the account (and all its history, projects, memory) stays intact.
If your real goal is a password specifically — for a password manager, or because magic-links feel less secure — that option doesn't exist yet on Claude. The closest you'd get is the email link method, which has no password to store.
One thing worth flagging given this account: if you unlink Google and move to email-link login, make sure the email you'll authenticate with is one you reliably control and won't lose, since the magic link is your only access path at that point. Want me to pull up the exact unlink steps?
