Based on the search results, here's the comparison:
MyMarket.ge is fundamentally different - it's a C2C classifieds marketplace (like Craigslist, eBay listings, or Russian Avito), not a centralized retailer like Amazon/Ozon.
Business Model:
MyMarket: Individual sellers list items, you deal directly with them. The platform facilitates the connection but doesn't hold inventory
Amazon/Ozon: Primarily B2C with centralized warehousing and fulfillment
Used vs New:
MyMarket: Both - listings are marked "ახალი" (new) or "მეორადი" (used). Heavy on secondhand goods
Amazon: Primarily new (plus Amazon Renewed)
Ozon: Primarily new
Shipping/Delivery:
MyMarket: No Prime equivalent. Delivery varies by seller. Typical times I found in listings:
Tbilisi: 1 day (via services like Delivo, Trackings.ge)
Regions: 2 working days (~10 GEL)
Many sellers offer in-person pickup
Third-party couriers like Yandex Delivery offer same-day/express in Tbilisi (~1 hour for extra fee)
The catch: Since it's C2C, there's no guaranteed delivery standard, no unified returns policy, and (as reviewers note) the sorting/categorization is messy. One reviewer explicitly compared it unfavorably to Avito, saying Avito does things 5x better.
For reliable new-item shopping in Georgia, Extra.ge and Vendoo.ge are more Amazon-like B2C marketplaces, though still without Prime-tier logistics. Many Georgians apparently still order from AliExpress, Amazon (via forwarding), and eBay despite the long delivery times.
