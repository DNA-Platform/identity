Searched the web for “best luxury hotels Tbilisi 2026 prices per night”
Here's the top tier of Tbilisi hotels and roughly what they run per night:
The true high end
The Tbilisi Edition (Marriott's design-luxury brand) - typically $350-500+
Paragraph Freedom Square (Marriott Luxury Collection, on Freedom Square) - minimum around $300/night, with a large spa complex and panoramic city views
Stamba Hotel (converted Soviet publishing house, the design icon of the city) - usually $250-400
Rooms Hotel Tbilisi (Stamba's sister property in Vera) - $200-300
Classic 5-star
The Biltmore (the glass tower on Rustaveli) - around $155-183/night with taxes
Tbilisi Marriott (the 1915 landmark on Rustaveli) - roughly $200-280
Radisson Blu Iveria - $180-250
Pullman Axis Towers (in your neighborhood, more or less - Vake/Saburtalo border) - from about $119-141/night
Wyndham Grand - around $141-167/night
Sheraton Grand Metechi Palace - $150-200
Wellness outlier
Bioli Medical Wellness Resort (up on the Kojori plateau above the city) - around $223-265/night, often booked as multi-day medical/detox programs.
Broad context: luxury five-stars start around $300/night and top suites can exceed $1,000, while mid-range runs $120-250. The 12-month average for a 5-star in Tbilisi is about $267/night on weekdays - Tbilisi luxury is still cheap by Western capital standards.
If this is for visiting guests, Stamba and the Edition are the two that consistently wow people; Pullman is the best value if you want them close to Saburtalo.

Right - those are all US dollars, which is how the booking sites list them. In lari at the current rate (~2.7 GEL/USD), roughly:
Paragraph / Edition / Stamba: ~810-1,350+ GEL/night
Biltmore: ~420-500 GEL
Pullman: ~320-380 GEL
Wyndham Grand: ~380-450 GEL
Bioli: ~600-720 GEL
The city-wide 5-star average of ~$267 ≈ 720 GEL/night
Worth knowing: hotels in Georgia are legally required to quote and charge in GEL at checkout, so the card charge will be in lari even when the booking site shows dollars. If you want, I can check the exact GEL rate today rather than my approximation.
