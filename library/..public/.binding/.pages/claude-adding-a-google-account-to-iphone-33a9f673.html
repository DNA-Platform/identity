How do I add a Google account to my iPhone accounts so that it uses my phone
Claude

Here's how to add a Google account to your iPhone:
Open Settings
Scroll down and tap Mail (or Contacts / Calendar depending on what you want to sync)
Tap Accounts
Tap Add Account
Select Google
Sign in with your Google email and password
Choose which services to sync — Mail, Contacts, Calendars, Notes — and toggle on what you want
Tap Save
Once added, your Gmail will appear in the Mail app, and your Google contacts/calendar will sync with the iPhone's native apps.
If you want Google to be your default account for things like contacts or calendar, go to Settings → Contacts → Default Account (or the equivalent under Calendar) and select your Google account there.
