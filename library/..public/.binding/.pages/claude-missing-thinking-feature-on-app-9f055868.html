Based on my search, here's what's happening with extended thinking:
The feature still exists — it hasn't been removed. To enable it, click the "Search and tools" button on the lower left of your chat interface, then switch the "Extended thinking" toggle on.
A few things that might explain why it "disappeared" for you:
It's under "Search and tools" now — The toggle lives in that menu (bottom left of the chat interface), not as a standalone button. If you haven't looked there recently, that might be why you're not seeing it.
Toggling it starts a new chat — If you've started chatting without extended thinking, toggling it on will start a new chat (and vice versa). So you can't turn it on mid-conversation.
Model availability — Extended thinking mode is available for Claude 4 models and Claude 3.7 Sonnet. If for some reason your account defaulted to a different model, you might not see it.
Possible UI update — The interface does get updated periodically, and the location/visibility of controls can shift.
To check:
Look for the "Search and tools" button (bottom left of the chat input area)
Click it and look for "Extended thinking" toggle
Make sure you're starting a fresh chat if you want to enable it
If you still don't see it there, it might be worth checking your account settings or reaching out to Anthropic support — there could be something specific to your account or subscription tier affecting availability.
