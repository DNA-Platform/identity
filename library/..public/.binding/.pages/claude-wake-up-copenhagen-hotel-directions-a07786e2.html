There are three Wakeup Copenhagen hotels, so check your booking for which one:
Wakeup Copenhagen Borgergade: Borgergade 9, 1300 København K, Denmark
Wakeup Copenhagen Bernstorffsgade: Bernstorffsgade 35, 1577 København V, Denmark
Wakeup Copenhagen Carsten Niebuhrs Gade: Carsten Niebuhrs Gade 11, 1577 København V, Denmark
All three share the phone number +45 4480 0000.
Getting there from the airport: Public transit is the best option. It takes about 15 minutes and costs much less than a car.
Borgergade: Take Metro M2 from the airport to Kongens Nytorv, then walk about 3 minutes. The hotel is only about 50 m from Kongens Nytorv. The metro runs 24/7.
Bernstorffsgade or Carsten Niebuhrs Gade: Take the regional train to Copenhagen Central Station (København H), then walk. Carsten Niebuhrs Gade is about 10 minutes on foot from the central station. Bernstorffsgade is closer.
You can buy tickets at the machines in the arrivals hall or in the DOT Tickets app.
Bolt and Uber: Both apps work in Copenhagen, but they are basically taxi apps there, not cheap rideshares. Uber left Denmark in 2017 after new taxi rules came in. It came back through a partnership with a taxi company. Uber in Denmark sends you licensed Dantaxi cars, not private drivers. Bolt entered Denmark in March 2025 by buying the Danish ride service Viggo. Bolt is often cheaper than Dantaxi for short trips in the city. Even so, prices are still close to normal taxi fares. A ride from the airport will cost several times the train fare. It's mainly worth it if you have lots of luggage or arrive late.

Yes, both apps work in Copenhagen. Your existing accounts and saved cards should work as they are.
Bolt: Bolt bought the Danish service Viggo in 2025 to launch ride-hailing in Denmark, and also partnered with Taxi 4×27, which added more than 600 cars to its platform. It's often cheaper than Dantaxi for short city rides.
Uber: Uber left Denmark in 2017 and later came back through a partnership with a local taxi company. In Denmark it books licensed taxis rather than private drivers.
Both give you an upfront fare, so check each one when you land and pick the cheaper option. You'll need mobile data to book, so turn on your Magti Scandinavia package before you leave. The airport is about 12 km from the hotel, so it's a short ride.

Yes, there's food in the hotel itself. The lobby café/bar is open 24 hours and sells drinks, snacks and sandwiches, so you can get something right now. The breakfast buffet runs 06:30–10:00 on weekdays and costs about €12–13. I couldn't confirm the weekend hours, so ask reception whether it's open this morning. Open early nearby (all by Central Station, roughly a 7–10 minute walk):
Lagkagehuset, Bernstorffsgade 16: a Danish bakery chain with pastries, bread and coffee. It opens at 5:00 every day, so it's open now.
7-Eleven, Istedgade 2: open 24 hours, just outside the station. It sells coffee, hot dogs, pizza slices and pastries.
7-Eleven inside the station, Banegårdspladsen: opens at 5:00 on Sundays.
Rug Bakery, Tietgensgade 39, inside the Villa Copenhagen hotel by the station: opens at 7:00. It's nicer and pricier, and reviewers like the cardamom buns.
For something quick right now, go to the lobby café. Lagkagehuset is the best bakery option open this early.

Probably not. Wakeup is a budget chain where breakfast is usually a paid extra, not part of the room rate. Room prices depend on economy vs. flexible booking, view and room size, and breakfast isn't one of those options. Ana's note about the booking didn't mention breakfast either.
To check for sure, ask reception tonight. They're open 24 hours and can look up booking 8663034833-1. If it isn't included, you can add it at the desk. Some booking sites say breakfast has to be ordered at check-in, so ask now rather than in the morning. One review site puts the price at about €12–13.
What they serve: it's a simple Scandinavian buffet. Based on the hotel's photos, it has oats, cornflakes and granola, bread rolls with butter and cheese, eggs, sliced vegetables, fresh fruit, coffee and orange juice. I couldn't find a full official menu.
Hours: tomorrow is Monday, so breakfast runs 06:30–10:00. On weekends it's 07:00–10:30.
If you'd rather skip it, the lobby café sells skyr, smoothies, sandwiches and croissants.
