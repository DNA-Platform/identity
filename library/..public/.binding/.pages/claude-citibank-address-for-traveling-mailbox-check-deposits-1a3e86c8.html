Setting Up Traveling Mailbox for Check Deposits
Traveling Mailbox charges $4.95 per check plus postage to mail the check to your bank. All checks are mailed via USPS Priority Mail or FedEx so a tracking number is available.
Steps to configure:
When a check arrives - Traveling Mailbox scans the envelope front and uploads it to your portal
Request open and scan - Have them open the envelope so you can verify it's a check
Submit a deposit request - When you see a check appear in your virtual mailbox portal, you simply select "deposit" on the envelope and send the request.
Provide your bank's address - You'll enter the Citibank Bank by Mail address above. Since Traveling Mailbox uses Priority Mail or FedEx with tracking, use the overnight/physical address (100 Citibank Drive) for better tracking.
They process it - Traveling Mailbox mails endorsed checks with a custom deposit slip to the bank within 24 hours, including tracking.
Include your account info - When setting up the deposit, you'll need to provide your Citibank checking account number so they can prepare the deposit slip correctly.
Pro tip: You can send several checks at one time or wait for other checks to arrive so you can consolidate and send them to the bank at one time. This will help you save on postage costs.
