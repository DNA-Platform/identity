Sunny King Projects
Sunny King remains pseudonymous to this day (he did a silhouetted fireside chat with Brandon Chez at a CoinMarketCap conference in 2019). King, who also created Primecoin, is a pseudonym. The Peercoin source code is distributed under the MIT/X11 software license.
His three main projects:
Peercoin (PPC) - August 2012, co-authored with Scott Nadal
Peercoin, also known as Peer-to-Peer Coin, PP Coin, or PPC, is a cryptocurrency utilizing both proof-of-stake and proof-of-work systems. It is notable as the first cryptocurrency to implement the proof-of-stake consensus mechanism.
Peercoin was the 4th in cryptocurrency market cap in 2014.
Primecoin (XPM) - July 2013 (the fun math one!)
Unlike other cryptocurrencies, which are mined using algorithms that solved mathematical problems with no extrinsic value, mining Primecoin involves producing chains of prime numbers (Cunningham and bi-twin chains). These are useful to scientists and mathematicians.
The mining searches for Cunningham chains of the first kind (each prime is 2p+1), second kind (2p-1), and bi-twin chains (pairs of twin primes)
47,334,011 prime numbers in 4,599,221 chains have been found since July 2013.
Vitalik Buterin called Primecoin "the first alternative coin that outweighs Bitcoin" back in 2013
V Systems (VSYS) - Mainnet launched Q4 2018
V SYSTEMS' mission is to create a future economy built upon millions of blockchains. V SYSTEMS creates a scalable and stable infrastructure with Supernode Proof of Stake (SPoS).
VGraph is the result of V Systems' exploration of next-generation solutions to improve the current V Systems network. The VGraph team has been focusing on implementing the updated version of the Supernode Proof of Stake consensus algorithm and testing Schnorr signature algorithm. VGraph was expected early-to-mid 2025.
Still appears active - their Medium published through December 2024

You're right. I was working backwards from "what functions need a real person" instead of seeing the obvious.
Programming communities are social. Social requires trust. Trust requires real humans.
That's it. That's the whole thing.
Bitcoin's design philosophy is trustlessness. The entire point is you don't need to trust anyone. Satoshi being a ghost is feature-consistent. The protocol verifies. No humans required.
Ethereum is "come build with us." That's a social invitation. You're asking developers to invest their time, careers, reputation into your platform. Humans don't mass-join social structures founded by ghosts. They need to look someone in the eye, decide if they trust them, then commit.
Vitalik isn't performing functions that require a real person. He IS the trust anchor for a social structure that couldn't exist without one.
Not legal. Not governance. Not education logistics. Just: humans need to trust humans to form communities, and developer ecosystems are communities.
