I'll help you set up your Xiaomi Mi WiFi Range Extender AC1200. The setup process is straightforward and doesn't require special considerations for Georgia (the country).
Here's how to set it up:
Basic Setup Steps:
Power on the extender - Plug it into a power outlet near your main router (for initial setup)
Connect to the extender's network
On your phone or computer, look for a new WiFi network named something like "Xiaomi_xxxx" or "Mi_xxxx" (the x's are random characters)
Connect to this network (no password needed initially)
Access the setup page
Open a web browser and go to: 192.168.31.1 or miwifi.com
You should see the Mi WiFi extender setup page
Configure the extender
Select your main WiFi network from the list
Enter your main WiFi password
The extender will connect to your router
You can choose to either:
Keep the same network name (recommended - seamless roaming)
Create a different network name (you'll see "_plus" or similar added)
Relocate the extender
Once setup is complete, unplug the extender
Place it roughly halfway between your router and the area with weak signal
The LED indicator should show good signal strength (usually blue or stable light)
Tip: If the setup page doesn't load, make sure you're connected to the extender's WiFi network and try clearing your browser cache or using a different browser.
Does this help? Let me know if you run into any specific issues during setup!

Good catch! You're absolutely right - my initial info was outdated. Based on current documentation, here's the correct setup for your AC1200:
Updated Setup Steps:
Power on the extender
Plug it into a power outlet near your router
Wait until the LED starts flashing red (this means it's ready for setup)
Connect to the extender's WiFi
On your phone, look for a WiFi network called "xiaomi repeater" (not Xiaomi_xxxx)
Connect to it (no password needed)
Setup Methods - Choose One: Method A: Using Mi Home App (Recommended)
Download "Mi Home" or "Xiaomi Home" app (Android/iOS)
Important: When setting region, select "China Mainland" - this is crucial even though you're in Georgia. Many users report setup fails with other regions
Log in or create a Mi account
Follow the in-app instructions to add the device
The app will detect your extender and guide you through connecting it to your main WiFi Method B: WPS Button (if your router has WPS)
Press the WPS button on your router
Within 2 minutes, press the WPS button on the extender
Wait for the signal indicator to turn solid blue
Final placement
Move the extender halfway between your router and the weak signal area
Look for solid blue light (good signal) rather than orange (weak signal)
Regarding Georgia: The AC1200 has the EU plug type and supports 100-240V AC (50/60Hz), so it will work fine with Georgia's electrical system (220V, 50Hz with Type C/F plugs). The setup process is the same regardless of country - the only regional consideration is selecting "China Mainland" in the app for best compatibility.
