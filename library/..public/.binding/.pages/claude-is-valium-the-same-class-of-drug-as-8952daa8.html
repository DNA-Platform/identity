Quick spelling check first: you almost certainly mean lorazepam (Ativan) and alprazolam (Xanax). Yes, all three — diazepam (Valium), lorazepam, alprazolam — are in the same class: benzodiazepines.
On "easier to obtain" within the benzo class in Georgia: no such thing exists.
Benzodiazepines are regulated as a class in Georgia under the Law on Narcotic Drugs, Psychotropic Substances and Precursors (List III — pharmaceutical products under special control). Since September 1, 2014, the sale of psychotropic medications, sleeping pills, and sedatives (except herbal-based ones) without a prescription has been prohibited in Georgia. Diazepam, lorazepam, alprazolam, clonazepam, bromazepam — all the same regulatory tier, all require a Form #3 prescription.
The trajectory is tightening, not loosening. PM Kobakhidze recently announced a ban on private import of psychotropic medications, specifically calling out benzodiazepine consumption as "unreasonable" and signaling tighter prescription/dispensing regulations. Pharmacy heads selling under the counter have been getting arrested — 1 to 3 years under Article 192 of the Criminal Code. Pharmacists are increasingly cautious; the back-channel that existed pre-2024 is closing.
The legitimate path is genuinely the easy path here. A Georgian GP or psychiatrist visit runs ~50–150 GEL, no insurance gymnastics, same-day appointments common. AMC, GT Clinic, or any neighborhood клиника can write a Form #3 if clinically warranted. That's faster and cheaper than chasing pharmacy workarounds — and removes legal exposure for you and your family during the active TRP process, where any law-enforcement contact would be a real problem.
For an actual alternative recommendation, what are you treating? The class of substitute differs sharply by indication:
Sleep onset/maintenance
Generalized anxiety
Acute panic
Muscle spasm / pre-procedure
Flight anxiety / one-off situational use
Some indications have OTC or non-controlled prescription options in Georgia that are genuinely effective. Others don't have a real non-benzo equivalent and the prescription is the answer. Tell me which and I can give you specifics with sources.
