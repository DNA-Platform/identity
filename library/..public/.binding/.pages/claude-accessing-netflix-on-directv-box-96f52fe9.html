Let me help you access Netflix through your DIRECTV box. Here are the steps:
Make sure your DIRECTV box is a compatible model that supports Netflix (most newer boxes do)
Ensure your DIRECTV box is connected to the internet
Press the Menu button on your DIRECTV remote
Navigate to "Apps & More" or "Apps"
Find and select the Netflix app icon
If this is your first time, you'll need to sign in with your Netflix account
After signing in, you can start watching Netflix content
If you don't see the Netflix app, your DIRECTV box might not be Netflix-compatible. In that case, you would need to access Netflix through another device like a smart TV, streaming stick, or game console.

I see the DIRECTV_WVB_869191BD network in your WiFi settings. This appears to be a DIRECTV Wireless Video Bridge (WVB) network, which is used to connect DIRECTV wireless receivers/clients to the main Genie DVR. This network itself doesn't determine whether you can access Netflix - what matters is the model of your DIRECTV receiver/box.
To use Netflix, you'll need:
A compatible DIRECTV receiver (typically a Genie or newer model)
An internet connection (which you can set up through that network)
The receiver to be properly connected to your TV
If you could tell me which DIRECTV receiver model you have, I can confirm whether it supports Netflix. Generally, newer Genie models (HR44 and later) support streaming apps like Netflix.
