I need to clarify something important: It's actually illegal for anyone except USPS postal workers to place items in mailboxes - this is federal law in the United States. Mailboxes are considered federal property and are reserved only for mail delivery.
As for leaving packages at apartment complexes:
Most apartment complexes won't accept packages for residents who aren't expecting them, due to liability concerns
Some complexes have package lockers or a front office that accepts packages, but typically only for deliveries from recognized carriers (USPS, UPS, FedEx, etc.)
The safest options would be to:
Arrange delivery when the person is home
Use an official shipping service
Leave it with them in person
Use Amazon lockers if available in your area
If you're trying to get something to someone in an apartment complex, what's the specific situation? There might be better alternatives I can suggest.

Ah, a wedding card - this is a common situation! Since you're already there, here are some quick solutions:
Call or text them if you have their number - they might be home or nearby
Tape it securely to their door (if complex rules allow this)
Ask a neighbor who knows them to pass it along
If there's a front office/leasing office that's open, explain it's a wedding card - they're often more willing to hold something small and personal like this compared to packages
If there's a gap above the door (some doors have this), you might be able to carefully slide it over the top
Just avoid leaving it on the ground or in an unsecured location where it could get lost or damaged. If none of these work, you might need to come back later or mail it through USPS.
