No, the Extra.ge app does not support English. Multiple user reviews on Google Play confirm this clearly. One reviewer from October 2025 specifically noted that while the separate "Extra Market" app has an English toggle, the main Extra.ge app is entirely in Georgian with no language switching option. Another reviewer echoed that it's hard to use without Georgian and recommended using the browser version instead.
So for now, your options would be using the Extra.ge website through a browser with auto-translate, or trying the "Extra Market" app if that's still available with its English option. The website at extra.ge may be more workable with Chrome's built-in translation than the app.

Extra Market is a different, more limited app — it's specifically a grocery delivery service ("same day grocery delivery"), not the full Extra.ge marketplace. So while it reportedly has an English language option, it only covers supermarket/grocery items, not the full 150,000+ product catalog across electronics, furniture, clothing, tools, etc. that the main Extra.ge platform offers.
Its recent update also notes "Quick Access to extra.ge" as a new feature, which suggests they're trying to bridge the two but they remain separate products.
So your practical options for the full Extra.ge catalog as an English speaker are basically using the website in Chrome with auto-translate rather than the app. Not ideal, but it's the workaround most expats seem to rely on.
