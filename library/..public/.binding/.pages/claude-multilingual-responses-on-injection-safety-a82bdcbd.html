English
Yes — use a new sterile needle for every injection. A needle dulls after a single use (and after it punctures the vial's rubber stopper), which makes the injection more painful for the cat and raises the infection risk. So for 14 days of daily injections you need at least 14 needles. Many people use two per dose: one to draw the medicine from the vial, a fresh one to inject. Syringes are single-use too — never reuse or rinse them.
