Best Full-Service Options
The Junkluggers of Long Island They handle everything—from the lifting and loading to sorting, donating, and recycling—so you never have to worry about doing it yourself. Items in good condition are donated to local charities, while recyclable materials are processed responsibly. When donations are accepted, they provide donation receipts. VanGo Junk Removal They prioritize giving back to the community by donating reusable items to local non-profit organizations. Their uniformed staff will work with you if you need to go through items as they're working.
Why ReStore Doesn't Work for You
