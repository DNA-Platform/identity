Short answer: no for Russia-resident users, yes for Russian passport holders living elsewhere.
Payoneer ceased providing services to residents of Russia on December 8, 2022, and closed all affected accounts on December 16, 2022. New accounts cannot be opened with a Russian address, and existing Russia-based accounts were shut down.
However, Russian passport holders can open accounts if they can prove residence outside Russia — typically via a recent utility bill or bank statement with their name and local address from the last 3 months. Relevant for Ana: a Georgian Credo Bank statement with her Tbilisi address should satisfy the proof-of-residence requirement, and her Russian passport is acceptable as ID.
One caveat worth flagging — Payoneer has been aggressive on sanctions compliance after their $1.25 million NYSDFS penalty in November 2023 for Russia sanctions violations, so they scrutinize Russian nationals carefully even with foreign residence. Expect thorough KYC review.
