What is the calorie count of buckwheat? Is it low calorie
Claude
06:44 UTC, 3 February 2026
Buckwheat contains approximately 340-360 calories per 100g of raw groats, which is similar to other whole grains like rice, oats, and quinoa.
When cooked, it comes out to roughly 90-100 calories per 100g (since it absorbs water and expands).
So it's not particularly low-calorie compared to other grains—it's pretty standard. However, buckwheat has some advantages that make it feel more satisfying relative to its calories: it's high in fiber and protein (about 13g protein per 100g dry), which can help with satiety. It also has a lower glycemic index than many grains, meaning it won't spike blood sugar as sharply.
If you're looking for lower-calorie grain alternatives, cauliflower rice (~25 cal/100g) or shirataki noodles (~10 cal/100g) are dramatically lower, though nutritionally quite different.
