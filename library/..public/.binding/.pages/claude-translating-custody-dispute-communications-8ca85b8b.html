Two educational facts:
Russian Legal History: The decriminalization of domestic violence in 2017 was championed by lawmaker Yelena Mizulina, who argued that "a man beating his wife is less offensive than a stranger doing it" because "when a husband beats his wife, it's out of love." This framing — that family violence is different from "real" violence — permeates Russian legal culture.
Comparative Law: Russia is one of only four Council of Europe member states that never signed the Istanbul Convention on Preventing and Combating Violence against Women and Domestic Violence. The other three are Azerbaijan, Turkey (which withdrew in 2021), and the United Kingdom (which has not ratified).
