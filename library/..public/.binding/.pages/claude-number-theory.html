Goldbach pairs and coprime symmetry
A conversation held in this project, 30 March 2026: Goldbach pairs and coprime symmetry.
March 20266 exchanges
The person is engaged in original mathematical research on prime numbers and Goldbach's conjecture. The conversation focused on developing a precise framework for understanding "Goldbach pairs" — pairs of primes equidistant from a center value N, which sum to 2N. The person established the foundational insight that these pairs inherit their symmetry from the coprime structure of N: since gcd(N−k, N) = gcd(k, N) = gcd(N+k, N), the coprimes of N are symmetric around N, and prime pairs naturally inhabit this symmetric skeleton.
