WHAT'S INSIDE THE CYSTS
Renal cysts are fluid-filled, not solid. They cause a progressive development of fluid-filled cysts in the kidney. The fluid is a serous transudate — essentially filtered plasma. Feline coronavirus: POSITIVE, Ct 22.07. This is a high viral load. Ct is inversely proportional to organism concentration — the lower the Ct, the more virus. 22 is a lot of fecal shedding. Feline coronavirus (FCoV) is extremely common — 80-90% of cats in multi-cat environments carry it — and in most cats it causes mild or self-limiting enteritis or nothing at all. But it is an enteric pathogen. It infects the enterocytes of the small intestine and colon. In a cat whose gut is already destabilized from months of dual protozoal infection and nitroimidazole damage, active FCoV replication could be a significant contributor to ongoing diarrhea that nobody was looking for because everyone was focused on the protozoa.
The critical distinction: FCoV itself is usually benign and self-limiting. The fear with FCoV is mutation to FIPV (feline infectious peritonitis virus), but that's a systemic disease with effusions, fevers, neurological signs — not what Penelope has. Her ultrasound showed clean liver, spleen, pancreas, no effusions. This looks like enteric FCoV doing what it does — causing intestinal inflammation in a gut that doesn't have the resilience to shrug it off right now.
