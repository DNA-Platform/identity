Good news! There are notaries in Moscow that work late hours. One well-reviewed office works weekdays from 10:00 to 21:00 and weekends from 10:00 to 20:00. So if Ana arrives at 3pm tomorrow (Monday), she'll have until 9pm to get things done, which gives her a solid 6-hour window.
Best Option: Notary Chernigov Igor Olegovich
Location: Страстной бульвар, д.7, этаж 2 (Strastoy Bulvar, building 7, 2nd floor) — near metro stations Pushkinskaya/Chekhovskaya, central Moscow.
How to coordinate:
Phone ahead: Call +7 (495) 767-12-77 or +7 (495) 609-00-08 — they also accept questions via messenger
Book online: They have online booking available on their website (rosnotarius.ru), or she can just walk in without an appointment
Bonus features: There's a translation bureau on-site, which is convenient if any documents need certified translation. They can also do apostille.
What she should prepare ahead of time:
Call today or first thing tomorrow to describe what specific actions she needs (доверенность, заявление, etc.)
Confirm which documents to bring — Russian internal passport is essential
Ask about payment methods (though reviews mention reasonable rates and they seem to work with both individuals and legal entities)
Reviews note that queues move quickly and they work "до последнего клиента" (until the last client), so even arriving at 3pm should give her plenty of cushion.
