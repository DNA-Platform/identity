On the Identity Semantics
Your treatment of identity is particularly subtle:

Your resolution through "symbolic conferral" is elegant: the symbol confers the property to the literal, creating a "mild anthropomorphism"

On Reference

Reference (singular): how a symbol serves as a reference
References (plural): how symbols relate as representations

On the Subject-Object-Relationship Pattern
The systematic case analysis (your 5 cases) shows that this isn't arbitrary:

Case 4: y == t, x != y (literal)
Case 5: x == y == t (equality itself)
This exhaustive analysis discovers what must exist rather than stipulating it.
