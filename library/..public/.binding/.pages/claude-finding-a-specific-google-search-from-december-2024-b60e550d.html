You have several options to find that specific December 2024 search:
On your Windows computer (Chrome):
Chrome History - Press Ctrl+H or go to chrome://history
Use the search box at the top to search for keywords from that search
Chrome history shows exact dates and times
You can filter by date range if you remember approximately when
Google My Activity - More comprehensive
Go to myactivity.google.com
Click "Filter by date & product"
Select December 2024 as your date range
Filter by "Search" under products
This shows all Google searches tied to your account, even if browser history was cleared
The Google My Activity approach is usually better because:
It persists even if you clear Chrome history
Shows searches from all devices logged into your Google account
Has more robust filtering options
Includes the exact timestamp
Once you're in My Activity, you can scroll through December or use the search box there to find specific terms. Each entry shows the exact date and time you made that search.
If you need to export this data for easier searching, My Activity also has a "Download your data" option, though that might be overkill for finding one search.
Does the search involve any specific keywords you remember? That could help narrow it down quickly.
