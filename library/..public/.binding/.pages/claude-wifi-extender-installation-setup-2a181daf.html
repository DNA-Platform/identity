Based on the image, you have a Xiaomi Mi WiFi range extender. Here's how to set it up:
Basic Setup:
Plug it in - Insert the extender into a power outlet roughly midway between your router and the area with weak WiFi signal
Wait for the indicator light - The LED should start blinking (usually yellow/orange)
Connect to it:
On your phone/computer, go to WiFi settings
Look for a network named something like "Xiaomi_xxxx" or "Mi_WiFi_xxxx"
Connect to it (no password needed initially)
Configure through the app or web interface: Option A - Mi Home App (Recommended):
Download the "Mi Home" app
Create/log into your Xiaomi account
The app should detect the extender
Follow the prompts to connect it to your existing WiFi network Option B - Web Interface:
Once connected to the extender's network, open a browser
Go to 192.168.31.1 or miwifi.com
Follow the setup wizard
Select your existing WiFi network and enter its password
Repositioning - After setup, the extender will reboot. Place it where the indicator shows good signal strength (typically solid blue light means good connection)
