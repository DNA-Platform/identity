Banners don't appear even when you're actively using the phone

Add specific apps to your Focus mode's "Allowed" list

Why This Changed
You're right that you used to get banners - iOS 14 and earlier had better notification behavior. Apple's Focus system in iOS 15+ broke the intuitive "DND only silences when locked" functionality, and many users have been frustrated ever since. The focus system is powerful but requires explicit configuration for every app you want to receive notifications from during Focus periods.
Bottom line: Check your Focus settings first - that's almost certainly why your banners disappeared.
