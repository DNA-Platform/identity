Got everything I need. Here's a full diagnosis:
The most likely culprit: 3DS lockout. Credo Bank's own documentation states explicitly that if the 3D Secure SMS code is entered incorrectly three times, the card gets restricted from online transactions. This is a silent, targeted block — the card keeps working at POS terminals and ATMs, only online payments die. Glovo and Bolt both use card-on-file with 3DS verification, so they'd both fail simultaneously, which matches exactly what Ana is seeing.
The fix is one call: 0. If a transaction cannot be completed successfully, contact Credo Bank's information center at *4242 or visit the nearest service center to remove the restriction. This is the fastest path — just explain the card stopped working on Glovo and Bolt and ask them to lift the online transaction block.
While she's on the call, also verify: that the phone number registered in MyCredo is correct and actually receiving SMS. If the registered number is an old Russian SIM or otherwise inaccessible, the 3DS codes never arrive, and failed attempts would pile up and trigger the lockout.
Secondary possibilities to rule out if the call doesn't resolve it:
Risk Monitoring flag. Credo Bank's security mechanism monitors suspicious operations related to Credo Bank cards. Repeated small charges from delivery apps can pattern-match to fraud heuristics. Same fix — the call center can clear it.
Card expired or overdraft limit hit. Worth checking in MyCredo app under the card details — balance and expiry visible there.
Glovo/Bolt side issue. Less likely since both apps broke simultaneously, but if the bank clears and it still doesn't work, Ana should delete and re-add the card in both apps (the saved card token may have been invalidated).
Google Pay/Apple Pay as workaround in the interim. Credo Bank Mastercard can be added to Google Pay or Apple Pay — this uses a different tokenized payment path that bypasses the card-number-based 3DS restriction and might work even while the standard card is locked for online use.
The action sequence: call *4242 first, get the restriction removed, verify the correct phone number is registered, then re-test Glovo. If still failing, delete/re-add the card in the apps.
