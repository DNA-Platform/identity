Here are your best fits — affordable, community-leaning, and weighted toward Saburtalo so you're not committing to a long commute every day.
Used places map display v0
For best community + Saburtalo proximity: Delisi Hub or Digital Jungle. Both 4.9 rated, both walkable to/from your area, both lean social rather than library-quiet. Delisi Hub reviewers specifically mention making real connections; Digital Jungle runs events and workshops.
For cheapest: Cona / Space Z share a building on Ana Politkovskaia (~25 GEL/24h). Cona is dead-quiet — focus mode, not networking. Space Z is slightly more social with weekend events but vibe reviews are mixed.
Worth the commute for community: Impact Hub at Fabrika courtyard (Marjanishvili). It's the established expat/nomad networking hub in Tbilisi — day pass around $9.50, and the courtyard itself is a social anchor (cafés, bars, hostel crowd). One day a week here might do more for "meeting people" than five days closer to home.
A couple of caveats since pricing across these came from review snippets and listing sites rather than the venues' own pages: confirm rates before committing — Tbilisi prices have moved up since 2023. And worth knowing about two newer Saburtalo options I didn't map: Vibe (in Jikia House, listed at ~10 GEL/day, very budget) and a recently opened Collider Saburtalo branch (more event-driven, pricier ~40 GEL/day). Both worth a walk-by if Delisi Hub doesn't click.
