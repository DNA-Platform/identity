If I have a child account on apple account, can I lock the device or limit screen time?
Claude

Yes, with a child account set up through Apple's Family Sharing, you have robust control over the device through Screen Time. Here's what you can do:
Screen Time Limits
Set limits for specific apps or app categories like games or social media. Your child receives a notification when their allotted time is almost up, and when they run out of time, they can ask you to approve more time.
Downtime (Device Lockdown)
With Downtime, you can block apps and notifications for specific periods. Only phone calls and apps that you choose to allow are available. This is essentially the "lock the device" function you're asking about.
Key Settings to Enable
If your child is still using an app after the limit is reached, you need to enable "Block at End of Limit." Without this, kids can simply tap "Ignore Limit" and continue using apps.
You can also create a dedicated passcode to secure settings, so only you can extend time or make changes.
Managing Remotely
To manage your child's Screen Time from your device, set up a Family Sharing account, then add your child to it. Open Screen Time on your device, then tap your child's name.
One Workaround to Watch For Some kids figure out they can change their device's time zone to bypass Downtime. To prevent this, go to Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > Location Services > System Services > Setting Time Zone and disable it, then set Location Services to "Don't Allow Changes."

Here's how to set up Downtime for your child's device:
From Your iPhone/iPad (Parent Device)
Go to Settings
Tap Screen Time
Under Family, tap your child's name
Tap Downtime
Tap the toggle next to "Scheduled" to turn on and customize the Downtime schedule
Set the start and end times (for example, 9 PM to 7 AM)
You can set up the same downtime schedule for every day, or choose "Custom" to set different times for each day of the week
Critical Step — Enable "Block at Downtime"
Next to "Block at Downtime," tap the toggle. This will mean that when Downtime starts, your child's device will be blocked. Without this setting, they can keep ignoring the time limit by pressing "Ask For More Time."
Set Up "Always Allowed" Apps
After setting Downtime, go back and tap Always Allowed to choose which apps (like Phone or Messages) your child can still access during Downtime. When you schedule downtime, only phone calls and apps that you choose to allow are available.
Instant Lockdown Option
You can now "turn on Downtime until scheduled" — this lets you brick the phone immediately rather than waiting for the scheduled time.
