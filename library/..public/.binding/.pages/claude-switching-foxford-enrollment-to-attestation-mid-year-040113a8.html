Yes, mid-year changes are allowed. Russian education law explicitly permits switching to family education (семейное образование) at any time, including mid-year. Foxford's documentation confirms: "You can send the notification at any time: before the start of the school year or in the middle of it."
Program Switching with Recalculation: Foxford allows changing programs during the contract period with a financial recalculation. Their FAQ specifically addresses this scenario.
For Your Situation - Key Options:
Courses Without Attestation: Foxford explicitly has programs for families who "don't need a certificate, only knowledge" (they address this in their FAQ for families abroad). You can:
Purchase individual course access without the school attachment component
Switch to recorded lessons (записи) instead of live webinars - more flexible for different time zones
Drop the attestation package entirely if attestation will happen through the Georgian center
How Foxford Attestation Works:
Foxford has a teaching license but no state accreditation
They partner with accredited schools (like "Наши Пенаты") for formal attestation
The attestation component is actually a separate add-on that can be removed
