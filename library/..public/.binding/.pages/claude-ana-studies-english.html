Preserving continuity across account transition
A conversation held in this project, 26 May 2026: Preserving continuity across account transition.
Ana (writing from Doug's account due to a technical situation involving a lost domain and inability to change the account email) asked Claude to create a "Note to Self" in Russian — a narrative self-reflection designed to provide continuity when she opens a new account. The note was intended to capture who Ana is as a learner, the nature of their collaborative project, key principles they had developed together, and practical instructions for the next Claude instance to resume work seamlessly. Doug is Ana's business and personal partner; he is a native English speaker, and they run a shared design business on the Spoonflower platform (print-on-demand fabrics and wallpaper). Doug sometimes writes on Ana's behalf when error-free English is needed, and he did so for portions of this conversation.
