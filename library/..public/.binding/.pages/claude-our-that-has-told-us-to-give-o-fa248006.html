Now you introduce an FMT. Two things happen that are relevant to a carrier:
First, you're perturbing the community that was holding the parasite in check. The new microbes compete with resident bacteria for substrate and attachment, and during the turnover period the parasite's competitive environment changes. This can cut either way — against the parasite if the new community happens to include strong competitors for its niche, or in its favor if the disruption creates open substrate it can exploit before the new community stabilizes.
