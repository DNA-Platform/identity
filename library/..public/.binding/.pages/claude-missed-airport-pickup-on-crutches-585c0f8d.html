At Istanbul Airport, special assistance is handled by HAVAŞ (the airport's ground services). Here's what you need to do right now:
Call the airport's Special Assistance Service directly:
+90 212 465 3000 (main airport information - ask for special assistance/handicap services)
Or dial 444 1 442 (HAVAŞ ground services hotline)
You can also try to flag down any airport staff member immediately and explain the situation - they should be able to radio for a wheelchair/assistance to come get you urgently since your flight is boarding.
If you're in the international arrivals area, there should also be information desks with staff who can call for immediate assistance. Look for anyone with an airport uniform or badge.
Given that your flight is boarding, I'd recommend calling one of those numbers immediately while also trying to get the attention of any visible airport staff. Don't wait passively - they may have forgotten you were waiting or there may have been a miscommunication about your location.
Which terminal are you in, and is this a domestic or international connection? That might help determine if you actually need to clear passport control or if you can stay airside.
