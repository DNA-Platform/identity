Yes. Magti has a "Roamer Days" deal that covers Denmark. Scandinavia: 5 GB for 15 GEL, valid 7 days. Sweden, Norway and Finland are included too. You can activate it until 31 Dec 2026.
How to set it up:
Turn on roaming before you leave Georgia: dial *133# OK. It's free.
Buy the package: dial *137# OK, or use the MyMagti app. The app also works from abroad.
Keep a positive balance on your account. Make sure Data Roaming is on in your phone settings.
There are also general "Roamer" packages if the Scandinavia one doesn't fit your trip: 1 GB for 9 GEL (7 days), 4 GB for 25 GEL (14 days), or 10 GB for 55 GEL (30 days). Magti's page has the full list of countries for these, and I didn't confirm Denmark is on it. Once a package runs out, data costs 0.25 GEL per MB, which adds up quickly. Incoming calls are also charged while you're roaming.

No. Roaming being ON is the only switch you need here. Leave the rest as they are.
Standard roaming internet tariff: this is pay-per-MB data, about 0.25 GEL/MB, with no package. Keeping it OFF is safer, because data should just stop when your package runs out instead of billing per MB. Magti only says you need it for VoLTE calling in the US, Japan and Australia, which doesn't apply to Denmark.
CLIR / CLIPOVR: these hide or reveal your caller ID. They have nothing to do with roaming.
SMS secretary, Number Maintenance, Block the service: also unrelated.
You still have to buy the Scandinavia package itself, which isn't on this screen. Dial *137# OK, or look under "Order" in the app (my guess for where it lives). Do it before you fly, and turn on Data Roaming in your iPhone's settings once you land.
