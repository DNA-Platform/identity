Deixis and determiners in English
A conversation held in this project, 9 February 2026: Deixis and determiners in English.
50 exchanges
The user engaged in an in-depth exploration of English grammar, specifically focusing on determiners and their function as reference operators in language. They began by analyzing the sentence "That question has hounded me ever since I was ten" and identifying what they called the "pointeriness" of determiners like "that," "this," "the," and "a." Through systematic questioning, they worked to understand the complete taxonomy of determiners, including articles (a, an, the), demonstratives (this, that, these, those), possessives (my, your, his, her, etc.), quantifiers (some, any, every, many, few, several), numerals (one, two, three), and interrogatives (which, what, whose).
