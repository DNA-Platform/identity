Here's my thinking for Dima's birthday — and I'll cut to the recommendation first.
The activity: Escape Room Tbilisi (Harry Potter room)
This is genuinely the right call for a 9 and 10 year old as a family of four. It feels like an adventure and a gift, not just a "place we went." A family doing a Harry Potter escape room together is exactly the kind of thing kids this age remember. The place has earned strong reviews specifically for the Harry Potter quest, with English-friendly staff and an owner who gets praised for storytelling — so the language barrier shouldn't be an issue. They charge 75 GEL per person with a maximum of 6 players per room, so for your family of four that's 300 GEL total for the experience. Note that it only opens at 3PM, so plan your day accordingly.
One honest caveat from the reviews: a couple noted it's relatively simple compared to escape rooms in Western Europe, and one group finished in 30 minutes. For kids who haven't done many escape rooms before, that's actually fine — it'll feel challenging without being frustrating. If you want a longer day, you could start with Magic City at City Mall (10AM, right in Saburtalo) — trampolines, arcade games, go-karts — it uses a card-based system with no time limits so kids can just play until they're done. Then Bolt to the escape room at 3PM.
For dinner: Maspindzelo Express (Akhvlediani St, near Rustaveli)
Maspindzelo is a Tbilisi mainstay popular with local families for birthdays and celebrations, known for its raucous, festive atmosphere. The Express branch on Akhvlediani is highly rated, has beautiful interiors, an English menu, and khinkali and khachapuri that reviewers call "10/10." It's affordable, lively without being overwhelming, and very easy to reach from the escape room by Bolt. Exactly the right energy for the birthday dinner cap-off.

Here's a broad menu — pick what feels right for the kids' energy:
Right in Saburtalo (City Mall)
Focus Mokus — arcade games, multi-level soft play, trampolines, small climbing wall, and a laser tag arena for older kids. Probably the single best all-rounder for ages 8-10 if you want flashing lights plus physical activity in one place. Card-based, no time limit, free entry.
Magic City — Also in City Mall, more arcade/game-machine oriented: racing games, shooting games, claw machines, air hockey, go-karts, cotton candy machine. More "pure arcade" than Focus Mokus. Both are in the same building so you could actually combine them.
Trampoline Parks
Boom Boom (East Point Mall) — Massive trampoline park with wall-to-wall trampolines, foam pit, dodgeball court, and basketball slam-dunk hoops. Better for burning energy than winning prizes, but kids this age tend to love it.
Crazy Hall — Trampolines, climbing wall, foam pit, plus basketball, dodgeball, and volleyball. More of a sports-activity vibe than a pure arcade.
Laser Tag
Lazer Lounge — Atmospheric and well-decorated laser tag arena with good equipment; reviewers specifically mention birthday parties with kids going well. Also has darts and table tennis for between rounds.
Go-Karts + Cinema + Arcade Combo
Astra Park (Digomi) — Large indoor park with go-karts, arcade games, a 7D cinema, and billiards. Open from 2PM on weekdays, noon on weekends. Go-karts start from 30 GEL/session. The 7D cinema could feel like a real birthday treat for a 9-year-old.
VR Gaming
Another World VR — A VR arcade chain with a Tbilisi location. Immersive games designed for all ages; for kids who've never done proper VR this can feel genuinely mind-blowing and very much like a birthday gift. Worth checking their current address and booking ahead.
Bowling + Cinema
East Point Mall — Bowling alley, kids fun zone with games and indoor rides, and an IMAX movie theater. Bowling as the birthday activity followed by a movie is a classic format that works well precisely because there's no pressure — it's just fun.
Galleria Tbilisi (Freedom Square area) — Bowling and a cinema, plus a free children's play area. More central, slightly easier Bolt ride.
Indoor Water Park (if you want something more ambitious)
Gino Paradise — The outdoor section is closed in early spring, but there's a large indoor swimming pool and a warm-water hot tub open year-round. More of a half-day commitment and pricier, but if Dima and his sister are water kids, arriving at a water park as a birthday surprise is hard to beat.
My honest read: for a 9 and 10 year old who like lights and games over puzzles, Focus Mokus + laser tag (either within Focus Mokus itself or Lazer Lounge separately) is the strongest single-venue birthday option. Boom Boom is the move if they're more physical/energetic. Another World VR is the highest "wow factor" per hour if they've never done it. Any of these pair naturally with Maspindzelo Express or similar for dinner afterward.
