But Maxim's appeal is the vector to watch: an adverse appellate outcome would be the document flowing through that pipe. Worth one direct question to the Georgian lawyers on whether Georgia still honors Minsk recognition in family matters post-CIS-withdrawal — and note that granted protection status would close that channel for Dima regardless, which quietly adds a second function to the application beyond the travel document: it doesn't just solve his papers, it severs the last legal pipe between a Russian courtroom and this child.
