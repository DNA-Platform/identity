Hamilton Morris did his undergraduate studies at The New School (Eugene Lang College) in New York, having also spent time at the University of Chicago. He studied science and anthropology, graduating with a bachelor's degree that combined those interests. He began writing for Vice magazine while still an undergraduate.
Most of his hands-on chemistry knowledge came later, though: he's noted that most of his knowledge of chemical synthesis came during the decade or so he spent researching the chemistry and pharmacology of psychedelic compounds at the University of the Sciences in Philadelphia, where he worked in a lab rather than as an undergraduate.
