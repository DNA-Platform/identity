Based on the search results, here's what I found:
Yes, Turkey has a centralized system — your ikamet status is in the national migration database regardless of which office processed it. However, the practical recommendation from multiple sources is to print your Müracaat Belgesi (application/approval document) with the QR code.
You can get this from e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr by clicking "I want to know the result of my application" and then clicking the "Print Application Document" button. This application document with the QR code entitles you to visa-free entry at airports even if you do not receive your residence permit card.
The QR code is specifically designed so officials can verify your status in the system.
For extra peace of mind:
Print that Müracaat Belgesi before you travel
Refer to the approval PDF document you were provided as proof in the meantime
If you need to leave Turkey before receiving the residence permit card, make sure you have an Approval Document (Müracaat Belgesi) and residence payment receipt with you at the airport that proves your application for residence permit.
One thing to be aware of: if you have applied for a residence permit and exit with your applicant form, you are exempted from the visa requirement when entering the country if you return within fifteen days each time.
So while the airport should be able to see your status, having that printed document with QR code is your safety net and the officially recommended approach.
