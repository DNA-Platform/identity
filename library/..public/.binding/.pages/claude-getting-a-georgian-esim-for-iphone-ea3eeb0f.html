Here's the reality — there's a catch. When buying a Georgian SIM card, you will need to present your physical passport/ID for registration. This is a Georgian legal requirement for all carriers.
What you can do online vs. what requires a visit:
You can activate a new or existing number with eSIM either via the app/web portal MyMagti or at any Magti service center. However, registration on MyMagti requires an existing subscriber number or personal email to begin. So the portal is really for managing an existing account, not getting a brand new one from scratch.
You can purchase a Magti eSIM at a service center, and you'll need to bring your passport or other form of identification.
So in practice, you'll likely need one visit to a Magti service center with your passport to register and get your number. After that, everything — top-ups, package purchases, eSIM management — can be done through the MyMagti app with a credit card.
The good news for long-term use:
Magti has the best network coverage in Georgia at 99% of populated areas, works underground in Tbilisi metro stations, and is now on 5G in most locations.
The eSIM costs just 10 GEL (~$3.70) as a one-time fee
Most packages have 30-day validity, and if you top up within 7 days, remaining data rolls over automatically.
You can top up and buy data packages easily through the MyMagti app with an international debit card.

Great news — you can do this entirely from your phone without going anywhere. Someone posted a detailed walkthrough on TripAdvisor that confirms it works:
Step by step via the MyMagti app:
Download the "MagTi" app (by Magticom) from the App Store
Open the app, tap "Order" on the bottom menu, then tap "Purchase a Number." Set both min and max price filters to 0 GEL and tap Check — a list of free phone numbers will appear. Choose one you like.
The app will ask you to scan your passport and complete facial recognition. It took a few tries for the camera to recognize the face, but it eventually worked. After that, a contract document is generated automatically — scroll down to sign it.
The app asked for email verification — enter your email, receive a message in Georgian containing a 4-digit code, and enter it back in the app.
An option appeared to activate the eSIM. After clicking it, the app generated a QR code, which was scanned through the phone's Network settings by selecting "Add Mobile Plan." The installation completed successfully.
Two things to watch for:
The app may automatically fill in your name from the passport scan, but it can be incorrect — so double-check before signing.
Sometimes foreign-issued cards won't work on the Magti website/app. If payment shows as declined even though it was processed, contact Magti's customer support through their chat and they'll help complete the transaction.
For data packages once you're set up: In the Home tab under "Offers," you can scroll sideways to find packages like Unlimited Internet Week (10 GEL) and Unlimited Internet Month (35 GEL).
So you can do the whole thing from the couch — passport scan, face verification, eSIM activation, and data purchase. No store visit needed.
