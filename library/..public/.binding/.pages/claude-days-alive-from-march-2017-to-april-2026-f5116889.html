From March 24, 2017 to April 25, 2026 is 3,320 days.
Quick breakdown: March 24, 2017 to March 24, 2026 is exactly 9 years. That span includes 3 leap days (2020, 2024, plus Feb 29 of any leap year in between — here 2020 and 2024), so it's 9 × 365 + 3 = 3,288 days. Then March 24 to April 25, 2026 adds 32 days, for 3,320 total.
