For an American palate in Saburtalo, the clear standout on both apps is Carrefour — it carries the widest range of recognizable Western/imported brands (peanut butter, cereals, sauces, snacks, larger meat cuts, baking staples) that the Georgian chains mostly don't stock. There's a branch right in Saburtalo at 37 Sulkhan Tsintsadze St (City Mall area), available on Wolt and Glovo.
Here's how the realistic options compare, all deliverable to Saburtalo:
Carrefour Saburtalo — best for breadth of Western/American-style products. The French hypermarket model means imported brands, international aisle, proper meat counter. One honest caveat: its Wolt rating sits around 8.6, noticeably below the boutique stores, which usually reflects out-of-stock substitutions and slower picking at large hypermarkets. Set your substitution preferences ("refund" vs "replace") carefully at checkout.
Goodwill Saburtalo — the first hypermarket chain in Georgia, premium-leaning with good fresh produce, a strong prepared-foods/deli section, and bakery. Closest local equivalent to a full American supermarket experience, though imported-brand depth is thinner than Carrefour.
Europroduct — stocks goods direct from European producers, with access to organic, lactose-free, gluten-free, vegan, and sugar-free lines. More European-specialty/dietary than American, but useful if you want familiar Western packaged goods and specific dietary items.
Agrohub (your comparison point) — skews fresh, local, organic farm produce and quality meat/dairy. Excellent for ingredients, weak for processed/branded "American" pantry items.
For your use case, I'd start with Carrefour as the primary cart for pantry and branded goods, and use Goodwill or Agrohub for fresh produce and meat where quality beats Carrefour's.
