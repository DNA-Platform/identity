Are basic telegram gifts on the blockchain?
Claude
18:40 UTC, 12 February 2026
Searched the web for “Telegram basic gifts blockchain”
No. Regular (basic) gifts are simple virtual items with unlimited supply that have no real value — they cannot be traded on the secondary market and have no possibility of being converted into NFTs. They're just in-app animations stored on Telegram's servers.
Only gifts that have been upgraded to "collectibles" become blockchain assets on TON. Upgrading costs a small amount of Telegram Stars to cover blockchain fees, and there's a 21-day hold period after receiving a gift before it can be upgraded. Once upgraded, the gift gets unique traits (backdrop, symbol, number) and lives on-chain as an NFT that can be traded on marketplaces like Fragment or Getgems.
So the short version: basic gifts = off-chain, collectible/upgraded gifts = on-chain (TON blockchain).

How to upgrade a gift:
Go to My Profile → Gifts, tap the gift you want to upgrade, and you'll see an "Upgrade" option. Upgrading costs a small amount of Telegram Stars to cover blockchain fees. Once upgraded, it gets a randomized unique appearance with custom traits (backdrop, symbol, number).
Can you upgrade after giving it?
No — the upgrade option appears after you receive a gift, not after you send one. So the recipient is the one who decides whether to upgrade it to a collectible. That said, when buying a gift for someone, you can send it already upgraded so they unwrap a collectible right in the chat. But once you've already sent a basic gift, it's in the recipient's hands — they'd need to upgrade it themselves.
