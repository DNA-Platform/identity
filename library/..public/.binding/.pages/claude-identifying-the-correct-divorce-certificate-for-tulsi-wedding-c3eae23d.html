Almost certainly the certified Judgment of Divorce — that's the Suffolk County Clerk's certification page (Vincent Puleo, certifying the Judgment filed 11/23/2021), together with the actual multi-page Judgment of Divorce (Rubino v. Rubino, Index 615428/2020, signed by Hon. Caren Loguercio, entered Nov 23, 2021), plus its apostille. In your screenshots, that's the document you captioned "record from my county where I was divorced" and the four-tile set in Image 3.
Two caveats worth flagging before you assume:
"Divorce certificate" isn't a precise term in New York. NY courts issue a Judgment of Divorce (what you have). Separately, NYS Department of Health Vital Records issues a Certificate of Dissolution of Marriage — a one-page summary document, different from the court judgment. You don't appear to have that one. Some agencies — and some non-US wedding offices working from translated checklists — actually mean the DOH certificate when they say "divorce certificate."
The Certificate of No Appeals (last document, Image 3) literally has "Certificate" in the name and confirms the divorce is final. Some jurisdictions specifically want this alongside the judgment.
My recommendation: don't guess — ask Tbili Events directly which they want. Something like:
"To confirm — by 'US divorce certificate' do you mean (a) the certified Judgment of Divorce from the Suffolk County Court (which I have, apostilled), or (b) the Certificate of Dissolution of Marriage from the NY State Department of Health Vital Records (a separate document)? I want to make sure I'm sending the right one."
If they say (b), that's a separate request to NYS DOH and takes time — better to find out now.
