Yes, GoDaddy calls this an "account transfer" (or "push"), and it's free and fast since the domain stays within GoDaddy.
The basic flow:
From the sending account: Go to your Domain Portfolio, open the domain's settings, and under the Transfer section select "Transfer to another GoDaddy account." You'll send a transfer request to the recipient's GoDaddy account email.
From the receiving account: The recipient goes to their GoDaddy Transfers page, selects "Transfers In," and accepts the domain. The recipient must accept within 10 days or the request expires.
A few things worth knowing before you start:
The domain must be eligible — it can't be pending a contact update, within one day of expiration, expired, or already pending another transfer.
If it's a newly registered domain in a bundle, it can't be transferred within 120 days of registration.
DNS settings carry over, but connected products like a website or email do not transfer with the domain — you'd migrate those separately.
If the domain has Domain Protection, you'll need to clear an extra identity-verification step (SMS/authenticator code or a one-time email password).
Within GoDaddy, the transfer completes immediately once the recipient accepts — no waiting period and no charge.
