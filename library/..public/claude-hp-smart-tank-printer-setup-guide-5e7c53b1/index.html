Claude
15:35 UTC, 18 March 2026
That's an HP Smart Tank printer — the cartridge you see is actually the printhead, not an ink cartridge. Here's the deal:
Why the "cartridge" (printhead)?
Unlike regular inkjet printers where the cartridge contains the ink, this printer separates the two. The printhead is the component that actually sprays ink onto paper — it lives inside the printer permanently (or for years). The bottles are just the ink reservoir that feeds it. You need both.
Setup order
1. Install the printhead first
The orange cartridge sitting in the middle of the printer bay — slide/snap it into the printhead carriage (the moving rail inside). Remove any orange protective tape/caps first.
2. Fill the ink tanks
On the left side of the printer you'll see four tanks with colored caps (matching your bottles: yellow, magenta/pink, cyan, black)
Open each tank cap, match bottle color to tank color, insert the bottle tip into the tank opening and let it drain in — they're designed to stop automatically when full, no squeezing needed
Recap the tanks when done
3. Power on and run setup
The printer will prompt you through a printhead alignment and priming cycle on its screen — just follow it
This takes 5–10 minutes and uses a small amount of ink
4. Connect to WiFi
Use the small touchscreen panel to connect to your network, or use the HP Smart app on your phone which walks you through the whole thing
One important note
Don't throw away the printhead — it's reusable for the life of the printer. Only the ink bottles get replaced when empty.

Here's exactly what to do:
1. Pull up the paper tray — it's the tray you can see in the photo that's already partially extended. Pull it upward/outward until it's fully open.
2. Slide out the paper guide — there's a sliding tab on the side of the tray. Push it outward to make room for the paper.

4. Adjust the paper guide back — slide the guide back in so it sits snugly against the edge of the paper stack. Not too tight, just touching it. This keeps paper feeding straight.

Then what happens next:
After loading paper, wait for the printer to print an alignment page. When the Open Lid light blinks, open the scanner lid, place the printed alignment page face-down on the scanner glass aligned to the indicated corner, close the lid, and press the blinking copy button. The "A" icon will blink on the display until alignment is complete.
Don't skip that alignment scan — it's what finalizes the whole setup.
