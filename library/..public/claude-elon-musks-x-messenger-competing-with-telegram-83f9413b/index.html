What XChat actually is
It's X's upgraded DM system, now being spun out as a standalone app, built on Rust with claimed "Bitcoin-style" encryption, vanishing messages, file sharing, and audio/video calling. No phone number required, no ads, no user tracking — and group chats up to 481 participants.
The bull case for XChat
The network effect argument is real: X starts from a position of genuine scale with 500+ million monthly users already on the platform. And the timing is opportunistic — WhatsApp is currently facing a class-action lawsuit alleging Meta intercepted private messages, and both Musk and Durov have publicly piled on.
Why it probably won't seriously threaten Telegram — especially your thesis
Three structural reasons:
Trust deficit on privacy. The App Store listing reveals the app may still collect data on user location, contacts, and search history — a direct contradiction to the "zero tracking" claims. Security experts are also dismissing "Bitcoin-style encryption" as a marketing term with no technical substance, and unlike Signal, XChat has no independently audited protocol.
Feature gap on power users. XChat currently lacks Telegram's stickers, channels, and bots — the ecosystem features that retain power users and communities. Channels in particular are where Telegram's censorship-resistance thesis actually lives.
The Musk discount. Musk originally promised XChat to all X users by June 2025 — it slipped roughly 10 months. That execution pattern, combined with frequent X policy changes and content moderation controversies, creates brand baggage that will spill over.
The thesis angle for you specifically
XChat competes in the Western consumer messaging market — people switching from WhatsApp. Telegram's moat for your thesis is entirely elsewhere: MTProto's censorship resistance for Russian and authoritarian-adjacent users, the channel/group infrastructure for dissident media, and the TON wallet integration. XChat cannot replicate any of that. An authoritarian regime blocking Telegram still has zero reason to allow XChat, and XChat offers no circumvention tooling, no Proxy/MTProxy architecture, no meaningful anonymous account infrastructure.
If anything, XChat validating that "private encrypted messaging" is a large and valuable market is mildly bullish for Telegram's positioning — it elevates the conversation Durov has been having for years.
The TONX Q1 call on Wednesday is still your near-term signal event. XChat is noise relative to that.
