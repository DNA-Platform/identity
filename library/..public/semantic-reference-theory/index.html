Semantic Primes
A conversation held in this project, 16 June 2026: Semantic Primes.
The conversation centered on semantic primes and Natural Semantic Metalanguage (NSM) theory, with the person requesting research on the topic and a structured editable artifact. Claude created a markdown document covering the history of NSM, the canonical 65-prime inventory organized by onto-syntactic categories, and the original 1972 set of 14 primitives proposed by Wierzbicka. The document included a table mapping original 1972 primes to their modern descendants and noted which three (IMAGINE, BECOME, WORLD) did not survive cross-linguistic testing.
