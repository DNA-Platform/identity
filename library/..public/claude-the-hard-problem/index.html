A conversation held in this project, 11 September 2026: The Project.
September 202688 exchanges
This conversation centered on the development of a philosophy paper titled Pointing Out at the Problem of Consciousness (later refined to Pointing Outward at the Problem of Consciousness), positioned in the lineage of Chalmers's "Facing Up to the Problem of Consciousness" (1995), Dennett's "Facing Backwards on the Problem of Consciousness" (1996), and Chalmers's "Moving Forward on the Problem of Consciousness" (1997). The person is developing this paper as a front piece to a broader theory called Semantic Reference Theory (SRT). The core argument developed across the conversation: for anything to have a quality there must be a qualifier, for anything to be individuated an identifier, for anything to be related a relative. The paper's stopping rule is that it may assert a referrer is required but may not assert the referrer contains its referents. The three principal figures—Chalmers (identifier of the problem), Dennett (disqualifier), and Hofstadter (relative to both, serving as referee)—mirror the paper's own ontological triad. A four-panel figure was designed showing a crossing-out procedure where the writer ultimately adds themselves to a completed ontology, justified as a fixed-point construction and a direct response to Dennett's first-person subtraction.
