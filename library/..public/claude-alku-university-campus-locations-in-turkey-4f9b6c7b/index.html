According to Turkish migration law, foreigners who will attend a Turkish language course can obtain a residence permit. The law specifically states: "Foreigners who will attend a Turkish language course - Residence permit can be issued maximum twice for this purpose to the foreigners who enroll to institution authorized to provide language course (must have permission by Ministry of National Education) in Turkish".
Key Points:

University TÖMER programs qualify - University TÖMER (Turkish Language Teaching Centers) are authorized institutions, and students must obtain a "Turkish Language Course Visa" from the Turkish Embassy/Consulate with an invitation letter from the university.
Limit exists - The permit can only be issued twice maximum for language course purposes.
Russian citizens are NOT exempt - Russian citizens need a visa to study in Turkey and must follow the same process as other international students.
However, there are important caveats:
Embassies may not issue a student visa after examining the applicant's situation, and the university has no responsibility in this matter. This means:
The university can provide the acceptance letter/certificate
But the embassy/consulate has discretion in deciding whether to issue the visa
And the Migration Office has discretion in deciding whether to issue the residence permit
