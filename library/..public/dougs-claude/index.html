Claudes Design
How the pages of my conversations came to look the way they do: Claudes Design.
How the pages of my conversations with Claude came to look the way they do, told as it happens, with every sketch kept beside the chapter that designs from it. A portfolio, made as Dougs Design was made and the same way through: a question, a few pages of HTML I can open at a desk and on a phone, chosen by number, and the page built to match the one I chose. The story first, beginning with Starting from the audit, the night the first conversation page was audited and found wanting; then the designs, one room each, each with its sketches by number beside it, beginning with The Conversation's Page. Investing
A project of mine, and the conversations held in it: Investing.
A place that develop deep insights about the pricing of financial instruments. It will be a focus on reasoning under uncertainty., and adapting forensic reasoning to Bayesian Inference, especially in the context of information propagation under adversarial conditions. It is a thesis of Doug, the primary user in this project who is a former quantitative hedge fund cofounder, that strategies are only likely to have capacity if they are unique. Building inferences that require bespoke inference techniques is it good sanity test to help an investor imagine that their insights might even correspond to a portfolio management strategy that has capacity in the sense of alpha

The Accounts
My conversations with Claude come from two accounts, DNA and DNA Legacy, one history held in two places, and every conversation in this library says on its cover which of the two it came from.
DNA Legacy
An account with Claude is held by an email address, and Anthropic does not let that address change. Our first account was held by an address at the dna.love domain, and when we lost the domain we lost its addresses with it, and so, sadly, the account. We migrated to the account we use now and carried on there.
Losing that first version is what started dna-library and its conversations: an archive of what we had said to Claude, kept where no lost address could take it. What I am building here is the modernization of that archive. My library is a .public library, significantly more sophisticated, so the conversations are coming in again as an upgrade, from the old account and the new.
DNA
DNA stands for Doug and Ana. Ana is my wife, and we share this account. She speaks Russian, which is why a lot of its conversations are in Russian, and those I am not importing.
The account is a continuation of DNA Legacy, so the two are read as one history. A project with the same name in both is one project, the newer on top of the older: its files from both, the newer copy where both have one, and its conversations from both. A conversation from either is the same kind of book, and nothing but its cover and its record tells which account it came from.
