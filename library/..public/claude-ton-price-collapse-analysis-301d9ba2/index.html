The non-transferability and non-redeemability are doing all the legal work. Under Howey, the "expectation of profits" prong gets weak when what you're getting back is more of the same service you already bought, denominated in service units rather than currency. The real concerns are downstream of securities law. The really elegant feature is that the marginal cost of "another month of Telegram" to Telegram itself is essentially zero, so they can be generous with bonus accrual without real margin cost.
