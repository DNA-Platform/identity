BotFather's mini app: bot settings now open in BotFather's MiniApp rather than the old command-driven chat flow. So your bot list is under @BotFather → open its mini app → "My Bots," instead of typing /mybots (though the command still works).
If you were looking for bots you've chatted with rather than ones you own, check the "Apps" tab in the search screen or the bottom-bar Apps section if your client shows one.
