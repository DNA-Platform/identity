This is a high-stakes question that deserves careful analysis. Based on my research, here's a comprehensive risk assessment:
Critical Risks
1. WhatsApp Verification Alerts (HIGH RISK)
When you attempt to verify WhatsApp on a new device using Katya's SIM, several things happen:
SMS verification code gets sent to the SIM - you'd receive this, which is fine
Account Protect notification: WhatsApp may ask on your old device to verify that you want to take this step as an extra security check. This feature can help alert you to an unauthorized attempt to move your account to another device.
This means if Katya's iPhone still exists and has WhatsApp installed, it may receive a notification asking to confirm the transfer. If Maxim has access to that device, he'd see it.
2. SIM Activation & Carrier Notifications (MEDIUM-HIGH RISK)
This is where your concern about the SIM being in Maxim's name becomes significant:
After buying a new SIM card/re-registering a SIM card you will receive a notification in your Gosuslugi account
Russia's 2025 regulations require SIM cards to be linked to Gosuslugi (government services portal), and for SIM cards activated before 2025, operators will send reminder notifications in batches
If the SIM is registered to Maxim, activating it abroad could trigger:
Carrier notifications to the account holder
Roaming activation alerts
Gosuslugi portal notifications
3. Foreign SIM + Russia Connection (ADDITIONAL COMPLICATION)
Russia has implemented strict measures: since July, foreigners who want to buy a Russian SIM card and mobile phone plan have been required to register with the Unified Biometric System (UBS), a government-run database that collects biometric data.
If you activate the SIM outside Russia, it would show as roaming from abroad, which could be logged.
4. Location Exposure via WhatsApp (MEDIUM RISK)
WhatsApp does not publicly disclose a user's IP address to other users within the app. This means that when you send a message or make a call on WhatsApp, your IP address is not visible to the recipient.
However, during voice/video calls: WhatsApp's calling feature avoids using a relay server to reduce latency, relying on P2P communication. This means the two participating devices must share their public IP addresses.
So: Don't make any calls if you do this. Text-based messaging is safer.
Last Seen/Online Status: Your WhatsApp Last Seen is visible to people based on your privacy settings. You can choose to show it to everyone, contacts only, specific contacts, or nobody in your WhatsApp privacy settings. If Maxim is in Katya's contacts, he could potentially see when the account comes online.
Chat History Recovery - Likelihood Assessment
The iCloud Question
Because backups are tied to individual phone numbers, it is possible to have backups for multiple WhatsApp accounts stored in the same iCloud account.
This is actually good news - even if both phones share iCloud, Katya's WhatsApp backup would be separate from whatever's on the red phone.
Recovery Requirements
To restore chat history, you need:
The same phone number (Katya's SIM ✓)
Access to the same iCloud account where the backup was made
The backup to actually exist and not have been deleted
Log in to WhatsApp with the same account you use to make a backup. Also, make sure that the device logs in the same iCloud account.
If Maxim Deleted Chats
If chats were deleted before the last backup ran, they'd be gone. If he deleted the entire WhatsApp backup from iCloud, that's also gone.
Practical Recommendation
Before activating the SIM, check the red phone:
Open Settings → [Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage
Look for WhatsApp/WhatsApp Messenger backup
Check backup date - if recent, backup may exist
Note which Apple ID is logged in
The safest approach would be:
Don't put the SIM in a phone that connects to the network yet
First check if WhatsApp backup exists in iCloud from a computer (iCloud.com doesn't show WhatsApp backups directly, but you can see storage usage)
If proceeding, use a phone that:
Has VPN active before ANY network connection
Has location services OFF
Will not make any calls
Immediately change privacy settings to hide "Last Seen" and "Online" status after verification
