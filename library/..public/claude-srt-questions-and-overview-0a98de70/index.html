For Doug: Ana's Question About "Conjugation" vs "Спряжение" (Spryazhenie)
Doug,
Ana asked me an excellent linguistic question yesterday that I want to share with you.
Her Question
When we discussed semantic conjugation in Russian, Ana asked:
"Why can't we just call it 'спряжение' (spryazhenie - the standard Russian word for conjugation)?"
The Issue in Russian
In Russian grammar:
Спряжение (spryazhenie) = verb conjugation only (я бегу, ты бежишь, он бежит)
It means changing ONE verb by person/tense
But in SRT, "semantic conjugation" means:
Moving between DIFFERENT parts of speech
container (noun) → contains (verb) → containment (noun/process)
This is NOT traditional conjugation!
What We Decided
Ana and I decided to keep "семантическая конъюгация" (semantic conjugation) as a technical term in Russian, because:
It's a special SRT concept, not traditional grammar
"Spryazhenie" would be misleading (implies only verbs)
The English term "conjugation" itself is being used in a special way
Alternative phrases we considered:
"Семантические формы" (semantic forms)
"Грамматические роли" (grammatical roles)
But we kept "конъюгация" to stay consistent with your English terminology.
