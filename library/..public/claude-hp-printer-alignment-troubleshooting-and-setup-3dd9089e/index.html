Good, I have enough to work with. Here's the full picture:
Setting up your computer first
The easiest path is the HP Smart app — it handles drivers, Wi-Fi setup, and gives you access to alignment tools all in one place.
Windows: Install from the Microsoft Store or search "HP Smart" there
Mac: Install from the Mac App Store
Don't connect a USB cable until the installer specifically prompts you to — if you're going wireless, make sure the printer is on your Wi-Fi network first. The 582 supports Wi-Fi, so you'll likely be connecting wirelessly.
The alignment issue
The alignment process on first setup works like this: the printer prints a page, you place it face-down on the scanner glass, and the printer scans it to calibrate. If it's stuck, here's the sequence to try:
Step 1 — Make sure the page is placed correctly. Lift the scanner lid, place the alignment page print-side down on the scanner glass, and position it according to the engraved guides around the glass. This is the most common reason it gets stuck — the page is slightly off.
Step 2 — Power cycle. With the printer turned on, disconnect the power cord from the printer, unplug from the wall, wait 60 seconds, reconnect to a wall outlet, and turn on. HP specifically recommends plugging directly into a wall outlet, not a power strip.
Step 3 — Paper quality. Try using a fresh stack of plain white paper — older paper can cause alignment failures due to slight warping or moisture absorption.
Step 4 — Clean the scanner glass. Dust or smudges on the scanner glass can interfere with alignment page detection — clean it thoroughly and retry.
Step 5 — If it keeps looping, you can cancel and re-trigger alignment manually: open the HP Smart app, go to Printer Settings → Tools / Advanced Settings → Align Printhead, and print the page fresh from there.
What to tell me next
A few things would help narrow this down fast:
Is the printer printing the alignment page and then failing when it tries to scan it — or is it not even printing the page?
What does the printer's control panel display say when it's stuck?
Are you on Windows or Mac?

Here's my reading of the panel state, cross-referenced with the HP documentation:
WiFi button (solid blue) — when the printer is connected successfully to a Wi-Fi network, the Wi-Fi light stops blinking and remains solid. So you're connected to the network. Good.
The display — it's showing a device/computer icon plus the WiFi icon at the top, with "00" and a document icon at the bottom. The number counter shows the number of copies during a copy job, and the document icon appears when the printer is in Document Copy mode. The device icon alongside WiFi likely indicates Wi-Fi Direct status or network connection.
The color copy button lit cyan — this is the most important one for your alignment issue. During printhead alignment: open the scanner lid, place the printed alignment page print-side down on the scanner glass, and then press the blinking copy button to scan the page.
So the most likely interpretation: the printer has already printed the alignment page and is now waiting for you to place it on the scanner glass and press that cyan copy button to complete the scan. It's not frozen — it's just sitting there waiting for your input.

Вижу — задание застряло в очереди со статусом "Printing, Error" и не отменяется кнопкой Cancel. Это классическая проблема с Windows Print Spooler. Вот что нужно сделать:
Шаг 1 — Принудительная очистка очереди:
Нажать Win + R, ввести services.msc, нажать Enter
Найти в списке "Print Spooler"
Кликнуть правой кнопкой → "Stop" (остановить)
Не закрывать окно
Шаг 2 — Удалить застрявший файл:
Открыть Проводник и перейти по адресу: C:\Windows\System32\spool\PRINTERS
Удалить все файлы в этой папке (саму папку не трогать)
Шаг 3 — Перезапустить Spooler:
Вернуться в services.msc
Снова кликнуть на "Print Spooler" → "Start"
Шаг 4 — Перезагрузить принтер:
Выключить и включить физически (или вытащить шнур на 30 секунд).
После этого очередь будет пустой и можно печатать новый документ. Главное — при следующей печати выбрать правильный масштаб/размер страницы перед отправкой задания.
