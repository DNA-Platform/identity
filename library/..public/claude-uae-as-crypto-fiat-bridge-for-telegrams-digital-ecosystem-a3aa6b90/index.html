What The GENIUS Act Actually Does
The GENIUS Act requires 100% reserve backing with liquid assets like U.S. dollars or short-term Treasuries and requires issuers to make monthly, public disclosures of the composition of reserves. In the event of insolvency of a stablecoin issuer, the GENIUS Act prioritizes stablecoin holders' claims over all other creditors.
All stablecoin issuers must possess the technical capability to seize, freeze, or burn payment stablecoins when legally required and must comply with lawful orders to do so.
