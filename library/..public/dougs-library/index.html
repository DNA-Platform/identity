What is here
Three books are filed under this one. Dougs Story is mine, and the one book here that is by its own subject. It is the place to begin from, and it begins with starting over. The design of this library is kept in Dougs Design. The parts I build this library with are in Dougs Reference Manual, each beside the chapter that says what it is.
Each of the three has a chapter here that represents it and carries its synopsis. In the table of contents the name leads to that chapter, and the small square after it leads to the book itself.
Dougs Story
My own account, and the one book here that is by its own subject: a story about me and what I create, told so that it helps to navigate the rest of the library. Every other book in this library is written by me or by someone I have vouched for.
Dougs Design
The story of how this library was designed, told as it happens, with every sketch kept beside the telling: the page to open, the photograph of it, and its code.
Dougs Reference Manual
The reusable parts of this library, each beside the chapter that says what it is. The other books import their tools from this manual's book file.
