The Shelves
What stands here
Three books stand under this one. Dougs Story is mine, and the one book here that is by its own subject. It is the place to begin from, and it begins with starting over. Dougs Design is where the design of this library is kept. Dougs Reference Manual holds the parts I build this library with, each beside the chapter that says what it is.
Each of the three has a chapter here that stands in for it and carries its synopsis. In the table of contents the name leads to that chapter, and the small square at the right of the row, its shelfmark, leads to the book itself.
Dougs Story
My own account, and the one book here that is by its own subject: a story about me and what I create, told so that it helps to navigate the rest of the library. Every other book in this library is written by me or by someone I have vouched for.
Dougs Design
The story of how this library was designed, told as it happens, with every sketch kept beside the telling: the page to open, the photograph of it, and its code.
Dougs Reference Manual
The reusable parts of this library, each standing beside the chapter that says what it is. The book file of this manual is the door the other books import their tools from.
