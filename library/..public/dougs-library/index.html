What stands here
Three books stand under this one. Dougs Story is mine, and the one book here that is by its own subject. It is the place to begin from, and it begins with starting over. Dougs Design is where the design of this library is kept. Dougs Reference Manual holds the parts I build this library with, each beside the chapter that says what it is.
