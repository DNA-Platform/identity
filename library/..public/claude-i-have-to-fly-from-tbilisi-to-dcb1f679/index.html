Direct answers first:
On the charge structure — oversize is dimension-triggered, not weight-triggered. Turkish Airlines charges a flat $90 per piece for anything between 158–292 cm linear (L+W+H). Weight is a separate axis: normal checked bag is 23 kg, up to 32 kg with an overweight fee, and 32 kg is a hard ceiling per piece — no amount of money gets you past that. Wood crates are mostly needed for very high value or oversize-beyond-baggage shipments.
