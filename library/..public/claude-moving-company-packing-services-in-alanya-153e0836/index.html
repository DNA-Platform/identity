Yes, there are quite a few local moving companies (nakliyat firmaları) in Alanya that offer packing services as part of their moving packages. Here's what I found:
Local Alanya Companies with Packing Services:
Özgüven Nakliyat (ozguvennakliyat.net) — Offers careful packing (paketleme), expert assessment of your home before the move, and covered truck transport. Serves all 81 provinces of Turkey.
Zertaş Nakliyat (evdenevealanya.com) — Based in Cikcilli Mah., Alanya. They do full packing including bubble wrap for fragiles, furniture disassembly/reassembly, and wardrobe boxes for clothes. Serves Alanya, Gazipaşa, Antalya, and intercity.
Tekin Nakliyat (alanyatekinnakliyat.com.tr) — Founded 2003 in Kestel. Covers all Alanya neighborhoods including Konaklı, Mahmutlar, Avsallar, Kargıcak, etc. Insured, with furniture specialists on staff.
Mahmutlar Nakliyat (listed on alanya-go.com) — Specifically in Mahmutlar. Offers packing, crane/platform services for bulky items, and door-to-door delivery.
Alanya Nakliyat (alanyanakliyat.com, phone: 0533 428 44 59) — Uses bubble wrap, foam-lined boxes for fragiles, and stretch film/blankets for furniture. Offers online quotes via photo/video of your belongings.
Pricing:
Specific packing-only prices aren't listed separately — it's typically bundled into the overall move cost. Based on the listings, local (şehir içi) moves in Alanya start around 1,000 ₺, while intercity moves start around 2,500 ₺. For reference, Alanya to Ankara runs roughly 2,800–4,500 ₺, and Alanya to Istanbul around 2,100–6,000 ₺, depending on volume.
All these companies do on-site assessments (ekspertiz) where they evaluate your belongings and give a custom quote based on volume and distance. The standard approach is to call, send photos/video of your stuff via WhatsApp, or have them come look in person.
Are you thinking about a local move within Alanya, or is this related to a longer-distance move? That would affect which companies and price ranges are most relevant.

Important notes for your situation:

Your best approach would be to WhatsApp one of the top two and explain you just need "sadece paketleme hizmeti" (packing service only) — that they bring boxes, wrapping materials, and pack your belongings.
