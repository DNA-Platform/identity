Short answer: the escrow mechanism exists in Russian law, but the specific condition you want to attach to it is the weak point.
The mechanism. Russia has had a proper escrow framework since 2018 — договор условного депонирования (эскроу), Art. 926.1–926.8 Civil Code — where a notary, bank, or other agent holds money and releases it on a verifiable condition. Notaries do these routinely for real estate; bank эскроу-счета are standard in construction. Conditions dependent on a party's own actions are permitted under Art. 327.1. So "release after X months if condition Y holds, return to depositor if Y fails" is a structure a Moscow notary or lawyer knows how to draft.
The problem. A contract whose explicit subject is paying a parent to not exercise a parental right (the ban under Art. 21 of 114-FZ is his statutory right) is very likely void under Art. 168/169 Civil Code and the Family Code principle that parental rights can't be traded. A notary would likely refuse to certify it in that form, and if Maxim took the money then re-imposed the ban, you'd be asking a Russian court to enforce a contract it would probably strike down.
How this is actually done. The money gets attached to something that is contractable — typically a court-approved мировое соглашение or notarized settlement covering property division, alimony arrears, and порядок общения (Art. 66 SK), with his withdrawal of the ban as one recited step. The property claim you're already weighing is the natural vehicle. Even so, two limits remain: (1) nothing prevents him from filing a new ban the next day, and (2) forfeiture returns your money but doesn't restore the passport window.
Verification is feasible. МВД notifies the other parent when a ban is filed, and Ana can confirm status by request — so the trigger condition can be checked.
The question for Гурина is narrow: can an escrow release tied to a recited ban-withdrawal survive inside a мировое соглашение, and would she draft it.

Non-notarial escrow is explicitly allowed. Under Chapter 47.1 Civil Code (Art. 926.1–926.8, since June 2018), law firms may act as escrow agents on par with banks and notaries; the agent, as an independent third party, verifies that the conditions are met and then transfers the money to the beneficiary. Precedent for this exact condition. I found no published case of money escrowed against ban withdrawal. What this means for your structure:
Release condition = МВД confirmation the ban is withdrawn, then N months without a new filing. Verifiable, since the other parent is notified on filing.

Almost certainly unpublished rather than novel. Three reasons:
Escrow disputes only reach published practice when the agent or a party breaches. A deal where a law firm holds money and releases it on a verified administrative act has no reason to surface — and Russian family settlements are closed hearings, so even court-approved versions aren't indexed.

Family lawyers' forums discuss отступные for "согласие на выезд" routinely; it's usually dressed as погашение задолженности, раздел имущества, or a lump-sum alimony capitalization under Art. 104 SK. Yes, and it's the cleanest wrapper available.
Mechanics. Property division between ex-spouses can be done by notarized соглашение о разделе имущества (Art. 38 SK) or by мировое соглашение in a division suit. Either can allocate unequal shares or a компенсация payment from one spouse to the other. A compensation owed by Ana to Maxim is a perfectly ordinary term — she keeps an asset, he gets money. Nobody questions why.
Where the escrow fits. The compensation is paid through a договор эскроу: deposited on signing, released to Maxim when the agreed conditions occur. The division agreement itself can say only "компенсация уплачивается в порядке, предусмотренном договором эскроу от такого-то числа." The ban conditions live in the escrow contract, which is private between the parties and the agent. A мировое соглашение requires a judge to approve the division terms, but the escrow contract isn't before the court.
Advantages over a bare escrow:
Gives Maxim a legitimate reason to receive money, which his side needs on paper.
Solves your "how does he justify signing" problem — he's settling a property claim, not selling a parental right.
If he already fears a property suit, filing one creates the litigation in which a мировое соглашение naturally arises. You're already considering the claim as leverage; this gives the leverage a landing place.
A court-approved мировое соглашение with ban withdrawal as an undertaking gets you an исполнительный лист if he refuses to file the withdrawal — escrow handles the money side, the writ handles his act.
Constraints to confirm with Гурина:
Whether there's still divisible marital property (or a claim to it) substantial enough for a compensation of the size you intend; a payment wildly out of proportion to the property invites a притворная сделка argument — though again, only if someone sues.
Limitation period for division is three years from when Ana learned her rights were violated, not from the divorce; check the clock.
Whether the judge approving the settlement will ask about the payment schedule.

08:52 UTC, 20 August 2026
мировое соглашение - what is this again?
Claude
08:52 UTC, 20 August 2026
A court-approved settlement. When there's a lawsuit pending, the parties can agree terms and submit them to the judge, who approves them by ruling (определение) and closes the case (Art. 153.8–153.11 CPC).
Two properties make it useful here:
It has the force of a judgment. If Maxim doesn't do what he undertook, Ana gets an исполнительный лист from the same court without a new suit — bailiffs enforce it.
It takes effect immediately on approval, no appeal waiting period.
The judge checks only that the terms don't violate law or third-party rights; she doesn't renegotiate them. Family settlements are heard in closed session, so the terms aren't published.
Contrast with a notarized соглашение: that's a private contract — faster, no lawsuit required, but breach means a new suit to enforce. Мировое соглашение requires an existing case, which is why the property division claim matters: it creates the case.

The structure is two documents:
Мировое соглашение (in court, two parties: Ana and Maxim). Says: Ana keeps X, Maxim receives compensation of Y rubles, payable in the manner set out in the escrow contract of such-and-such date; Maxim undertakes to withdraw the ban by such-and-such date.

The agent's only job is holding money and checking the conditions. Under Art. 926.1 the agent must be independent of both parties; an advocate has an ethical bar against acting as agent for a client's deal anyway. So it's a separate firm, a bank escrow account, or a notary depot — chosen jointly, since Maxim has to trust it as much as you do.
Your lawyers' role is to draft both documents and negotiate the terms. The agent receives a fee and a copy of the release conditions, nothing more.

Short version: there isn't a standalone "escrow firm" market for private individuals in Moscow. Chapter 47.1 escrow agents that actually advertise are securities registrars like ВТБ Регистратор and Reestr, built for share deals. For a cash deposit between two people, the realistic agents are:
Bank эскроу-счёт (Sber, ВТБ, Альфа, Т-Банк). Cheapest and most neutral; Maxim can't object to Sber. Downside: banks run them mostly for real estate, with document-based release conditions, and a branch may refuse a bespoke "МВД confirmation plus N months" trigger. Worth asking anyway.

One useful detail: an escrow contract must be notarized except where non-cash money or uncertificated securities are deposited, so a bank-transfer deposit needs no notary regardless of agent, and the term is capped at five years.

The part the escrow can't do: it can't make the passport issue. It only keeps the window open. So the sequencing matters — Russian passports can be renewed well before expiry, so Ana applies the day the withdrawal is confirmed, and the term is long enough to cover consular processing (Tbilisi Interests Section can take up to three months). Twelve months covers that comfortably.
One consequence to be clear on: this is ungameable against a filed ban. It does nothing if he obstructs through some other channel. For passport renewal of a minor, the ban is the only mechanism he has, which is why the design works.
