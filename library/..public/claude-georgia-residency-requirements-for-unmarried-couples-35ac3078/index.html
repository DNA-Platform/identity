One person signs the lease
The landlord provides a notarized consent letter allowing the other person(s) to register at that address
This is standard practice in Georgia and landlords familiar with foreigners are used to it. The children would be registered under Ana's application.

Get the TRP applications moving as fast as possible. You register a Georgian LLC, which can be a subsidiary of your US company or simply a new entity you own. The key requirements for a work-based TRP through a company:
The company must have an annual turnover of 50,000 GEL (~$19,000) per each foreigner employed
The founders and director of the company may NOT be citizens of Georgia, and persons applying for the residence permit may be directors or founders but must also work in the company under an employment contract

Same 50,000 GEL turnover requirement per person, but lighter structure. You need IE status, VAT registration, and documentation of the turnover for the 12 months preceding the application. Option 3: New IT residence permit (if applicable)
As of September 1, 2025, Georgia offers a special IT residence permit valid for three years, renewable up to twelve years, open to IT employees, small business owners in IT, and executives of international IT companies, along with their family members. Requirements are at least two years of IT experience and annual income of at least $25,000. If your US company does anything IT-adjacent, this could be the fastest and most favorable pathway — three-year permit out of the gate rather than 6-12 months, and it explicitly covers family members.
Critical timing consideration: March 1, 2026 changes
Starting March 1, 2026, every foreigner who intends to work, operate a business, or even provide remote services for a Georgian company must obtain the Right to Engage in Labor Activity. This authorization becomes the gateway to legal employment — without the work right, any labor or entrepreneurial activity will be unlawful. Fines of 2,000 GEL per violation. Those already in Georgia must apply within 10 days for a labor residence permit after receiving work authorization.
This means the window to get established under the current (simpler) rules is closing fast. Foreigners registered in the labor migrant system by March 1, 2026 will have until January 1, 2027 to obtain the new work authorization and correct residence permit. That's a meaningful grace period if you get registered before the cutoff.

But here's the problem. The turnover proof has to come through Georgian systems. Turnover can be proven by: a VAT turnover statement issued by the Revenue Service, a bank statement of your Georgian business bank account in GEL, or a licensed auditor verifying turnover — though recent practice shows that auditors' reports are getting rejected.
So even if your US company has done $100K in the last year, the Georgian Revenue Service doesn't care about US bank statements. They want to see money flowing through a Georgian bank account or Georgian tax filings. A brand new branch registered tomorrow has zero history in the Georgian system regardless of how much the parent has earned.
The real question is whether a branch structure lets an auditor certify the parent company's existing turnover as the branch's turnover, since they're legally the same entity. Before auditor reports started getting rejected, this was apparently a viable path. Now it's shaky.
This is genuinely a question for your legal counsel — specifically, someone who deals with the Public Service Hall regularly and knows what's being accepted in practice right now, not just what the statute says. The answer may differ month to month as the 2026 reforms roll in.
