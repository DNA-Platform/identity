The main variable is how you funded the purchase:
ACH bank transfer (most common): Coinbase enforces a 7–10 day hold on crypto withdrawals when funded via ACH, though it could be longer depending on account history, payment activity, and transaction history. Some users report up to 18 days on large purchases.
Wire transfer: Wire transfers generally have holding periods of 3–5 days, significantly shorter than ACH.
Debit card: Shortest holds — sometimes immediate for accounts with good history — but fees are high and purchase limits are low, making it impractical for large amounts.
At $10K via ACH, expect 7–10 business days as the baseline. A few complicating factors:
Large deposits and irregular account activity can trigger Coinbase's anti-fraud systems for manual review, which can extend the hold further.
Account verification level matters — a fully verified account tends to get shorter holds.
The BTC is yours and tradeable on-platform immediately. The restriction is only on sending it to an external wallet. If you need it out faster, wire funding on future purchases is the cleaner approach.
