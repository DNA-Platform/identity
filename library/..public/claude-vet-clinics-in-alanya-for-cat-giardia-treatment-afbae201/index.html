Perfect! I found the key distinction:
CRITICAL TERMINOLOGY:
In Turkey, there's a legal difference:
Hayvan Hastanesi (Animal Hospital) = Full facility with inpatient/hospitalization ("yatarak tedavi"), ICU, surgery, diagnostics
Veteriner Kliniği/Poliklinik = Outpatient clinic only ("ayakta tedavi")
Muayenehane = Basic examination office
You need a "Hayvan Hastanesi" - NOT just a "klinik"!
