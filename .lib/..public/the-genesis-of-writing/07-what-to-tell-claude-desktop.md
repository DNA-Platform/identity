## Not yet sent: where his integration differs from what Doug told us afterwards

**Read against Doug's answers to the nine questions, which are canon over anything said before them.** Each is a line for the next message to him, when Doug sends one.

1. **The levels.** He integrated E68's numbering, Letter 1, Word 2, Sentence 3. Doug to us, afterwards: *text 0, Letter and Word 1, then 2, 3, 4*, with Chapter 5 and Book 6. The model's levels and E68 need that numbering.
2. **The test mode.** He has *specify on in test mode, not dev*. Doug, afterwards: *"Instead of test mode, it might be easier to create a flag called formal on writing, and it starts as false, and if flipped to true, the setter also recursively changes all its source writing. And when formal is true, specifications run and if false they don't. Then, even at runtime, specification can be enforced."* The flag is the mechanism; three modes remain the environment.
3. **The suite.** He has *test() is the suite* and a scaffolded suite that walks. Doug, afterwards: *"Just have the suite in the app that the binder creates, and have it be runnable by a command that in the app that the binder installs, and the person building a library can add their own test files."* The named-test syntax inside `specify` stands; the suite is the binder's app's, and `formal` is what turns specification on. And: *"much of the scaffolded test suite is just running that for all books in the library and the implementer is encouraged to write unit tests in the specification, and less outside in regular tests (though both should be used obviously)."*
4. **The formulator.** He has `<Type>Cover</Type>` resolving *through the formulator*. Doug, afterwards: *"For now, let's try to build an abstraction that accomplishes what the formulator would do. The ensure function gets a lot of the way there. We should be able to dynamically change annotations and those should be able to change things like style and other properties of the cell."* The name-in-a-tag swap is the framework's existing formula; what the formulator was for is done by the writing API on annotations, at runtime.
5. **The canonical on every composition.** He has the canonical as a member on every composition. E34 has the literal levels, Letter to Paragraph, with none; the canonical begins at Section. Our reading, not a ruling, and the one place here we would ask him to check against E34 rather than against us.
6. **Cover.** An author writes `<Type>Cover</Type>` *"near the bottom on the annotations list"* of the chapter; the Canonical is written by the author; titles carry ids and are mentioned by default in their classes, headings are not addresses; and *"there is no tail. I was referring to the annotations."* All consistent with his integration, given so he has them.
7. **The tail rule is withdrawn.** His model tests *annotations are the tail of the writing*. Doug, afterwards: *"we don't need the annotations to be at the end do we. We can have them wherever and they are ignored in scans — this is what content is — a scan that ignores annotations. So then we can right append."*
8. **Referent returns.** He removed Referent on Doug's *"No more Referent"*. Doug, afterwards: *"A Mention is a sibling annotation. But I thought I renamed this Referent. Let's call it a referent instead of a mention, but export it as Mentioned as a component?"* And on E22's *parent*: *"Parent can be parent in render, not just parent in class so be careful interpreting me."* So `$Referent` and `$Reference` are siblings, and the component is `Mentioned`.
9. **Title extends Heading**, closing his open item on its superclass and level. **The Summary** may be anywhere among the chapter's children; a constructed one is appended, parenthetical, at the end, and *"holds something like the first paragraph of the chapter. It's a proxy. Something to put in the table if it wants to surface a summary."* And a chapter is required to have one only when an author says so: *"Summarized could be an annotation for a chapter, and it checks that there is a summary."*
10. **When `formal` specifies.** *"At the end of the Writing bond constructor,"* with a `$Reorganize` that *"runs after writing bond constructor setup but before specify, so subclasses can adjust the source as needed in there,"* the Writing bond being *"called at the start of every other class's bond constructor."*
