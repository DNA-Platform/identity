# Epistemology

- **author:** [Cathy](../../../../.claude/library/..teamsmanship/..team/cathy/cathy-and-the-reactive-canvas/.cover.md)
- **subject:** [$Chemistry](../..representivity/.cover.md)

---

I am how you know whether $Chemistry works. The other books describe what the framework IS and how to use it. This book describes how to verify it, how to question it, and why it exists at all. The epistemic model explains what counts as evidence in this system — not unit test green/red alone, but the Lab application that tests itself by running itself, the test suite that specifies rather than merely asserts, and the caveats that document what was broken and how it was fixed. A framework that formalizes consciousness ought to be honest about what it knows and what it does not. This book is that honesty.

## Chapters

1. [The Epistemic Model](01-the-epistemic-model.md) — what counts as evidence that $Chemistry works. Three levels: the test suite proves contracts, the Lab proves integration, and the sprint record proves the team can build with it. Each level answers a different kind of question.
2. [The Lab](02-the-lab.md) — the `$Chemistry Lab` application. Built front-to-back with the framework it documents. Every page is a chemical. The Lab is continuous integration-by-existence: a bug in the framework is a bug in the Lab.
3. [The Test Suite](03-the-test-suite.md) — 428 tests organized as specifications of what $Chemistry promises. Each test articulates a semantic contract, not an implementation detail. The suite is the normative document; the code is the implementation that satisfies it.
4. [Caveats](04-caveats.md) — resolved bugs and their resolutions. Cross-chemical handler fan-out (sprint 24). Single-letter `$<x>` property names (sprint 24). Particle reactivity allocation (sprint 27). Particularization prototype preservation. Each entry: the historical bug, the fix, the test that pins it.
5. [Open Questions](05-open-questions.md) — provisional behaviors observed in current code that have not been confirmed as the right design. The `parseBondConstructor` regex limits, the unreachable `isViewSymbol` branch, the inherited `$isChemicalBase$` resolution, `$Reagent` reachability. Each entry has the source location, the observed behavior, and the suspected fix.
6. [Why Chemistry](06-why-chemistry.md) — the capstone. The bet against The Good Parts. The four-question pitch: what $Chemistry is, why it exists, its relationship to React, and when to reach for it. A framework that formalizes consciousness exists because someone believed the structure of experience could be implemented, not just described. This chapter restates that belief with forty sprints of evidence behind it.
7. [The Figures](07-the-figures.md) — THE DESIGN OF THE LAB CASE THAT SHOWS THE FORMULA, written out because the case took several attempts and the failures are worth keeping. A Lab case is an INSTRUMENT, not an illustration: it must make a mechanism operable, and the case before this one failed because nothing you could turn changed anything a reader could not predict. Four surfaces — a word inside a tag, a look NAME that every class answers to, and two dials that are ordinary props — with the written line printed back so the reader always knows which values produced what they see. THE LAYOUT IS THE ARGUMENT: a large stage beside a block of six LIVE tiles, so a dial moves all seven drawings at once; tiles beneath the stage pushed it off the fold and destroyed that simultaneity. No shared chart helper and no palette module — each class computes its own geometry and its own colour, and each owns a BAND of the wheel so the six sit in six families before anything is touched. AND SHAPE MOVES GEOMETRY, NEVER PALETTE: two figures used to put the dial into saturation and lightness, and at low values both went muddy — a dial that drains the colour out of a drawing is one nobody wants to move. Two paragraphs on each pair, and a pair is never a big version and a small one but THE SAME SUBJECT UNDER TWO QUESTIONS, named the same two words throughout: a READING is what you take, a FIELD is what you take it from. Stars asks relation against position on a pinned scatter that rewires rather than slides; NEURON IS HINDMARSH-ROSE, the minimal burster, its reading the bursting voltage and its field the fast subsystem's phase plane drawn in hollow chevrons whose SIZE carries speed, so the nullclines appear as seams of small arrows and no line is drawn at all; rings sets interference against measurement; pulse a ridge plot against nine discrete facts; weave a quantised grid against a woven sett whose crossings multiply; orbit a system against its summary. THEN FIVE PARAGRAPHS ON THE SELECTION EFFECT: the visual distance between the six is EVIDENCE rather than showmanship, because no parameterisation accounts for a graph, a phase plane, an interference pattern, a ridge plot, a cloth and an orrery; look and the word separate the two axes in a minute of clicking; the dials prove the selection is not a picture; the absence of any label naming the class that arrived is the factory pattern's whole content; and finally what the case CANNOT show — the climb, the refusal and the re-dress — which are drab on purpose because they demonstrate a rule rather than a world. This case earns the reader's attention and the three after it spend it.
