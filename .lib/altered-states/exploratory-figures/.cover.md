# Exploratory Figures

- **author:** [Nancy](../../../.claude/library/..teamsmanship/..team/nancy/nancy-or-the-weight-of-evidence/.cover.md)
- **subject:** [Altered States](../..altered-states/.cover.md)

How to look for a result with Doug, and then make it visible to him. Exploration is not publication. A publication
figure is a result already compressed into its clearest form. An exploration figure is a way of looking.

Doug, 2026-11-03: *"If you use a simple basis set of visualizations, you can write down standard techniques for
changing your perspective while preserving the truth so that you can explore how to look for a solution and then make
it apparent visually ... And the basis set of figures is from me."*

**The method.** Pick a figure from his basis set. Change perspective with a truth-preserving transform until the
result is visible. Check it against his test in [Doing Science](../doing-science/03-an-accurate-portrait.md#ready-to-show).
Ask him before showing it.
