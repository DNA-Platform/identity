Read on 2026-11-03. "Read" means the text itself; "second-hand" means through other sources that cite it. The

## Figures and perception

- **Tukey, *Exploratory Data Analysis*** (Addison-Wesley 1977; read in the 1970 preliminary edition, chapters 3, 7-9).
  Residuals; re-expression and the ladder of powers.
- **Cleveland and McGill 1984**, "Graphical perception", *JASA* 79:531-554 (read, scanned pages); and 1986, *Int J
  Man-Machine Studies* 25:491-500 (read). The ranking of encodings; superposed curves hide their difference.
- **Heer and Agrawala 2006**, "Multi-scale banking to 45°", *IEEE TVCG* 12(5) (read). Aspect ratio.
- **Heer and Bostock 2010**, "Crowdsourcing graphical perception", *CHI 2010* (read). The ranking replicated.
- **Gelman 2004**, "Exploratory data analysis for complex models", *JCGS* 13:755-779 (read, sections 1-3). The graph as
  a test against a reference.
- **Wickham 2010**, "A layered grammar of graphics", *JCGS* 19:3-28 (read). Common scales across panels.
- **Wilke 2019**, *Fundamentals of Data Visualization* (read, eight chapters). Bin widths, blended colours, log scales.
- **Rougier, Droettboom and Bourne 2014**, "Ten simple rules for better figures", *PLoS Comput Biol* 10:e1003833
  (read).
- **Weissgerber et al. 2015**, "Beyond bar and line graphs", *PLoS Biol* 13:e1002128 (read). Show pairs.
- **L'Yi, Jo and Seo 2021**, "Comparative layouts revisited", *IEEE TVCG* 27:1525-1535 (read, arXiv version).
  Superimpose for local comparison.
- **Tufte 1983**, *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information* (second-hand).

## Signal processing

- **Mitra and Pesaran 1999**, "Analysis of dynamic brain imaging data", *Biophys J* 76:691-708 (read, arXiv
  version). Multitaper spectra, error bars.
- **Pesaran 2008**, "Spectral analysis for neural signals", SfN short course (read). Log power; spectrogram
  conventions.
