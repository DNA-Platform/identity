The notebook of scientific explorations on this project: one chapter a question, written while the exploring is done,
so that an exploration is never held only in a conversation.

Doug, 2026-11-19: *"Why don't you explore to find the pattern tiling fixed point a little more deeply, while taking
notes. Make a library book in .lib for research notes, and keep track of your scientific exploratiosn there. Thsi is
important."*

**How to use it.** Before exploring anything, open the chapter for the question, or start one. Read its first
section, *Where it stands*, and continue from there. After any break in context this is where an exploration resumes.

**How a chapter is kept.**
- It opens with the question in his words, dated, and my reading of it, marked as mine until he confirms it.
- *Where it stands* comes next: what is established, what is not, what is next. This section is edited, never
  appended, so it is always true now.
- Then the entries, in the order they were made. An entry is dated and says: what was asked, what was done (the
  code, the run, the files, so it can be done again), what was seen (the numbers, and the figure by its path), what
  it rules out and what it does not, and what it leads to. An entry is written when the thing is done, not later.
  A wrong turn is an entry too.
- A number is given with what it was measured on (which neurons, how many, which step size). An example is marked
  as an example.

**What goes elsewhere.** What was built in a sprint, and where the work stands, is in
[Projection](../projection/.cover.md). A settled answer goes to [The Ledger](../the-ledger/.cover.md). Theory worked
out in mathematics is in `library/theory/`. What Doug teaches about doing science is in
[Doing Science](../doing-science/.cover.md). This book is the exploring itself.
