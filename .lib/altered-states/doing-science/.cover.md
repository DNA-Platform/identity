What Doug teaches about doing analysis, collected as he teaches it. Doug, 2026-11-03: *"I want you to go to your
library, .lib, research how it works, make a new book about science, and take notes on everything I am telling you
about doing analysis. I need you to learn a lot from me."* He is a computational neuroscientist and the scientist on
this project; I am his research assistant. Every lesson here is in his words, dated, with the mistake of mine that
drew it and the practice that replaces it. The book is edited, not appended: when a new correction sharpens an old
one, the old one is rewritten. Read it before building any analysis or figure for him, and add to it in the same turn
he teaches something. [The Altered Cortex](../the-altered-cortex/.cover.md) holds what the experiment is;
[Solutions](../solutions/.cover.md) holds defects; [The Exploration Report](../the-exploration-report/.cover.md) holds
the craft of one report. This book holds how to work with Doug on the science.

**The short form.** He is the scientist; do what he asks and nothing he did not. Look at the data, see the result,
then refine the analysis and the figure until it is clear to him. A figure is read by a person who cannot ask you
questions: one quantity on each axis with its units and its computation, zoomed to the effect, data that looks like
data. Say what a term means the first time it appears. Name the reference behind every judgement. Call a null null.
His time is the scarce thing.

## Chapters

1. [The scientist and the assistant](01-the-scientist-and-the-assistant.md) — who decides what. He asks; I do
   exactly that, fast, and bring him the decisions that are his. No exploration unless he asks for it; no
   innovation on a direct instruction; ask far more than feels necessary; published methods and the lab's own
   code, never invented ones; the question is how DOI acts, never whether.
2. [Look first](02-look-first.md) — the order of work. Look at the data, see a result, then refine the analysis
   and the figure until it is clear to him. The simplest estimator first; check the instrument before the finding;
   convict the measurement, not reality; measure what survives infinite data; controls only if they change the
   decision.
3. [A figure a person can read](03-a-figure-a-person-can-read.md) — what he asked for, drawn so he can read it
   alone. Pairs of histograms when he asks for them; no stacked plots; an axis is a quantity with units and states
   its computation; zoom to the effect; data must look like data; one idea made visible, not a plate.
4. [Words for the reader](04-words-for-the-reader.md) — define every term on first use (the test images); his
   lexicon or the computation spelled out; no tour, no lecture, no reassurance; a report to the lab motivates.
5. [Reading a result](05-reading-a-result.md) — name the reference or report bare; call a null null; read each
   recording on its own terms; replication is shape; behaviour is evidence; sameness is the control; parity
   between paired arms; an uneven test is said to be uneven; don't project conservatism.
6. [Case: the metameric-moments deck, 2026-11-03](06-case-the-metameric-moments-deck.md) — one afternoon in
   which he asked for pairs of histograms and got stacked bars, scatters and plates first: each message, what it
   corrected, and what the deck became.
