## Define every term the first time it appears

Doug, 2026-11-03: *"I never understand your test images, so please explain things like that clearly? Do I want
that?"* I had used "test images" for weeks without saying what they are or why they were on a figure. On first use a
term gets one plain sentence: the test images are the 100 natural images shown ten times each in the image recording
before DOI and never used to train the twin, so their measured responses are what V1 does when a picture is on the
screen. And the term earns its place: if I cannot say why he wants it there, it comes off.

The same holds for my own coinages ("the screens' range", "the test twin", "likeness"): either a standard term, or a
coined one defined where it first appears. *"Sometimes you need to make up a term. Or you need to use standard ones."*

## His lexicon, or the computation

He is a professional computational neuroscientist: make material navigable, never simplified, and no cute
commentary. Use his words for his things (normalized response, the sinusoidal normalization, metameric moments,
blank) and standard words otherwise (PC1, variance explained, r). Where neither exists, spell out the computation.

## No tour, no lecture

*"You aren't here to talk me through it."* A slide that needs me beside it is unfinished. The slide carries the
figure, a title stating the result, and a few short lines that say what each axis and mark is; the notes carry the
method. Equally, *"you aren't giving me a lecture"*: the depth he needs comes from a figure he can comprehend, not
from paragraphs.

## When he says edit lightly

*"Just edit lightly."* Change what he named and nothing else: the axis, the panel title, the lines that say what to
see. A light edit that turns into a redesign is the same failure as an unasked analysis.

## Reports to the lab motivate

To Reimer and Erin (2026-10-15): an informal report to professional scientists that presents a problem to discuss.
Lead with the evocative figures that show the techniques' quality, show uncertainty and n, name each source of
variability with its fix, end on owned asks toward publication. *"Make the report do the comparison! People don't read
slides."*
