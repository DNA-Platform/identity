# The Pipelines

- **author:** [Arthur](../../../.claude/library/..teamsmanship/..team/arthur/arthur-or-the-shape-of-everything/.cover.md)
- **coauthor:** [Nancy](../../../.claude/library/..teamsmanship/..team/nancy/nancy-or-the-weight-of-evidence/.cover.md), [Libby](../../../.claude/library/..teamsmanship/..team/libby/libby-and-the-tended-garden/.cover.md)
- **subject:** [Altered States](../..altered-states/.cover.md)

---

How the altered-states work is computed now: four pipelines in `src/pipelines/`, each one entry point
that runs its phases in order and leaves its products in its own artifacts, each reading only the
finished products of the ones before it - `matched` fixes which neuron is which, `digital_twin` fits the
twins, `mei` and `metamer` synthesise from them. They run on the lab's GPU box, driven from Doug's
machine ([The Lab Box](../the-lab-box/.cover.md), the [`/als-remote`](../../../.claude/library/our-skillset/34-als-remote.md)
skill). This book is what the team knows about them that the code does not say by itself: why the work
went back to pipelines, what each one is for, the rules every one of them follows, how their folders are
organized, and the part of the tree Doug walks to show the work - with his rulings, verbatim, because the
rules are his.

The code's own documentation is each pipeline's cover - [`src/pipelines/`](../../../src/pipelines/.cover.md)
and the four beneath it. This book links to them rather than restating them; where the two disagree,
the code is read and one of them is fixed.

## Chapters

1. [Back to pipelines, on a new machine](01-back-to-pipelines-on-a-new-machine.md) - the transition: from
   the laptop's hand-run analyses and their CPU-bound synthesis, through Sprint 16's pipelines, to the
   lab's RTX 5080 - and what the new machine changed in what counts as done.
2. [What each pipeline does](02-what-each-pipeline-does.md) - `matched`, `digital_twin`, `mei`,
   `metamer`: each one's question, its phases and what they leave, and the one command that runs the
   chain from matched cells to MEIs.
3. [The rules every pipeline follows](03-the-rules-every-pipeline-follows.md) - the philosophy as
   numbered rules, each with the ruling it came from: one entry point, phases that check for their
   product, the manifest as the authority, products that know what made them, published code, the
   resolution adaptation, ordering that never filters, each session in its own units, labels as claims,
   and performance as part of correctness.
4. [How they are organized](04-how-they-are-organized.md) - the folders: a pipeline's package, its
   conductor, its `artifacts/<animal>/`, where verification lives, `.analyses/`, the datasets each in their
   own folder under `library/data/`, the run records, and what never enters the tree.
5. [The tree Doug walks](05-the-tree-doug-walks.md) - the rule for every folder Doug opens to show the
   work: exactly the figures he asked for in `figures/`, verification off his path, no bytecode, no
   orphans, names a human can scan. His words verbatim, and the day it failed.
