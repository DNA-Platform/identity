## The keyword vocabulary

Every chapter carries a `keywords:` line drawn from this list, so the book is greppable without being
opened. The vocabulary grows **here, on the cover**, only when a genuinely new class appears — a
keyword invented per chapter helps nobody.

**Surfaces:** `twin` · `analysis` · `pipeline` · `library` · `data` · `deliverable`

**Mechanism classes:** `wrong-instrument` — the measurement was made with something other than the
thing under study · `unsurfaced-substitution` — a load-bearing choice was made without being put to
anyone · `circular-gate` — a check scored against the quantity it was derived from ·
`broken-null` — the control was miscomputed, so the comparison had no floor · `stale-artifact` — a
result outlived the code that made it · `lost-work` — a long run was destroyed before it saved ·
`overwritten-parameter` — a setting was applied and then silently reset by something downstream,
so the knob was never connected to the result · `assumed-units` — a library's Fourier convention or a
coordinate conversion was assumed rather than read or measured, so an axis was wrong while every
number on it stayed plausible

## Chapters

1. [The decoder that replaced the twin](01-the-decoder-that-replaced-the-twin.md) — **an entire day of percept figures, spectra, dimensionality and hallucination statistics, in a project called the perceptual twin, produced by a `sklearn` ridge regression that never touched the network** — the twin was loaded, used to measure receptive fields, and never inverted; the substitution was made for speed on the first analysis, then inherited silently by every analysis after it. Two controls convicted the instrument mid-day (68% of the 'structure' reproduced by white noise through the same decoder) and were read as findings rather than as reasons to stop.
2. [The seed that was overwritten](02-the-seed-that-was-overwritten.md) — **five checkpoints that were the same network** — torch.manual_seed ran before condition_loaders, whose static_loaders(seed=1) resets the global RNG, so every run initialised from seed 1 whatever was passed. Different runtimes, hashes and epoch-0 losses; identical weights at max|diff| 0.0
3. [The frequency axis that was assumed](03-the-frequency-axis-that-was-assumed.md) — **a cortical spectrum plotted on an axis that was wrong by 2 pi and then by a further 4 to 15 times** — powerbox's `get_power` returns angular wavenumber (its defaults are `a=1, b=1`) and was read as cycles; the microns-per-pixel magnification was obtained by matching the cells' 614 um extent to the coverage mask's 31-pixel bounding box, when the lab's own `validation.retinotopy_map` on the model-free RF measures 80.9 and 291.1 um per pixel, anisotropic 3.6x; and the estimator compared against figure 03 was a radial average where 03 is a ky-marginal. White noise through the same route reproduced its spectrum, real-to-noise 1.0 across the band. Corrected, the arithmetic gives the wall: the band of interest is 474-1088 um per cycle of cortex and the imaged patch is 614 um wide, so one cycle does not fit at the coarse end.
