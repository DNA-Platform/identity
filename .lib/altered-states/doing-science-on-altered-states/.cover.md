## Each recording has its meaning

*"I think you'll find the meaning of different recordings pretty intuitive and completely different"*; and of pre-DOI
spontaneous, *"A mouse is staring at a blank screen for 40 minutes while walking. They are going to stop paying
attention. Jake named the protocol spontaneous activity not emptiness-evoked activity"* (2026-11-20).

- Pre-DOI spontaneous: a blank grey screen. The protocol is named spontaneous, not emptiness-evoked, and the mouse may
  stop paying attention, so it need not be a pure featureless reference. What it contains is measured, never assumed.
- The image recordings, before and after DOI: a mouse looking at natural images, each sample with its own literal.
- The stimulus pauses, about every 10.6 minutes of each image recording: a mouse looking at a blank screen. They are
  in the scans' full traces, not in our exports.
- Post-DOI spontaneous: the question.

## A representation is defined by the twin

*"A representation is objective in the context of a twin. The definiton of a complex number is not according to
anyones rules. You folow a definition in a book or paper. The twin is published."* (2026-11-19)

- The twin takes an image to neural activity. That neural activity is the representation of the image, and the literal
  is that activity taken as the objective representation of the stimulus. A pattern literal is the neural activity
  the twin gives a pattern.
- *"to create an image that doesn't correspond to an activity pattern is not a representaion made by the twin."* An
  image made by using the twin as a stamp, on an arrangement of my choosing, is a drawing made with the twin (*"When
  you use my code to draw mickey mouse with the MEI, will you be confused about whether we should refer to it
  alongside actual representations in this paper?"*). A pattern has to be endogenous: its shape and frequencies do
  not change with the grid, the spacing or the order of processing.
- Nothing can beat passing the image through the twin: for one image the twin gives one activity, and by the twin
  that activity fits the image with no error.

## Justified on natural scenes, and the work is to generalize

*"Oy! Yes! Justified on natural scenes! We want generalization based on thsi... Patterns might be essential for
representation... and we know hallucinations are patterned."* (2026-11-19)

- That the twin was trained on natural scenes is where its activity is justified. Whether it generalizes to something
  else is a question to test, never a reason to dismiss. The test: pass the image through the twin, make the metamer
  of that activity, and see whether the image comes back, beside natural images (it does) and white noise (it does
  not). *"We can run white noise through thetwin or bizarre unfathomable mathematical patterns. These aren't normal
  and unless we can justify the activity given ... then it's hard to say we have it."*
- A uniformity assumption is mine until shown: a neuron's type at every spot of the twin's map is not a neuron the
  recording has at every location.

## What the twin is, which is not news

- It represents only within the recorded neurons' receptive fields (*"it pains me that you dnn't know this and think
  you are finding some great fact"*).
- It has no behaviour and no eye position: the animal's behaviour was too erratic and the model would not converge
  with it; mice do not saccade as we do (*"crist this was our oldest result"*). Never an open question.

## The twin is its five seeds, combined

*"We do not interpret things as each core is different. We combine them. If you believe the twins represent different
things and not just resolving one thing, then you believe you have evidence that contradicts what the labs have
published."* And: *"Remember I store 5 core data. Unless you prove cores don't matter ... we have to assume they do."*
(2026-11-05)

- Every result uses the whole five-seed twin. One seed is a different instrument: a run on it is a test without its
  control, because a difference may be the process not replicated rather than the representation.
- Every result uses every cell the question allows. A subset the question forces is said first, loudly.

## A screen off is not darkness

*"Not a human. You think a screen being off means you are in the dark!!!"* (2026-11-05) A mouse is not a human, and a
screen being off is not the animal in the dark. "Off" does not make black the blank.
