## The twin is its five seeds, combined

*"We do not interpret things as each core is different. We combine them. If you believe the twins represent different
things and not just resolving one thing, then you believe you have evidence that contradicts what the labs have
published."* And: *"Remember I store 5 core data. Unless you prove cores don't matter ... we have to assume they do."*
(2026-11-05)

- Every result uses the whole five-seed twin. One seed is a different instrument: a run on it is a test without its
  control, because a difference may be the process not replicated rather than the representation.
- Every result uses every cell the question allows. A subset the question forces is said first, loudly.

## A screen off is not darkness

*"Not a human. You think a screen being off means you are in the dark!!!"* (2026-11-05) A mouse is not a human, and a
screen being off is not the animal in the dark. "Off" does not make black the blank.
