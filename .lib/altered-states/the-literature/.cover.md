The branch's record of the papers we stand on — what *we* learned from each, and what that learning informs. The papers themselves are full books in [`library/papers/`](../../papers/digital-twins-tolias-2022/.cover.md), each with its own synopsis, figures, and commentary; this catalogue does **not** duplicate them. Each chapter points to a paper's book and adds the one thing the paper book cannot hold: *our* reading of it — what we took, which of our code or reports it touched, and what is still pending. The purpose is memory: next time we open one of these papers, this is where we find what we already know about it and what it informed, so we do not relearn it from scratch.

## Chapters

1. [Digital Twins of Visual Cortex (Cobos / Tolias 2022)](01-digital-twins-tolias-2022.md) — the encoding-model twin and the reconstruction / MEI display recipe; what informs Sprint 4 and the twin-MEI build
2. [Functional Connectomics (Tolias 2024)](02-functional-connectomics-tolias-2024.md) — uses the twin's feature-weights as scalar similarities and shows no MEIs or reconstructed images; informs methodology, not image display
3. [Sequence Filtering (Histed 2025)](03-sequence-filtering-histed-2025.md) — recurrent V1 sequence filtering via holographic stimulation; a conceptual neighbour, not yet load-bearing
5. [Scale Mixtures of Gaussians (Wainwright & Simoncelli 1999)](../../papers/wainwright-simoncelli-scale-mixtures-1999/.cover.md) — the natural-image prior for wavelet/Gabor coefficients: heavy tails and coupled variances from one hidden scale multiplier. Supplies the density Garon et al. leave uniform, and the substitution that makes our high-dimensional latent well posed
4. [Error-in-Variables Regression (Garon, Keeley & Williams 2026)](04-garon-error-in-variables-2026.md) — the coupling between what is presented and what is represented, made a fitted parameter κ; the rival explanation for our resolution finding, and the technique Sprint 12 adapts by supplying the twin as its tuning function
