The experiment, made analyzable. This book is the working record of the altered-states dataset and the plan to interrogate it: two-photon calcium imaging of the same mouse V1 population recorded twice — once before and once after the animal received DOI, a 5-HT2A agonist psychedelic — while the same natural images were shown both times. It documents what was actually recorded (verified against the files, not the walkthrough's prose), turns the project's animating word — *hallucination* — into a numbered set of falsifiable hypotheses each paired with the observation that would disprove it and the control it is measured against, and lays out an analysis plan grounded in the digital-twin encoding model and gradient inversion. Its discipline is the branch's discipline: a model that fits is not a mechanism, and what can be decoded is not what the animal sees. Read it before running anything; it is the context the analysis resumes from. It complements [Sprint 1](../projection/01-sprint-1--does-the-mouse-hallucinate.md), which states the goal and the tasks; this book holds the dataset, the hypotheses, and the method.

## Chapters

This book has **three kinds of chapter**, and the kind tells you how to read it and how often it changes. The
kinds are not decoration: mixing them is what let a live count rot inside a timeless contract, and what buried
the reading list under sixty lines of state.

### Specification — *what a turn must do.* Read every turn; it changes rarely.

Carries a [`specification:` label](../../../.claude/library/bookkeeping/11-on-specifications.md#the-specification-label)
and a [validator resource](../../../.claude/library/bookkeeping/02-on-chapters.md#resources) beside it, because a
specification without a validator is only a convention.

0. [The turn](00-the-turn.md) · `specification: Turn` · resource: [`00-the-turn--check.py`](00-the-turn--check.py) — **read this first, every turn.** The recursive reading list as a link graph (depth 0 the book → 1 the three contracts → 2 the map → 3 the one code file that owns it), the code-reading policy that stops rivals being born, the three symptoms that prove a turn happened, and the rule that this book is maintained by **editing, never appending**. Its validator walks the same graph and fails on drift.

### Map — *what owns what.* Read before touching code; written to stay true as the project grows.

Concepts and owners, never a count. Everything dated or measured was moved out of here on purpose — a synopsis
carrying [state snapshots](../../../.claude/library/bookkeeping/09-on-synopsis.md#write-for-evolution) generates
consistency debt every time the library grows.

3. [The analysis, as actually built](03-the-analysis-plan.md) — **the map: open the file, do not guess.** The **conventions** (where code lives, where outputs go, the `-bh`/no-suffix rule, bins from the data) and the **DRY-homes table** naming the one owner of every piece of logic; the 2×2 of twins on the 749 matched cells and the three instruments (MEIs, population metamers, the twin-independent Cobos Fig-2 decoder); why the readout is **free-μ** and how a **circular gate** (μy-R² scored against the very cortical coordinates μ was fit from) chose the wrong readout and hid a collapsed elevation range; and **[the lessons that cost the most](03-the-analysis-plan.md#the-lessons-that-cost-the-most)** — every expensive mistake compressed to one line and its reason, because a lesson stops being news the turn after it is learned.

### Working state — *what is true right now.* Read every turn; rewritten every turn.

The **only** volatile chapter, and deliberately so: it is supposed to go stale between turns. Keeping the dated
scratchpad here is what lets every other layer stay timeless.

5. [The working state](05-the-working-state.md) — the active-code list (what is live, what is a rival, what not to touch), which studies currently import, what is still open in dependency order, and the **audit trail** — what was read, what was measured, and each thing the book itself got wrong. Counts are absent by design: they live in `results/_progress.txt`, their one home.

### Reference — *what the experiment is.* Read once, then when the science needs it; near-timeless.

Describes the data and the question, not our progress. If one of these changes, something about the experiment changed — not about us.

1. [The dataset and the design](01-the-dataset-and-the-design.md) — what was recorded and what the files actually contain: two matched-cell scans of animal 33328 (session 6 pre-DOI, session 7 post-DOI — the assignment confirmed from the protocol slides), the GrayImageNet stimulus, trials and tiers, behavior and pupil, the 749 cells tracked across both scans, the 100 repeated test images shared by both conditions, and what is and is not analyzable.
2. [The question, made falsifiable](02-the-question-made-falsifiable.md) — operational definitions of "hallucination" in V1 population data and seven numbered hypotheses, each with its disproof and the control (pre-DOI baseline, time-matched epoch, trial and identity shuffles) it is weighed against. The chapter where the exciting word is forced to make predictions it can fail.
4. [Cataloguing the deck](04-cataloguing-the-deck.md) — Erin Neyhart's project-overview presentation, kept de-named in the project layer, read from inside the analysis: each slide-claim mapped to where it is made falsifiable, and the preliminary drug-state classifier read as the arousal/locomotion confound the whole analysis must survive. The rigorous counterpart to the orienting deck.
