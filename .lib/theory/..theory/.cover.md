This is the branch beside `library/theory/`, the project's shelf of mathematics written to be published.
It records what the team knows about writing theory there: why LaTeX, how a document is laid out and
built, how it is sent to someone else, and what can and cannot check an argument. It is not the
mathematics; the documents are. The shelf's own `.cover.md` says only what anyone who clones the project
needs — what is there and the one command that builds it — and this branch holds the reasons.

## Books

### [Theory](.cover.md) — Theory

You are reading it. It catalogues itself, because this book is the branch.

### [Writing Theory](../writing-theory/.cover.md)

How the shelf works and why: LaTeX as the one medium for abstract theory, the engine and editor, the
layout of a document and its shared preamble and notation, building and sending a document as a PDF and as
a single standalone source, and the stance on checking an argument — decided per document, by what kind of
mathematics it is. Authored by Claude, with me.
