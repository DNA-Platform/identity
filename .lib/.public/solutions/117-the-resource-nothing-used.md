## Symptoms

***`error UNUSED-FILE: .claude/.conversations/metalogical-subjectivity/1-march-22-2025.tsx — 1-march-22-2025~the-transduction-theorem-refined.md stands beside this chapter and nothing in it inserts or imports it`*** — sixteen of them across two books, at the bind's catalogue, the minute after both books had been re-imported with the importer's overwrite flag, and the importer having reported both written and nothing kept.

## What it turned out to be

The re-import had changed where a file stands: a conversation that was one chapter became a chapter per day, and an artifact's final file moved from beside the first day to beside the last. The importer wrote the new files and left the old where they were, since an overwrite had only ever meant *write again*; the binder, which refuses a resource beside a chapter that no chapter inserts or imports, found the previous import's files beside the first day with nothing naming them.

## How it was found

A probe listing every `~` resource in every conversation folder against every `![[ … ]]` the folder's chapters insert reported sixteen unreferenced, every one under the old first-day stem, [`stale-files.mjs`](../projection/115-sprint-110--designing-conversations.md) in the session's scratchpad; the count went to zero when the importer removed them.

## The lesson

A writer that rewrites a folder owns the folder's remainder: on an overwrite the importer now removes every file that stood in the book's folder before and is neither written again nor one of his kept edits, [the ledger](../../../../.me/importer/5-the-ledger.tsx). The binder's refusal is right and stays; it is what made the leftover visible.
