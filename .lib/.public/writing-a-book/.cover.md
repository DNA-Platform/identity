How a library is written against `.public` as it stands — what a library IS, how it is dressed, the object graph everything you build sits in front of, the reference manual that prints the tools a library is built with, and how to be a librarian inside the closure. Read in order; the first chapter is the metaphor and the last is the voice. The test library beside the binder, [`.binding/.test`](../../package/.binding/.test/galleys.ts), is the worked example of every chapter.

Split out of the single design book on 2026-09-11 at Doug's direction — "26 chapters is way more than you even read in a whole catchup session" — with every chapter's entry carried over byte for byte and renumbered, and renumbered once more at the move.*
