Written 2026-10-07 on Doug's word — *"compact the documentation, so that it is short enough to actually be read"* — as the fold of the thirteen chapters after them, which are kept as the record, where each of his rulings stands whole and dated. Read the four in order; go to the record when a ruling's whole context is wanted. The test library beside the binder, [`.binding/.test`](../../package/.binding/.test/galleys.ts), is the worked example; [The Bookshelf](../../../../.me/.design/5-the-bookshelf.tsx) in his library is the worked page.
