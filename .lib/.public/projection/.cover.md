# Projection

- **author:** [Arthur](../../../../.claude/library/..teamsmanship/..team/arthur/arthur-or-the-shape-of-everything/.cover.md)
- **subject:** [Publicity](../..publicity/.cover.md)

---

This book is the sprint-by-sprint record of the team's effort to build the public library — the view onto the repository, the [`@dna-platform/lib`](../../package/) package, and the machinery that renders the library to the world. A projection maps the team's sprints onto this branch: each chapter is one sprint, told in the branch's terms — what it aimed to do, what was built, what was learned — and the last chapter is the current state. Following [the Projection convention](../../../../.claude/library/library-tree/03-sprints.md#sprint-chapters), each sprint earns an entry written when the sprint begins and completed at its retro; a sprint with no chapter here is work the branch will forget. Empty at creation, the record grows as the work proceeds.

## Sprints

- **Sprint 44 — [Composition](00-sprint-44--composition.md)** — the branch's real beginning: turn [*The Semantics of Books*](../the-semantics-of-books/.cover.md) into code. Polished, fully-tested versions of every `@dna-platform/lib` class, with the composition chain (`$Character → $Word → $Sentence → $Paragraph → $Section → $Document`) as the spine — where a composition must materialize child components its author wrote only as text. Runner Arthur; Cathy & Queenie lead; Libby et al on the branch. The framework primitives it forced — `$()` eval and the block model — are recorded in the [chemistry projection](../../../chemistry/.lib/projection/43-sprint-44--eval-and-the-block-model.md).
