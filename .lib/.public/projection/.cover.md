This book is the sprint-by-sprint record of the team's effort to build the public library — the view onto the repository, the [`@dna-platform/lib`](../../package/) package, and the machinery that renders the library to the world. A projection maps the team's sprints onto this branch: each chapter is one sprint, told in the branch's terms — what it aimed to do, what was built, what was learned — and the last chapter is the current state. Following [the Projection convention](../../../../.claude/library/library-tree/03-sprints.md#sprint-chapters), each sprint earns an entry written when the sprint begins and completed at its retro; a sprint with no chapter here is work the branch will forget. Empty at creation, the record grows as the work proceeds.
