---

This book is the sprint-by-sprint record of the team's effort to build the public library — the view onto the repository, the [`@dna-platform/lib`](../../package/) package, and the machinery that renders the library to the world. A projection maps the team's sprints onto this branch: each chapter is one sprint, told in the branch's terms — what it aimed to do, what was built, what was learned — and the last chapter is the current state. Following [the Projection convention](../../../../.claude/library/library-tree/03-sprints.md#sprint-chapters), each sprint earns an entry written when the sprint begins and completed at its retro; a sprint with no chapter here is work the branch will forget. Chapter zero is the planning scratchpad — a true numbered chapter under [the convention](../../../../.claude/library/library-tree/03-sprints.md#the-planning-scratchpad--chapter-zero), whose content is overwritten as sprints absorb it. Empty at creation, the record grows as the work proceeds.

**A closed sprint is compacted, and this is what that means.** A sprint chapter is written at full working weight while the sprint runs — requirements, units, cleanup, test scenarios, order, risks, a self-check — because that is what runs it. At [compounding](../../../../.claude/library/..librarianship/17-compounding.md) it stops being a plan and becomes an **index**: the scaffolding is replaced by a stub that says what stood there, the identifiers the record cites survive as a register so no link breaks, and the findings stay whole. **The scenarios became the suite, the order happened, and a risk that fired is in the record.** Compacted 2026-08-13: 122,595 words to 105,982, and three links that had never resolved were repaired.
