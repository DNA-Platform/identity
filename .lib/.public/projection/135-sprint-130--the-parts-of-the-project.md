## <a id="spec"></a>The sprint's specification — draft, said back for his corrections

1. The sprint as content
   - 1.1 The sprint is a small project with a specification that evolves; this record carries it, amended in place, never replaced.
   - 1.2 The two sides of a sprint are figured out by working this record as one: what belongs on its front and what on its back is itself a question of the sprint. *(Open: his answer to what the two sides of a sprint are.)*
2. The ground
   - 2.1 The working ground is the textbook's first chapter, The Symmetry of Arithmetic — refining it is the sprint's deliverable.
   - 2.2 He writes the other documents with Claude Desktop; the room transcribes them in verbatim through the content's machinery.
3. Progress
   - 3.1 Progress gains a clearer description: what a progress entry is, said precisely enough to build against.
   - 3.2 Progress maps to the result — here, the textbook chapter; the form of that mapping is to be specified. *(Open: the mapping's form — the first question of the walk.)*
4. The parts
   - 4.1 The parts under consideration: specification, identification (the front, the deliverable), justification, intuition.
   - 4.2 A justification is an annotation on the identification that refers to the specification, noting what has been satisfied.
   - 4.3 Intuition covers how the spec relates to existing things, how results will work, what might be relevant. *(Open: where intuition lives — a part of the project side, annotations, or the front.)*
5. Annotations
   - 5.1 Annotations become toggleable — visual ones — and are, if possible, the same sort of annotation as the .public infrastructure already carries: the mechanism read in Sprint 129 stands, a visible annotation being a sort whose note() answers with its writing, no second species.
   - 5.2 Their uses: connecting related things, writing ideas, writing justifications for progress.
6. The views
   - 6.1 A way to choose different views, so reading is not constant scrolling: toggling annotations, showing one part, showing parts within a project section. *(Open: the chooser's form and the set of views.)*
