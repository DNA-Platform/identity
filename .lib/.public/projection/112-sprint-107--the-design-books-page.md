## <a id="the-process"></a>What the design process has been like, read off the record

*Read 2026-10-11 from the eight sprint chapters, [98](103-sprint-98--dougs-design.md) to [106](111-sprint-106--the-manual-spread-out.md), the design book's four written chapters and Choosing a Design in his story, on his question; drafted for his correction, since the verdicts are his and only the reading is ours. This is the portfolio's spine and the first draft of what it must say.*

**Three methods, in order.**

1. **Latitude, 2026-10-04 to 05, [Sprint 98](103-sprint-98--dougs-design.md).** Many designs explored many ways: twenty-five concepts sketched by kind of page at a desk and a phone, each numbered for good, then eight more; questions by letter with his answers verbatim; a design chosen per book, by number. *What was useful, in his words:* *"I can't design from a description. I need to see things, many of them and quickly, and choose"*; the numbers as the way of speaking; the answers written under the questions; and what the answers began, the design language that still holds, the top bar as the cover and the side bar as the contents, less of the dark, a scheme per book, the cover as the landmark, the author on every screen. *What it produced that was later designed:* the shelf of 1 and side by side of 6, each the start of a page designed from its print. *What it produced that was built and never designed:* the reading view of 25, which is his story's Sheet, and the frame of 15 and 16, both built in the sprint that failed and standing as decided; his word, 2026-10-11: *"my autobiography hasn't been designed yet."* Every other concept went nowhere. *What did not work:* he liked no design whole and we could not tell which aspects of which he liked; the matching of designs to books was read from his answers and hallucinated, the two-bar design he never approved; the sketches were all black, copies of products, a conversation interface of our own; a layout question put in words rather than shown; a blue guessed where his page had one to measure; and three things kept outside the book, which taught closure.
2. **Building from the sketches, 2026-10-05 to 06, [Sprint 100](105-sprint-100--the-big-plan.md) and [Sprint 101](106-sprint-101--the-design-into-the-semantics.md).** The plan, then the build: the four books from structure with no look, which he liked for showing *"the DNA of the .public framework"*, then each book made to look like its concept from the frame file's values. *What was useful:* structure first; the frame drawn once by the base book in regions; the object model, a book is layout and annotations make components; the measurement that a tone and an arrangement are classes a press adds and never Formats, since a Format pressed in front remounts the book. *What did not work, and why it was cancelled as a failure:* it built from sketches that were never designs, matched a frame file's values and called that a design, and answered his feedback by recolouring the live site six times in an afternoon; *"okay but sad"*, *"a halloween nightmare blackbook library"*, *"I feel so much pain and sadness looking at this work"*; black too loud, out-of-the-tube effects, a book with a colour where he wanted a scheme, pink, no comparable named before invention. *The lesson that became the next method:* a sketch is not a design; a design is made with him, on his own data, one change at a time.
3. **Designing together from the print, 2026-10-07 to 10, [Sprint 102](107-sprint-102--designing-together.md) to [Sprint 106](111-sprint-106--the-manual-spread-out.md).** His method, verbatim: *"you and I are going to iterate rapidly in HTML… LITERALLY start with the data that is in each book, bring it into html, so we can rapidly evolve the design by just looking at the same HTML page."* One page per book per sprint, made from the book's bound print by The Print, opened from the file, his rounds, one thing per round, comparables before invention, ported with the page open and measured, photographs after. *What it produced:* everything that is in production.
