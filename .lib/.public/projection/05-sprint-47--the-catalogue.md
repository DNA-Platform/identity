Catalogues, and the refinement of references they force. The sprint's charge, Doug's (2026-08-01): **a catalogue is a composition of references** — and references have a second composition rule we have not yet made explicit: **the telescope**.

## The frame, Doug's (2026-08-01)

$Chemistry has become what it was only inspired by: a reflection of Semantic Reference Theory. SRT is a universal theory for specifying semantics; it hosts its own canonical specification, so it needs canonical semantics — and as ZFC has set semantics, **SRT has library semantics**: the library that is objectively the organization of knowledge (books its objects) and subjectively about the librarian who organizes it (the librarian its subject). IXP is the home of the open-source project containing SRT, and this work is the realization of its semantics — everything in this repo to be documented in `@dna-platform/lib`, the library written in library semantics. Letter / word / sentence … / book / subject / library is **one realization of a more abstract thing, all understood as compositions** — the composition monad imported across scales. References are being given their nesting property in that same world, and the monad structure will then apply to catalogues. These are first drafts of very fundamental abstractions for organizing knowledge; they must be elegant and exact.

## The design, as landed (2026-08-02; suite 93, types clean)

**Two connections.** `read()` goes forward — a reference reads its referent, fresh at every call. `ref` comes back — every piece of writing offers the reference that speaks for it, **specialized per level**: a letter is its own reference (the literal floor), `sentence.ref` is a `$$Sentence`, `book.ref` is its **cover**. Every part knows its canonical catalogue — `catalogue`, a public member the reading assigns (`part.catalogue = this`); sameness climbs catalogues and indexes to a shared ground, reading only public members. There is no `symbol`/`literal` property pair: read and ref are the whole dyad. Validation lives only where authoring is uncontrolled — a book's shape, a section's title, a row's kept reference; what the framework itself assigns is not re-checked.

**A catalogue is a composition of references** — literally the type: `$Catalogue<T>` extends `$Composition<$Reference<T>>` and `$Reference<$Composition<T>>` (a catalogue is also a reference for what it organizes). The `$$` classes (`$$Word` … `$$Chapter`, each beside its literal, holding `of`) realize it per level. The book splits: the **cover is its reference** (title, later author); the **table of contents is its catalogue**, whose contents are `$Row`s — sections that are references — validated as such, dereferencing to the book through the guaranteed cover.

**Composition.** A `$Path` is two references: read the first; check by sameness that the next stands at what was read; read the next. `then` builds paths. `$Location` is the indexed reference `at(index)` writes — its read is the unique index match among its composition's contents. `Composible` (utilities; statics as extension methods — `contents()` is enough) carries `canonical`/`where`/`select`/`at`/`follow`, and the aspect grades stack descending in every class body, each using the previous property (`book.sections` from contents, `paragraphs` from `sections`, … `letters`) — two things at once, all the way down. **Equality is sameness of arrival**: grouping doesn't matter, independent buildings agree, another book is another place. A bookmark builds its path fresh from its `for` address at the book it is rendered inside (a protected member; the belonging is the parent). A catalogue can **`follow()`** — dereference wholesale into the composition of what its entries read, castable onward to another catalogue when the read things are themselves references (the card drawer becoming the literature).

**Laws in force.** Uniqueness lives in `read` (a duplicated index reads nothing; `single` is gone). Letters tile losslessly on the sentence; words claim their letters. Properties forced by bond or validation are non-optional (`Row.path`, `Section.title`, `Cover.title`; the contents page trusts its book). No canonical presentation: views are free; none of this machinery runs at render.

## The demo integration (2026-08-02; driver 44/44)

The references walked into the manifold. A new chapter, **The Atlas** (08), says the catalogue in the book's own geometric voice — an atlas is a catalogue of charts, the contents page is the book's own atlas, the cover the one reference that answers for the whole — and carries live links down to word depth. Ribbons are plural now: the chip presses one for the chapter and takes it home on a second press; **double-click presses a ribbon into a paragraph**. The depth law landed in `follow`: below the paragraph, the page opens to the paragraph that holds the word — the model still reads the very word; *the page rounds; the book does not*. ## After the close — the document and its apparatus (2026-08-03)

The retro's design session ran the same day, and Doug's rulings reshaped the reference layer before the sprint's records cooled. **The rulings:** reference equality is gone from the interface — *"equality must be mediated by the being using the references"* — and `same()`, the `catalogue` member on referents, and every `equals` died with it, the driftwood question closing by deletion; `$Referent` became an **interface** carrying `valid()`, so a reference is a referent; `read(): T` never answers undefined — a false reference **throws**, `valid()` is the no-throw guard; `single(match)` returned to the composition (C# semantics — reading is comprehension, never collection lookup); `contents` returned to **`parts`** (*part is the true singular*); and the **types-express-expectations law** entered Structural Patterns — after the bond, what must exist is typed as existing.

**The parent rule, in chemistry itself:** an element in a block reaches outside the block for its parent — the chemical whose bond interprets it. The one-line lift fix (`chemical.ts`) closed the `$Eval`-orphan gap the probe had exposed, and a pre-existing freeze (the unguarded `$Bookmark.book` climb spinning on self-parented roots) got its guard. **The citation rulings (2026-08-03, Doug, designing together):** citations follow LaTeX — **linear for now**: numbers are never assigned, they derive from first appearance in the writing (`document.keys`, the walk in reading order); the filing bond numbers nothing, and the foot lists in citation order. The key is authored as `for` — and **`for` IS `$for`**: the prop and the property are one member, no getter twin, consumers read `$for` as itself (probed: props bind only through the `$` backing — the membrane is how props enter). `$Footnote` carries `$for` alone; a citation is a keyed note, `$Citation` a marker kind; the marks echo the entries (`<Cite for="euler"/>` beside the command form *Cite Euler*, `key` falling from `$for` to the copy). The cross-document machinery I had invented (address prefixes, `source`, declining reads) was **never consented to and is deleted** — the lesson filed: "I don't know what this means" is an explanation order, never a change order; design directions get raised and designed together, not taken.
