## <a id="approaches"></a>The approaches, at mechanism altitude

**A. By the notes' citations.** Every work a note names is a candidate; a work is ranked by how many notes lean on it and whether a chapter of The Publication, the paper's own parts, names it. Cheap and faithful to the notes; blind to what the paper needs and the notes never said.

**B. By the paper's parts.** For each part of the publication as the notebook specifies it, the preface, the object, the mechanism, zero for proof, zero as the horizon and as the counterexample, two-sided arithmetic, the surviving sentence and Goldbach, and the formalism's options, the publications a reader would expect cited there, found in the notes where the notes name them and searched for where they do not; the candidates are what the parts need. Faithful to the paper; some searching.

**C. By his word.** He names the publications from his reading and the team finds, places and cites them. Fastest to a chapter; the identification is his alone.

**Recommendation: B with A as its check.** The parts drive and the notes supply; what a part needs and no note names is a gap the sprint fills by search and puts to him; what a note names and no part needs stays in the entry as a reference and not a publication.

**What his answers made of them.** A, and not B: the publications are associated with the notes, *"We don't need the publications parts"*, and a note is added where a publication needs one; the set is every work named and his additions, the geometries of other postulates, Hilbert's sphere and the fundamental theorem of algebra; one version each; the chapters the team's under his signature.
