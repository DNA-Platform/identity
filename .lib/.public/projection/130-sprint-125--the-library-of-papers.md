### The first resource: Gödel 1931

**The paper.** Kurt Gödel, *Über formal unentscheidbare Sätze der Principia Mathematica und verwandter Systeme I*, Monatshefte für Mathematik und Physik 38 (1931), 173–198, Leipzig; summarized to the Vienna Academy in 1930, Anzeiger No. 19. Four parts: the outline of the proof; the system P, its Gödel numbering, the forty-six relations, Propositions I to VI; the generalizations, Propositions VII to X; the consistency result, Proposition XI. Proposition VI is the first theorem and XI the second. A second part was never published.

## <a id="approaches"></a>The approaches, at mechanism altitude, and what his answers made of them

Three were put to him: **A, the text**, a paper a book whose chapters are the paper's, written by an importer from a text; **B, the record**, the same book with chapters the team's reading of it, the propositions as mentions, the sources linked out, recommended and shaped for A; **C, the entry**, a paper a row of the library's table. His answers took none as put: a paper is a citable publication held as a resource, the file itself, beside a chapter that describes it and carries its citation, which he writes; and the paper is read on the page. That is a fourth shape, nearer A's end than B's means, with no import and no book per paper, and it is what the [Requirements](#requirements) say.
