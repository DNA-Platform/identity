## Where brittleness lives

**A bind is brittle in proportion to the distance between a fault and the gate that finds it, times what the refusal throws away.** This week every fault was small, three headings, one selector, one line of a barrel, and the cost ran from nothing to two hours by nothing but where the gate stood. The code is not what is brittle; the order of the gates is, and the record's place after the last of them. So the sprint's shape is not a faster render first. It is: **no fault found after the specify costs more than its own page, and nothing drawn is ever lost**; then the full render made affordable; then, if the number he gives is still not met, the conversation drawn by the client, which is his "resource read from the server" with the book kept.

## The approaches, at mechanism altitude

- **A · The gates move before the render, and the record keeps what proved.** The catalogue makes the id each heading will wear, from the same words the draw uses, and holds it with the spots to the rule an id is worn once, so a clash is refused in the catalogue's second with both places named. The record keeps every page the proof passed and leaves a refused page unrecorded, so the next bind draws the refused page alone; a refused proof still fails the bind. *Trade-off:* two changes under the package, his yes; the catalogue learns the draw's slug rule, one function shared and not copied. *Gain:* the night cannot be lost, and the class of fault that cost 3,204 s costs one second.
- **B · The full render made affordable, by measure.** Each drawing child already says its peak; the render says which page peaked and what each page cost, so the whale is named. The library's `rendering` configuration is set on the measure, batch and budget by the whale, children at once by what the machine has free, which the binder reads at the start and reports, choosing no more than fit. *Trade-off:* the binder reads the machine, a new input, and a child at once is a number he set to one on the 28th. *Gain:* the full render from 53 minutes toward 20 if three children fit under the peaks, which the measure decides; the register's page may want its own budget.
- **C · A conversation drawn by the client, said on the book.** A book that says so is written its shell with its chunk and no drawn markup, so the render draws nothing for it and the bundle, kept by book, is its whole cost: a change to the conversation's page chapter rebuilds the stale chunks, about six minutes for all 983 and seconds for one, and draws no page. The proof reads such a page in a browser, as the test library already drives pages, or exempts what it cannot read. *Trade-off:* the first paint is blank until the chunk draws, about what hydration costs today, five seconds on the longest conversation; the proof weakens for those pages. *Gain:* the conversation's page chapter and every chapter it imports stop costing a render; the book, its cover, its chapters cut by hand and its annotations are all kept, which his "different kind of resource" would not have.
- **Set aside: a conversation as data drawn by one page.** The import would write data and one page would draw any conversation from it. It meets "a project needs to have a book and we find a way to query a conversation" and loses the conversation as a book, its chapters, its annotations and the contract chapter whole. C meets the same words with the book kept, so this is not proposed.

**Recommended:** A and B in this sprint, measured; C designed in this sprint, with its page form sketched, and built only if B leaves the full render over the number he gives in Q1 below. The project page's own requirements, R6 and R7, stand beside them, since "functional" is what he reviews.
