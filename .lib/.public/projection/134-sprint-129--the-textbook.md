## <a id="where"></a>The deliverable — what stands at the close

**The chapter written by meeting the specification.** The textbook's first chapter, The Symmetry of Arithmetic, teaches the statement of work part by part — the formalism, what is known, the three types of theorem with the basic cases proven as their ordinary proofs, the object and its constraints, the meaning, the tests — and where only X itself can complete a passage, a brace stands interpolated, naming the open determination it waits on. Its front is the textbook; its back is the project, holding his two documents verbatim.

**The content, lifted to the library.** A piece of content is the content of its chapter, a front and a back flipped by one unlabelled control at the title's right. The general family — Content, Project, Specification, Progress, Markdown, Cite, Interpolated — lives in Our Reference's ninth chapter, The Content, written in the numbered form with its code appended; mathematics keeps Theorem and Proof and re-exports the family so its books keep one door. One flagged wart rides the lift: a subject's dress sits inert in the lifted style until the framework grows a seam for extending a format's style.

## <a id="story"></a>The story, compressed

**12 November.** The seed: classes to define a textbook, a chapter with parts, visibility so one thing is the only thing on the screen. The brainstorm walked: the specification named the most important part; the form arrived — a title with a numbered list of lists, items cited by number; the permanence rule — specifications are never thrown away, a change is made by changing the specification; the manuals gained their rule, every subject carrying one with its idioms and enforcing code beside the chapter that states them. Four chapters were specified — Formal Logic, The Logical Form, Formal Arithmetic, The Arithmetic Form — and the bridesmaid analogy entered the notebook mid-brainstorm, notes never gated.

**15 November.** The change of plans: the four chapters retired to the notebook as The Potential Chapters; a chapter became an attempt to specify one mathematical object. The first object arrived with his statement of work — X, the symmetry of arithmetic — and with it the content idea: a thing with a front and a back, the two sides of a wikipedia page, maybe called Content. The progress document followed, and the ask to write the front as the best guess of what has been identified.

**16 November, the day.** His corrections: first person everywhere relative to him — the perspectival sweep crossed the whole library, some 120 prose rewrites with every quotation byte-identical — and project and content are things, not states; the four-mode bar withdrew. His answers respecified the model: the content like a section below the title; the project holding the specification (a statement of work identifying the deliverable) and the progress (a narrative with authors), nothing mandatory; one unlabelled toggle. The documents transcribed, then un-transcribed on "I said verbatim" — the files render through marked, every apparatus between the file and the page removed. The helper session shipped the navigation seam and the perspectival read-through, correcting one of this record's own overclaims.

**16 November, the night.** His eye drove the design through the pages: the navigation became the three catalogue characters chosen by measured ink after four guessed dresses each cost him a look; the chrome fell — no running head titles over chapters, the book's identity on the front and the table; the project side stripped to exactly the two documents. Then the legal form: the specification is the legal document — the thing agreed between buyer and seller, identifying the deliverable uniquely — its conventions the specification part's alone, learned by dressing the textbook a layer too wide and undressing it the same hour. The register became literal markdown — the source's own face — driven to the closest match by sweeps, his editor's eyedroppered colors, the sameness rectangles, the quiet matrix, and his anchor: the feel of the permutation while preserving identity.

**17 November.** The transition earned its final form through conviction by frames: the glide retired, the dip tried and convicted — its keyframes had resurrected the glide, its trough was the white flash — and the platform's dissolve took their place. The square returned to the table's page as an attribute. The cover grew to the screen. CHAPTER 1 stopped changing with the title. Equations arrived. The family lifted to the library level. The sprint closed.
